Product Feedback
Library navigation broken at scale
Can create categories but can't collapse them — everything renders as one long flat list. Tutorials get buried. Interlude has too much content in the library and needs folder hierarchy with collapsible sections to make it usable. Top pain point for this account.
Library data updates are 100% manual
To update a PDF: generate file → save it out → manually go to admin → upload → relink. Wants FTP or automated feed equivalent for library content, the same way product files work. Someone has to do this weekly. "We keep our price list in there — anything we could automate would be tremendous."
Order recall / undo
After a rep submits and realizes there's a mistake, there's no way to pull it back before it syncs. Admin must manually intercept. If it syncs, they're chasing two orders through the system. ~2% frequency but causes customer-facing duplicate acknowledgements and internal chaos.
Share widget as a conversion blind spot
Reps who don't want their quote conversion tracked use the share widget to send quotes directly to the customer, bypassing ECat. Quote never shows up in the system; order appears out of nowhere. No visibility into those conversion rates.
Pending orders / scheduled date filtering
All upholstery orders are scheduled. Reps currently go to a PDF to see their production schedule. Want to filter orders in the portal by scheduled date and see backlog in one view. Currently not a filterable field.
Multi-brand / multi-catalog order writing
Two sides of the business (ICU/Wyoming + Interlude Home) require reps to log out and back in to switch catalogs mid-order. Deal breaker for showroom scanning. Visual Comfort (three lines) has the same problem. One-to-many architecture needed.
