Intelligence report — cross-client feedback synthesis
What universally landed
Dormant high-value accounts, rep behavior → AOV correlation (presentations drive order value), and new customer acquisition with one-and-done filter. Every account understood these immediately and could connect them to existing manual workflows they were already doing.
What needs to be built — scorecards
Rep scorecards and customer scorecards are the most-requested addition not currently in the product. Andrew (Hubbardton Forge) named it the single most important thing. Sean (Interlude) wants customer scorecards surfaced to reps pre-call. Suzanne (Renwill) wants rep coaching broken down per individual with trajectory data. Manufacturers will want them too — not just operators.
Data completeness is the blocker
Three of six accounts have less than ~50% of actual revenue visible in SuperCat. The report cannot be meaningfully acted on at Charleston Forge (12%), Linon (field sales only), or Interlude (no custom upholstery) until the data gap is closed. Connecting ERP / extended sales data is a prerequisite, not an enhancement.
Segmentation as a recurring ask
Multiple accounts need customer type as a lens across the entire report: designer vs. stocking dealer (Interlude, Hubbardton Forge), retail vs. trade (Renwill), custom vs. standard (Charleston Forge, Kindel). Behavior patterns are too different across segments to interpret in aggregate.
False alarm risk — context matters
Charleston Forge's 50% order volume drop flagged as false positive (only sells at market; no market in 90 days = no orders). The report needs business-type context to avoid misleading VP-level operators who will lose trust in the data quickly if they see nonsensical flags.
