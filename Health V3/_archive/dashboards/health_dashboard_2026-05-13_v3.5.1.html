How the score is built

The composite is the equally-weighted average of four dimensions — Engagement, Adoption, Value Delivery, and Operational Health — each contributing 25%. Two overrides can cap the score downward: Ghost Account (zero logins, high ARR → Critical cap) and Behavioral Floor (low engagement + low value delivery → Watch cap). All four dimension scores are always shown so you can see what's driving the result.
