## 1. Executive POV

**The verdict in one line:** A third of the book is genuinely send-proud; a third needs a one-line cover note; a third should be held until there's more data (or, in one case, until a data bug is fixed).

| Bucket | Count | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **GREAT — send proudly** | 12 | Reads like example-co: all-channel data connected, named accounts/reps/items with dollars, real volume, a complete Team section. |
| **GOOD — send with a one-line note** | 10 | Genuinely useful and specific, but carries one honest caveat (eCat-only data, greenfield eCat volume, or a missing section). |
| **BAD — hold / heavy context / fix first** | 14 | Thin underlying volume (an early-2026 launch surge then dormancy), missing sections, Limited-confidence data, extreme single-point concentration — or, for `shl`, a corrupted source figure. |

### The 5 to send first (flagship quality)
1. **cci (Currey & Company)** — $8.6M eCat on $87.6M business, 37-rep order-based leaderboard, 5 coaching cards, Full confidence on 3 of 4 sections. The closest thing to example-co in the batch.
2. **hfg (Hubbardton Forge)** — 39.9% digital capture of a $42M business, Full confidence, 4 coaching cards, clean stockout + churn + activation narrative.
3. **ril (Ratana)** — $17.6M eCat, Full confidence, 5 coaching cards, territory-growth + churn signals that read like a strategy memo.
4. **kal (Kalco/Allegri)** — Full confidence across the board, $2.6M/$11.7M, tight named-account actionability.
5. **fal (Sarreid)** — Strong across all 4 sections, 53 high-priority signals, dense and specific.

(**gh / Gabby** is GREAT-quality too — $50.2M eCat — but fix one number before it goes out; see Flags.)

### The ones to hold
- **`shl` — hold for data integrity, not polish.** A corrupt ~$464M "Carrington Lighting / Matt Rowland" order survived into the report: the Team section header reads **"$466.7M eCat sales LTM"** and a coaching note cites one rep's **"$464.2M."** The narrative findings (peak quarter, penetration) and digital-share were correctly suppressed, but the §5 leaderboard total is visibly absurd. **Do not send until the order is purged and §5 re-rendered.**
- **The "empty-feel" thin nine:** `df, dals, hvl, ml, afx, all, bp, eglo` (plus `prog`) — these are early-adoption status snapshots (a January/spring-2026 launch spike, then everyone went quiet), dominated by onboarding counts, catalog-completeness, and "data is 313 days stale" findings. Tiny dollars, often no Team and/or Product section.
- **Concentration traps:** `sca` (one rep placed 352 of 412 orders), `eglo_can` (80% of the year from one banner's single January order), `gcl`/`arl` (no Team section, launch-surge volume).

### Cross-cutting themes
1. **Confidence tracks one thing: is the all-channel / total-business feed connected?** Every "Full/Strong" report has it; every "Partial/Limited" report is eCat-only or engagement-only. That single data connection is the difference between a wallet-share strategy doc and an in-app activity log.
2. **A whole cohort is in eCat infancy.** ~12 orgs show the identical shape: a coordinated rep onboarding push in Jan–Mar 2026, a one-month volume spike, then dormancy. Their reports are honest but thin — they read as "here's your launch and your runway," not "here's your business."
3. **Fleet-wide data-hygiene flag:** the **"~313-day stale pricing/config/options import"** finding appears in well over a dozen orgs (afx, df, eli, ml, sca, da, eglo, eglo_can, wac, gcl, mlc, sbl, hvl, dals). This is one batch re-import campaign, not 14 separate problems — worth raising internally.
4. **The winning recipe is consistent:** all-channel connected + a named "$X in proven demand buying off-eCat" activation list + a stockout board with exposed accounts + reorder-cadence churn alerts + rep coaching cards. The GREATs all have all five; the BADs have one or two.

## 4. Flags (data integrity & things to fix)

1. **`shl` — corrupt order leaked into the report.** ~$464.2M phantom order ("Carrington Lighting," attributed to rep Matt Rowland) inflates the Team section to **"$466.7M eCat sales LTM"** against a real ~$49.7M total business. Digital-share and the signal-summary dollars were correctly suppressed/reframed, but §5's leaderboard total and that one coaching line are visibly wrong. **Blocker.**

2. **`gh` — implausible co-purchase figure.** Finding #2 cites **"$302,760,334 in addressable co-purchase volume"** — ~7x the company's entire $41.6M total business. The "addressable co-purchase volume" metric is summing pair-volumes into a meaningless headline number. Reframe or drop the dollar figure before sending an otherwise-flagship report.

3. **Inflated cross-sell / co-purchase dollars are a pattern**, not just `gh`: `ril` ($20.6M), `clm` ($9.1M), `vic` ($45.6M modeled on a $9.5M business). The methodology produces eye-popping numbers that undercut credibility. Recommend capping/reframing "co-purchase volume" as a count or a % of relevant base across the template.

4. **`gh` also shows eCat ($50.2M) > total business ($41.6M)** — a feed-lag artifact (the report correctly held digital-share for this reason). Cosmetically odd; worth a footnote so an exec doesn't double-take.

5. **Fleet-wide stale-import flag.** The "~313-day frozen pricing/options/config" finding recurs across afx, df, eli, ml, sca, da, eglo, eglo_can, wac, gcl, mlc, sbl, hvl, dals. One internal batch re-import would clear it for the whole fleet and materially improve the next reporting cycle.

6. **Missing-section reports.** No Team (§5): `afx, all, arl, bp, prog, gcl, vl`. Also missing Product (§3): `all, prog, gcl`. `vl` is the only one of these that's otherwise GREAT-quality — worth noting its Team section was gated out by low order-rep count, not by lack of data.
