Investigative Context

Declining unit prices may reflect several dynamics: competitive pricing pressure driving buyers to request discounts, a natural shift toward lower-priced product tiers, changing buyer mix within an account, or seasonal promotional activity. These patterns warrant individual account review — they are signals to investigate, not conclusions to act on.

Action: FEI / Ferguson is the account to look at first: its average unit price slipped 13.5% yet it still placed 151 orders ($151,967) last quarter, so the relationship is active but the product mix is shifting toward lower-priced items. The same pattern at Build.com (−23.3%) and Progressive Lighting (−25.0%) suggests buyers trading down rather than walking away. Use a focused pricing or mix conversation on these high-volume accounts before assuming the decline is competitive loss.
