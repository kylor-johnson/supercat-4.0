Order Type & Workflow
Hold-for-Confirmation orders carry a 5.6x value premium

Your Hold-for-Confirmation (HFC) orders average $10,603 — 5.6x the standard confirmed order value ($1,881). These large, governed transactions are where your highest-value volume sits; tightening the approval-to-fulfillment loop on them is your highest-leverage workflow opportunity.
