Order Type & Workflow
Single-Workflow Ordering

Effectively 100% of eCat orders (4,179 of 4,180) are placed as Confirmed orders — there is no quote-then-convert step in your current flow. That keeps ordering fast and frictionless, but it also means none of your $4.78M in eCat sales passes through a quote stage where larger project deals are typically negotiated and grown.

First-time eCat buyers rose from an average of 1.8 per month in the first half of the year to 3.0 per month in the second half, peaking at 5 new buyers in May 2026. Every new buyer was rep-acquired through iPad — your field team, not self-service, is driving onboarding onto eCat.

Account-level trajectory data would show which of these new buyers are growing fastest; request customer-level first-order dates and subsequent revenue for the next cycle so we can name the winners, not just count them.
Action: Commerce momentum is real and broad — $4.78M in eCat sales across 4,180 orders, accelerating new-buyer acquisition (1.8 to 3.0 per month), and a diversified buyer base with no single-account dependency.
