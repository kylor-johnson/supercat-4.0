Product Mix Shift

Several accounts show their average unit price drifting down quarter-over-quarter while their overall order volume holds or grows. This pattern usually reflects buyers shifting toward lower-priced items within their mix rather than across-the-board discounting — a signal to understand, not an alarm.

Investigative Context

Declining average unit prices may reflect several dynamics: competitive pricing pressure driving discount requests, a natural shift toward lower-priced product tiers, changing buyer mix within an account, or seasonal promotional activity. These patterns warrant individual account review — they are signals to investigate, not conclusions to act on.
