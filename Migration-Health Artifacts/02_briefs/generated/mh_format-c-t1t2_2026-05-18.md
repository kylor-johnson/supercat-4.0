## What's Not Working Right Now

Magnussen Home has **76 active reps** running orders through the platform — and **411 buyers** loaded who can browse the catalog today. Every order in the system currently runs through a rep. That means:

- Every reorder, every standard SKU, every after-hours request — a rep takes it, enters it, confirms it
- Buyers who want to submit or check orders outside business hours either wait or email your team
- 76 reps are doing both high-value relationship work and routine transaction processing simultaneously

**B2B Cart adds a checkout flow directly in your catalog.** Buyers with access can browse with Magnussen Home's pricing and product rules, build an order, and submit — without rep involvement for the transaction.

The 11pm-before-a-trade-show order that currently either misses a window or lands in a rep's inbox at midnight — B2B Cart captures it. The reorder a buyer submits on their own schedule — B2B Cart handles it. The rep team concentrates on the customers and orders that actually need them.

With 411 buyers in the system and 76 active reps, that's roughly five accounts per rep to manage personally. B2B Cart lets the highest-engagement buyers self-serve their routine orders — freeing rep capacity for relationship-driven sales.
