## What's Not Working Right Now

Magnussen Home has **1,533 dealer accounts** in the customer file — all of whom can browse the catalog today. Of those, **105 ordered in the last 12 months**, submitting **293 orders** — every one processed manually by a rep. Another **310 accounts have ordered historically but have gone quiet** in the last year.

Every order in the system currently runs through a rep. That means:

- Every reorder, every standard SKU, every after-hours request — a rep takes it, enters it, confirms it
- The 105 active buying accounts generate all 293 trailing orders with no self-serve path
- The 310 dormant accounts require rep outreach to re-engage — there's no low-friction way for them to place an order on their own

**B2B Cart adds a checkout flow directly in your catalog.** Active buyers can build and submit orders on their own schedule — no rep involvement for the transaction. Dormant accounts get a lower-friction path back in without requiring a rep call for every reorder.

The 11pm-before-a-trade-show order that currently either misses a window or lands in a rep's inbox at midnight — B2B Cart captures it. The reorder a dormant account would place if it were easier — B2B Cart enables it. The rep team concentrates on the customers and orders that actually need them.

## The Objection Worth Addressing

**"Our reps are handling orders just fine."**

They are — and that's exactly the point. 76 reps submitted 293 orders last year for 105 active buying accounts. B2B Cart doesn't replace that rep team. It handles routine reorders and standard SKU submissions those accounts could manage themselves — so rep capacity shifts toward new account development, complex orders, and the 310 dormant accounts worth reactivating.

The question isn't whether the current system works. It's whether a portion of those 293 orders could have been self-serve — and whether the 310 quiet accounts would order more if submitting didn't require a rep call. If yes to either, $452/month activates that channel.
