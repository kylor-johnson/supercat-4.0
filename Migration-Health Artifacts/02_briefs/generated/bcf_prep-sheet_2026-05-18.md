## Migration Driver: What You're Correcting

**user_rate_normalization**

Braxton Culler signed in 2022 at a $20/additional-user rate — set before the current rate card was in place. The contract provided 25 included users with 25 additional users billed at $20 each ($500/month in user charges). The platform base of $1,880 was also above the current T3 book rate, creating a mixed picture: legacy users at a discount, legacy platform slightly above book.

Under the new structure: the platform normalizes to $2,295 (T3 standard), the included base expands from 25 to 40 users, and user charges are calculated on trailing average usage (44 users, per migration table). At 44 trailing-avg users, only 4 are excess above the new 40-user base, charged at $25/user (first tier of the graduated curve) = $100/month. Platform base increases by $415/month; user charges decrease by $400/month. Net delta: **+$15/month**.

This is the simplest migration math in Wave 1. No discount to retire, no module compression, no multi-org complication. The number is driven entirely by user rate normalization with an offsetting included-base expansion.
