## Likely Objections — With Responses

### "Why is this happening now?"

> "We've been working through a full pricing standardization this year — the first time we've applied a consistent structure across all accounts. Every account at every tier is moving to the same rate card in 2026."

### "We just signed in 2024. Why is the price already changing?"

*This is the most likely tenure objection for a 2024 cohort — not an early-adopter grievance, but a "we just got here" reaction. Prepare for this specifically.*

> "Your rate at signing reflected a discount we applied at that time. That discount was tied to an early-commercial arrangement, not a permanent rate. What we're doing now is bringing every account onto the same structure — your tier, service level, and your team's access are all unchanged. The rate is moving to what every comparable account now pays. I know the timing feels quick, and I want to make sure we handle this transparently."

### "My original rate was part of a deal — I earned that."

> "That rate was set at a discount, and we're not pretending it was arbitrary. What we're doing now is bringing every account onto the same structure, because running materially different rates indefinitely isn't fair to accounts who joined at book rate. [IF strong relationship:] I want to make sure we handle this in a way that respects your partnership with us, which is why I'm delivering this directly."

### "Why us? Are other accounts getting this too?"

> "Yes — 100% of the install base is moving to the same structure. You're not being singled out. Accounts at every tier and tenure level are getting the same correction. Your number reflects your account profile, nothing else."

### "This is a 17% increase. That's not something we can absorb easily."

> "I understand that. We can phase this in — 50% of the increase for the first 60 days, then the full rate. The effective date is July 17, 2026, which gives you approximately 60 days to plan for it. I'm happy to work with your finance team on timing if that's helpful."

### "What happens if we say no?"

> Option 1 (firm): "This is a standard migration we're completing across the full install base. The effective date is July 17, 2026. I want to work with you to make the transition smooth, but the pricing structure itself is the same for everyone."
> Option 2 (accommodation available): "If you have a specific budget constraint — a fiscal year challenge, a timing issue — let's talk about what we can do to bridge the gap. A phase-in ramp is on the table. What I can't do is hold a legacy discount rate indefinitely."
