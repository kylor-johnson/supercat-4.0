Migration driver: what you're correcting

Driver: discount_correction

International Home Miami signed in 2024 at a discounted rate of $725/month — approximately 15% below the standard T1 Catalog Essentials rate of $849/month. ($849 − $725 = $124; $124 / $849 ≈ 14.6% discount.) As part of the 2026 pricing standardization, account-level discounts are being retired across the full install base. The entire $124/month change is the discount removal; the tier and user structure are unchanged.

Likely objections — with responses

"Why is this happening now?"
"We've been working through a full pricing standardization this year — the first time we've applied a consistent structure across all accounts. Every account at every tier is moving to the same rate card in 2026."
"We just signed in 2024. Why is the price already changing?"
This is the most likely tenure objection for a 2024 cohort — not an early-adopter grievance, but a "we just got here" reaction. Prepare for this specifically.
"Your rate at signing reflected a discount we applied at that time. That discount was tied to an early-commercial arrangement, not a permanent rate. What we're doing now is bringing every account onto the same structure — your tier, service level, and your team's access are all unchanged.
