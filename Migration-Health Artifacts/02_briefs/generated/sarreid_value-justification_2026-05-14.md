---

## Why This Is Happening Now

SuperCat is standardizing its pricing across the full install base for the first time. For the past decade, pricing was set account by account, contract by contract — which meant accounts that joined at different times ended up on materially different rates for the same platform. That's not fair to anyone, and it's not sustainable as a business. 2026 is the year we fix it.

The platform is also a fundamentally different product than it was in 2014. You signed on when SuperCat was early-stage. What you're running today — Commerce Enterprise, with full catalog, cart, portal, and integration infrastructure — is the mature version of that product. The pricing should reflect that.

---

## The Two Things Changing

### 1. Platform base: $1,370 → $2,295

Your platform rate of $1,370/month reflects your original 2014 contract — a period when SuperCat's pricing structure was different. The Commerce Enterprise tier (T3) is priced at $2,295/month. That's a +$925/month change on the platform side.

### 2. User rate: $16/user → graduated standard

Your additional users have been billed at **$16/user** — a rate locked in at signing in 2014. The new graduated curve is:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

With 54 average billable users, and Commerce Enterprise now including 40 users in the base (up from your legacy 25), you have 14 excess users. At the graduated rate, that's $338/month in user charges — down from $608/month previously. The expanded included base absorbs 15 more of your team.

### The net

| | Monthly Impact |
|---|---|
| Platform base correction | +$925 |
| User rate correction + included user expansion | −$270 |
| **Net change** | **+$655/month** |
