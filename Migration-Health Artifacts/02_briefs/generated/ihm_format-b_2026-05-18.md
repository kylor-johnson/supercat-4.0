# International Home Miami: Your Pricing Is Changing

*May 18, 2026*

---

Effective **July 17, 2026**, International Home Miami's monthly subscription moves from **$725** to **$849** — an increase of **$124/month (+17.1%)**.

SuperCat is standardizing its pricing across the full install base for the first time. For years, pricing was set account by account at signing — which meant accounts that joined at different times ended up on materially different rates for the same platform. That's not fair to anyone, and 2026 is the year we fix it.

The math appendix at the end of this letter shows the full calculation. Here is the plain-language explanation.

---

## Why Your Number Is Changing

Your current pricing reflects an approximately 15% discount applied at signing in 2024. As part of this refresh, legacy account-level discounts are being retired across the install base. All accounts are moving to the same standard tier pricing. Your new monthly rate is the Catalog Essentials (T1) standard rate for your account profile.

| | Monthly Impact |
|---|---|
| Discount removal | +$124 |
| User structure update | N/A |
| **Net change** | **+$124/month** |

---

## What You're Getting at $849/Month

Catalog Essentials is SuperCat's entry point — the iPad rep app and buyer-facing catalog running on shared infrastructure. Your reps carry your full catalog offline, write orders in the field, and sync back. The tier covers your included users and standard support.

---

## How This Compares

Across T1 accounts after migration, monthly pricing ranges from **$774 to $1,629** at the 25th–75th percentile. At $849, International Home Miami is at the low end of the peer range — reflecting your account size and profile.

Every account in the install base is moving to this same structure.

*(T1 p25–p75: $774–$1,629/mo | T2: $1,295–$1,589/mo | T3: $2,295–$2,875/mo)*

---

## What Doesn't Change

Your workflow, your team's access, your catalog, your integrations, and your data — none of that changes. The platform operates identically. The only change is the invoice.
