SuperCat is standardizing its pricing across the full install base for the first time. For years, pricing was set account by account at signing — which meant accounts that joined at different times, or under multi-entity programs, ended up on materially different rates for the same platform. That's not fair to anyone, and 2026 is the year we fix it.

The math appendix at the end of this letter shows the full calculation. Here is the plain-language explanation.

Driver: multi-org retirement + user rate standardization. WAC/Modern Forms was enrolled in SuperCat's multi-organization pricing program as a WAC Group rollup child. That program — and the cross-entity discounts it applied — is being retired. Each entity moves to individual standard T1 pricing.

1. Program rate → standard tier rate. Your platform base moves from $652.50 (multi-org program rate) to $749.00 (T1 standard) — a +$96.50/month change.

2. User pricing standardization. Your user rate moves from $18.00/user flat to the graduated standard. The $18.00 rate reflected a 10% multi-org discount applied on top of your original $20.00 contracted rate. Under the standard structure the included user base is 10 (T1 standard), and users above that base are billed at the graduated rate. Based on your 97-user trailing average, 87 users fall above the standard included base.

What you're getting at $2,495.00/month

Catalog Essentials is SuperCat's entry point — the iPad rep app and buyer-facing catalog running on shared infrastructure. Your reps carry your full catalog offline, write orders in the field, and sync back to a single source of record. The tier covers 10 included users with additional users billed at the standard graduated rate, plus standard support. That is exactly what your team is running today across your full rep network.
