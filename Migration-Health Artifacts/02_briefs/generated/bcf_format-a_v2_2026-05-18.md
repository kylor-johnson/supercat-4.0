## Why the Number Is Changing

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2022. That rate reflects the pricing in place at signing — it hasn't been updated to match the current rate card, and we're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Here's how that plays out for your account:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,880 | $2,295 (T3 — Commerce Enterprise) |
| Included users | 25 | **40** |
| Additional billable users | 25 users at $20 = **$500/month** | 4 users at graduated rate = **$100/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$2,380** | **$2,395** |

The new included base of 40 users absorbs 21 of your current contracted additional users — those users no longer generate a charge. The net effect on your user charges is –$400/month. The platform base normalization offsets that reduction, landing your net change at +$15/month.
