## What B2B Cart Changes — Specifically

Right now, every order Magnussen processes runs through a sales rep: the buyer calls or emails, the rep takes the order, enters it, confirms it. For complex orders, custom configurations, and new customer relationships, that rep involvement is the right call. For routine reorders and standard SKUs, it's rep time that could be going elsewhere.

**B2B Cart gives buyers a checkout flow directly in the catalog.** They browse, they build an order with Magnussen's pricing and product rules, and they submit — without rep involvement for the transaction.

What this changes:
- **Reorders move off the rep's plate.** A buyer who needs to reorder the same items they've ordered before doesn't need a rep to take that order. B2B Cart handles it.
- **After-hours orders get captured.** A buyer needs to submit an order on Sunday night before a Monday delivery window. Right now that either waits until Monday or doesn't happen. B2B Cart captures it.
- **Rep capacity concentrates on high-value work.** With routine orders self-serve, the 76-rep team focuses on new accounts, complex orders, and customer relationships that actually require them.

With 63 average users actively working in the platform, even a modest shift of reorders to self-serve represents meaningful time recovered per quarter.
