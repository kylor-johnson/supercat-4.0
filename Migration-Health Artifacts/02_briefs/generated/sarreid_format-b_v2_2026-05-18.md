# Sarreid, Ltd.: Your Pricing Is Changing

*May 18, 2026*

---

Sarreid has been on SuperCat since 2014 — twelve years. In that time, your team has built a Commerce Enterprise operation running at full strength: 5,990 logins in the last 90 days, 60 active reps consistently in the field, every configured feature in use, every channel producing commerce outcomes. Your monthly subscription is moving from **$1,978** to **$2,633** — an increase of **$655/month (33.1%)** — and this letter explains exactly why.

You've been with us since 2014, and I want to address this directly because you deserve that. A change of this size warrants a real explanation, not a form letter.

SuperCat is standardizing its pricing across the full install base for the first time. For years, pricing was set account by account at signing — which meant accounts that joined at different times ended up on materially different rates for the same platform. That's not fair to anyone, and 2026 is the year we fix it. The platform is also a fundamentally different product than it was in 2014. What you're running today — five connected surfaces sharing one catalog, one customer file, and one order infrastructure across rep, buyer, and analytics workflows — is a commercial operating system. The pricing should reflect that.

The math appendix at the end of this letter shows the full calculation. Here is the plain-language explanation.

---

## Why Your Number Is Changing

Two things are changing on your invoice:

**1. Platform base: $1,370 → $2,295**
Your platform rate reflects your original 2014 contract — a rate set when SuperCat's pricing structure was materially different from what it is today. The Commerce Enterprise (T3) base rate is $2,295. That's a +$925/month change on the platform side.

**2. User rate: $16/user → graduated standard**
Your additional users have been billed at **$16/user** — a rate locked in at your 2014 signing. The new graduated curve is:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Your included user base expands from 25 to 40 — absorbing users who previously generated a per-user charge. With 14 excess users remaining at the new graduated rate, your user charges decrease by $270/month.

**The net:**

| | Monthly Impact |
|---|---|
| Platform base correction | +$925 |
| User rate correction + included user expansion | –$270 |
| **Net change** | **+$655/month** |

---

## What You're Getting at $2,633/Month

Commerce Enterprise is SuperCat's full commercial operating system — five connected surfaces sharing one catalog, one customer file, and one order infrastructure across every channel your sales team and buyers use. Your current stack — iPad App, Catalog, and Portal — is fully covered at this tier. The subscription also includes B2B Cart (buyer self-serve ordering you can activate at any time), the full Insights Layer with rep performance scorecards, cross-customer benchmarking against similar manufacturers, dormant customer signals, and 42 queryable value moments across 8 domains. Plus 40 included users, a dedicated CSM, priority support, and CPQ/Credit Card with no additional line item. This is the tier that correctly reflects the infrastructure you've built.

---

## How This Compares

Across T3 accounts after migration, monthly pricing ranges from **$2,295 to $2,875** at the 25th–75th percentile. At $2,633, Sarreid is above the midpoint — reflecting your 14 excess users above the standard 40-user included base. For external context: equivalent platforms offering comparable full-stack commercial operating scope are priced at $3,000–$3,500/month at comparable user scales.

Every account in the install base is moving to this same structure.

*(T1 p25–p75: $774–$1,629/mo | T2: $1,295–$1,589/mo | T3: $2,295–$2,875/mo)*

---

## What Doesn't Change

Your workflow, your team's access, your catalog, your integrations, and your data — none of that changes. The platform operates identically. The only change is the invoice.
