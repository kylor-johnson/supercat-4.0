## Why the Number Is Changing

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2022. That rate reflects the pricing in place at signing — it hasn't been updated to match the current rate card, and we're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Here's how that plays out for your account specifically:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **Platform base** | $1,880/month (legacy) | $2,295/month (Commerce Enterprise) |
| **Included users** | 25 (legacy standard) | **40** (Commerce Enterprise) |
| **Additional billable users** | 25 users at $20 = **$500/month** | 4 users at $25 = **$100/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$2,380** | **$2,395** |

*Note: The legacy charge reflects 25 additional users billed under your 2022 contract terms; the new-side charge reflects your current trailing 12-month average of 44 active users, of which 4 are above Commerce Enterprise's 40-user included base.*

The platform base went up $415. Your user charge went down $400 — because 21 of your current additional users are now absorbed into Commerce Enterprise's 40-user included base. Those 21 users stopped generating a charge.

The net is **+$15/month**. That's the full scope of the change.
