## Why Your Number Is Changing

WAC/Modern Forms Lighting has been part of SuperCat's multi-organization pricing program — a structure that applied cross-entity benefits across WAC Group and its related accounts. That program is being retired as part of this refresh. Each entity moves to individual standard T1 pricing.

Two things are changing:

**1. Program rate → standard tier rate**
Your platform base moves from $652.50 (multi-org program rate) to $749.00 (T1 standard) — a +$96.50/month change.

**2. User pricing standardization**
Your user rate moves from $18.00/user flat to the graduated standard. The $18.00 rate reflected a 10% multi-org discount applied on top of your original $20.00 contracted rate. Under the standard structure, the included user base is 10 (T1 standard), and users above that base are billed at the graduated rate. Based on your 97-user trailing average, 87 users fall above the standard included base.

| | Monthly Impact |
|---|---|
| Multi-org program retirement (platform base) | +$96.50 |
| User rate standardization (rate + included base) | +$648.00 |
| **Net change** | **+$744.50/month** |

---

## What You're Getting at $2,495.00/Month

Catalog Essentials is SuperCat's entry point — the iPad rep app and buyer-facing catalog running on shared infrastructure. Your reps carry your full catalog offline, write orders in the field, and sync back to a single source of record. The tier covers 10 included users with additional users billed at the standard graduated rate, plus standard support. That is exactly what your team is running today across your full rep network.

---

## How This Compares

Across T1 accounts after migration, monthly pricing ranges from **$774 to $1,629** at the 25th–75th percentile. At $2,495.00, WAC/Modern Forms Lighting falls above that range — which reflects your user scale directly. With 97 active reps against a 10-user included base, the additional-user charge is the primary driver of your invoice at any rate structure. That cost is not a surcharge; it is a function of how many people your team has working inside the platform.

Every account in the install base is moving to this same structure.

Your workflow, your team's access, your catalog, your integrations, and your data — none of that changes. The platform operates identically. The only change is the invoice.
