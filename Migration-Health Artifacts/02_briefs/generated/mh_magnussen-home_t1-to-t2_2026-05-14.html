What Commerce Professional adds

Two capabilities. Everything else in your current setup — iPad App, catalog, rep tools, Admin Console — stays exactly the same.

B2B Cart

Buyers get a checkout flow directly in your catalog. They browse using your product rules and pricing, build an order, and submit — without involving a rep for the transaction.

Order & Invoice Tracking

Buyers see their own order status, invoice history, and account activity — any time, without calling your team. "Where's my order?" becomes self-serve.

What B2B Cart specifically changes for your operation

Reorders move off the rep

A buyer reordering the same items they've ordered before doesn't need a rep to take that order. B2B Cart handles it — rep's time goes to higher-value work.

After-hours orders captured

Sunday night before a Monday delivery window — that order currently waits until Monday, or doesn't happen. B2B Cart captures it the moment the buyer submits.

Rep capacity concentrates

With 76 active reps, even a modest shift of reorders to self-serve represents meaningful time freed per quarter — directed toward new accounts and complex orders.

What Order & Invoice Tracking specifically changes

Buyer experience matches expectations

Self-serve account visibility is the default expectation from every other vendor your buyers work with. This brings Magnussen in line.

Team time shifts

Status-check time redirects to customer development — lower-value contacts are handled by the platform, not your team.
