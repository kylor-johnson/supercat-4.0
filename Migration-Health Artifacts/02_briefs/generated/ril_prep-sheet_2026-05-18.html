Migration driver: what you're correcting

Driver: discount_correction (secondary: included_user_gain)

Ratana signed in 2024 on a monthly deal at $2,155/month total — $1,880 platform component plus $275 in user charges (11 users above the 25-user included base at $25/user). The legacy platform rate of $1,880 is below the current T3 standard. As part of the 2026 standardization, all accounts move to T3 Commerce Enterprise at $2,295/month with 40 included users.

The mechanics of the delta: the platform component increases by $415 (from $1,880 to $2,295 base), but the expanded included-user base (25 → 40) absorbs all 11 current additional users, eliminating the $275/month user charge entirely. Net delta: +$140/month (+6.5%).
Embedded benefit
Secondary driver (included_user_gain) is an embedded benefit of the migration — Ratana gains 15 additional included users at no incremental charge, and their trailing average user count (31) sits comfortably within the new 40-user base with headroom to add up to 9 more reps before incurring user charges.

Likely objections — with responses

"Why is this happening now?"
"We've been working through a full pricing standardization this year — the first time we've applied a consistent structure across all accounts. Your rate was set two years ago when you came on board and hasn't been updated since."
"My original rate was part of a deal — I earned that."
"Your rate reflects where the platform was priced when you signed in 2024. We're not pretending it was arbitrary. What we're doing now is bringing every account onto the same structure, because running materially different rates indefinitely isn't fair to accounts who joined later at book rate."
"Why us? Are other accounts getting this too?"
"Yes — 100% of the install base is moving to the same structure. You're not being singled out. Accounts at every tier and tenure level are getting the same correction. Your number reflects your user count and tier, nothing else."
"This is a 6.5% increase. That's not something we can absorb easily."
"I understand that. The effective date is July 17, which gives you two billing cycles to plan for it. At $140/month, we're not talking about a significant operational number here — and with the new 40-user base, you have room to add nearly a third more reps before seeing any user charges. Happy to walk through the math if that's helpful."
"What happens if we say no?"
Option 1 — firm"This is a standard migration we're completing across the full install base. The effective date is July 17, 2026. I want to work with you to make the transition smooth, but the pricing structure itself is the same for everyone."
Option 2 — accommodation available"If you have a specific timing constraint — a fiscal year boundary or budget cycle issue — let's talk about what we can do. What I can't do is hold a legacy rate indefinitely."
