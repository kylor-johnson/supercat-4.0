# Sarreid, Ltd.: Your Pricing Is Changing

*May 18, 2026*

---

Effective **July 17, 2026**, Sarreid's monthly subscription moves from **$1,978** to **$2,633** — an increase of **$655/month (+33.1%)**.

You've been with us since 2014, and I want to address this directly because you deserve that. A change of this size warrants a real explanation, not a form letter.

SuperCat is standardizing its pricing across the full install base for the first time. For years, pricing was set account by account at signing — which meant accounts that joined at different times ended up on materially different rates for the same platform. That's not fair to anyone, and 2026 is the year we fix it. The platform is also a fundamentally different product than it was in 2014. What you're running today — iPad rep ordering, a buyer-facing digital catalog, and a full sales portal operating on one shared catalog, one customer file, and one order infrastructure — is a commercial operating system. The pricing should reflect that.

The math appendix at the end of this letter shows the full calculation. Here is the plain-language explanation.

---

## Why Your Number Is Changing

Two things are changing on your invoice:

**1. Platform base: $1,370 → $2,295**
Your platform rate — covering iPad, Catalog, and Portal — reflects your original 2014 contract. The T3 Commerce Enterprise base rate is $2,295/month. That's a +$925/month change on the platform side.

**2. User rate: $16/user → graduated standard**
Your additional users have been billed at **$16/user** — a rate locked in at your 2014 signing, before SuperCat established a consistent rate card. The new graduated curve is:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Your included user base is also expanding from 25 to 40 — absorbing 15 users who no longer generate a per-user charge. With 14 excess users at the graduated rate, your user charges decrease by $270/month.

**The net:**

| | Monthly Impact |
|---|---|
| Platform base correction | +$925 |
| User rate correction + included user expansion | –$270 |
| **Net change** | **+$655/month** |

---

## What You're Getting at $2,633/Month

Commerce Enterprise is SuperCat's full commercial operating system — a single infrastructure layer connecting your rep-facing iPad app, your buyer-facing digital catalog, and your sales portal from one shared catalog, one customer file, and one order engine. You're already running all three. At T3, that infrastructure includes 40 active users, a dedicated CSM, priority support, and the Insights Layer: rep performance scorecards, cross-customer benchmarking against similar manufacturers, dormant customer signals, and queryable value analytics across your account portfolio. If Sarreid ever wants to add B2B Cart, CPQ, or Credit Card processing, they're included in the tier with no additional line item. This tier reflects what you're running — and what you can expand into from here.

---

## How This Compares

Across T3 accounts after migration, monthly pricing ranges from **$2,295 to $2,875** at the 25th–75th percentile. At $2,633, Sarreid is slightly above the midpoint — reflecting your user count and a legacy rate set in 2014. For external context: equivalent platforms offering comparable full-stack commercial operating scope are priced at $3,000–$3,500/month at comparable user scales.

Every account in the install base is moving to this same structure.

*(T1 p25–p75: $774–$1,629/mo | T2: $1,295–$1,589/mo | T3: $2,295–$2,875/mo)*

Your workflow, your team's access, your catalog, your integrations, and your data — none of that changes. The platform operates identically. The only change is the invoice.
