## Migration Driver: What You're Correcting

**user_rate_normalization + platform_base_correction (dual driver)**

Sarreid signed in 2014 at a $1,370/month platform rate and $16/user — both early-adopter rates set before SuperCat established a consistent rate card. Two corrections are being applied simultaneously:

1. **Platform base correction:** $1,370 → $2,295/month (+$925). The T3 Commerce Enterprise standard rate is $2,295. Sarreid has been on a below-book platform rate for 12 years.
2. **User rate correction + included-base expansion:** The $16/user flat rate moves to the graduated standard ($25/$22/$20/$18). The included user base simultaneously expands from 25 to 40 users — absorbing users who were previously subject to the per-user charge. Net user charge change: $608 → $338 (–$270/month).

Net delta: +$925 – $270 = **+$655/month (+33.1%).**

The large delta is the two corrections compounding. This is not a revenue expansion move — it is a structural correction bringing a 12-year-old contract to the 2026 rate card that every other account is on.
