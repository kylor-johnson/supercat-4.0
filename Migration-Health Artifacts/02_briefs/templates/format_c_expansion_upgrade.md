# Format C: Expansion Brief — Tier Upgrade Opportunity
*Template | SuperCat Migration-Health Artifacts*

---

**When to use this template:** Accounts where migration has been confirmed and the account has signaled positive acceptance — and where a genuine upgrade opportunity exists. This brief is never sent at the same time as the normalization brief. It requires a positive signal from the migration conversation first.

**Who sends it:** CS team / CSM — this is a conversation-support document, not a cold send. The CSM uses this to prepare for the conversation and may share it as a follow-up after a call, not before.

**Two subtypes:**
- T1→T2: pitch is B2B Cart + Order & Invoice Tracking (specific operational unlock)
- T2→T3: pitch is Sales Intelligence + user seat math (data workflow + per-user economics)

**Routing gate:** Do not queue this brief if:
- Account has expressed any objection to the migration brief
- Account is in Watch band AND delta_pct > 20%
- Account is At Risk or Critical (any delta)
- CSM has an open support issue with the account

## What Sales Intelligence Changes — Specifically

Most T2 accounts pull order data manually for quarterly reviews: export from SuperCat, format in Excel, send to the team. **Sales Intelligence replaces that workflow** — order trends, territory performance, rep activity, and buyer engagement are in dashboards, updated automatically, and exportable to your ERP.

But the deeper unlock at T3 is the **Insights Layer** — SuperCat's cross-customer intelligence framework. Because SuperCat runs the same platform across the full install base, we can surface benchmarks that no single-instance analytics tool can produce: how your rep team's behavior compares to similar manufacturers, which selling patterns correlate with order outcomes, where dormant customers represent recoverable GMV, which products are being browsed but not ordered.

What this changes in practice:
- **Quarterly reporting prep drops from hours to minutes.** The data is already assembled.
- **Rep performance is visible without a spreadsheet project.** Which reps are active, which territories are quiet, where volume is concentrated — and how each rep's behavioral pattern compares to the peer median.
- **Dormant customers are identified and prioritized automatically.** Buyers who haven't ordered in 90+ days, ranked by trailing GMV — the save list is produced, not manually built.
- **Cross-customer benchmarking is live.** How does your catalog activation rate, rep engagement, and order velocity compare to similar manufacturers running the same platform? That benchmark exists only at T3.

This is not an analytics tool for an enterprise ops team. It's a workflow tool that eliminates the data-gathering work your team is already doing manually — and adds the cross-instance intelligence layer that no competitor can replicate.
