## What this is

A weekly read on every onboarding client answering one question per client:

> Where are they in the onboarding lifecycle, and what is the next concrete thing that has to happen to advance?

Output: one dated markdown file at `output/{YYYY-MM-DD}-phase-assessment.md`.

## What changed vs the old model

- "Stages 1–11" → "Phases 1–7", terminating at Go-Live. Once a client hits Phase 7 they leave the cohort entirely. Post-live monitoring is a separate concern, not this framework's problem.
- The 11 old stages were data-completeness facets graded in parallel; the framework forced them into a linear ladder, which produced "out of sequence" apologies in the v2 output. The 7 phases are an actual lifecycle position. Within a phase, postgres facets are graded; that keeps the quantitative signal without the false-ladder artifact.
- HelpScout + Fathom signals are PART of the phase definition, not bolted on as a separate "validation layer." A phase like Admin Training is detected from HelpScout author/recipient patterns directly. The HelpScout/Fathom mix is itself a phase signal.
- Ambiguity is a first-class output. Cases where signals contradict are surfaced as flags, not silently smoothed. The standup decides; the agent doesn't.
- **Integration approach is tracked as a parallel workstream, not inside Phase 6** — verified empirically across TCS Catsy, LIBCO BC, and MALI Endeavour. None of those integrations gates or is gated by a phase.

## Change log

- 2026-08-18 — **v3.6 (first ground-truth validation).** The framework was run against an independent blind reconstruction of TCD's onboarding, built only from raw Fathom transcripts, HelpScout threads, and the live DB, by an agent barred from reading any interpretation document. Full result: `ground-truth/SCORECARD.md`.
  **What held:** both backtestable transitions matched exactly — Phase 2 on 2025-11-11 and Phase 3 on 2025-12-09. Every checkable claim in the blind read was confirmed against Postgres with zero corrections. The Phase 1–3 anchors are sound.
  **What changed:** (1) **Schema fix — `customers.company_name` does not exist**; Phase 7 clause 3 now reads `customers.name`. Run verbatim, the old clause threw `UndefinedColumn` on every Phase 7 evaluation, so the framework could never complete a run (`Phase_Anchors.md`). (2) **HubSpot removed from `client_domains[]` resolution** — it resolves 3 of 7 clients; Postgres alone resolves 7 of 7 (`Phase_Anchors.md`, `RUN_PROMPT.md`). (3) **New § Phase regression — the at-risk state:** phases are not a ratchet. TCD's Phase 3 clause 1 failed for six weeks during a near-churn that a blind read independently dated to the same window; v3.5 had no way to say so. (4) **New § Counting HelpScout threads:** deduplicate on normalized subject (21 of 31 TCD tickets collapse to 9 conversations) and require ≥5 threads before any ratio fires — TCD's Phase 7 clause 4 fired at 100% on a denominator of 2. Applies to Phase 6 trigger #4 and Phase 7 clause 4.
  **Known-unfixed, recorded not resolved:** phases are a checklist, not a sequence — Phase 7 needs any two of four clauses, so Phase 6 is skippable by construction (TCD went live 15 days before admin training, which never happened as a session). And there is no "live but not selling" state: TCD satisfies every Phase 7 condition while one rep wrote 100% of its orders and 23 of 24 have never opened one. See `SCORECARD.md` § D2, § D3.
  Files touched: `Phase_Anchors.md`, `Phase_Progression_Framework.md`, `RUN_PROMPT.md`, plus `collector/` (dead `import_active` dropped, `ipad_visible_products` and rep-order concentration added).
