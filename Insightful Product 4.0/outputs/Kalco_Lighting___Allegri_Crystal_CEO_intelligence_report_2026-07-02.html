What this report can't see

Each of these arrives the day the data does:

  • True margin (gross/net profit). No cost data. We can tell you who's growing and who's fading — we can't tell you who's profitable.
  • Returns / credit memos. Present (901 credit memos LTM) but not decomposed into a returns view.
  • AR and collections. Terms billed, not collected.
  • Stock-outs. Backorder field is unpopulated.
  • Confirmed competitive losses. Need a second booking source.
  • The "why" behind any decline. The data shows the slope. The cause is a phone call.

Two specific upgrades worth queuing

  • A cost feed (any channel). Adds margin to every finding in this report. Lets us tell you not just who's growing in revenue, but who's growing in profit dollars — by account, by rep, by SKU. The single biggest unlock available.
  • An item-level invoice-date series. Calls the seasonal shape of your growth families — which SKU peaks at which market, which dealers reorder within 90 days — and turns a static cross-sell list into a calendared play.

How to trust these numbers

Every dollar in this report is a direct result from your invoice data — same method, same window, reproduces exactly. Where a figure is directional (estimated upside, addressable subset), it is flagged inline.

Every dollar in this report was pulled live from Kalco Lighting / Allegri Crystal's ERP invoice data on 2026-07-01 (read-only). Topline figures are billed-not-collected and cover the trailing 12 months vs the same prior 12-month window — the only calendar-neutral comparison in a market-driven industry.

Two things worth your knowing:

  • One view of your business. Invoice data is the single canonical source; cross-channel completeness cannot be claimed from one source alone. "Your invoiced business" is the honest framing, not "your total business."
  • Rep identity is clean. 47 of 47 invoice reps were named via the order-rep bridge (100.0%). Every coaching card uses a named person, not a code.
