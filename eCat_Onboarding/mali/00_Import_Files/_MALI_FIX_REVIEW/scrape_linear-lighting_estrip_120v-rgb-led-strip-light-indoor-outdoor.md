# 120V AC RGB eStrip

![Image 18: Product Category Outdoor](https://magiclite.com/wp-content/themes/magic-lite/img/outdoor_Circle.png)Outdoor![Image 19: Product Category RGB](https://magiclite.com/wp-content/themes/magic-lite/img/rgb_Circle.png)RGB![Image 20: Product Category 120V](https://magiclite.com/wp-content/themes/magic-lite/img/120v_Circle.png)120V
The 120V RGB LED strip light offers dynamic, customizable lighting in a compact, easy-to-install design. Powered by 18 SMD 5050 LEDs per foot, this strip delivers smooth, vibrant illumination with an output of 67 lumens per foot (220 lumens per meter). Built for durability, the strip is IP67-rated, making it resistant to water and dust, and operates reliably across a temperature range of -20°C to 60°C. With a maximum single-run length of 164 feet (50 meters), it eliminates the need for additional connections or power sources in large installations.

**Salient Features:**

*   **Vivid RGB Lighting:** Provides vibrant, customizable colours for dynamic lighting effects.
*   **Long Continuous Runs:** Supports single runs up to 164 feet (50 meters) without interruptions.
*   **Indoor and Outdoor Ready:** IP67 rating ensures protection against water and dust.
*   **Compact Design:** Slim dimensions (0.59 x 0.31 inches / 15 x 8 mm) for versatile applications.
*   **Dimmable Functionality:** Compatible with controllers for smooth brightness adjustments.
*   **Wide Operating Range:** Performs efficiently in temperatures from -20°C to 60°C.
*   **Certification and Warranty:** ETL certification ensures compliance with safety standards, accompanied by a warranty of 2 years.

Whether you’re transforming a home, enhancing a commercial property, or elevating your outdoor lighting setup, the 120V RGB strip is the perfect solution. Designed for seamless installations up to 164 ft, it combines long-lasting performance with the versatility of RGB lighting, ensuring vibrant colour control.

_*Please note that this product is not field cuttable and requires assembly to be completed at the warehouse._
