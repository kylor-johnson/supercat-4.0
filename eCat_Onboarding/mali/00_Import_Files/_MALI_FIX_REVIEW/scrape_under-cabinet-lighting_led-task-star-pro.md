# LED Task Star PRO

![Image 12: Product Category 120V](https://magiclite.com/wp-content/themes/magic-lite/img/120v_Circle.png)120V
Elevate your lighting experience with the LED Task Star Pro (LTSPRO) – the innovative under cabinet LED light bar that redefines convenience in your everyday life.

**Features of LED Task Star Pro –**

*   **Sleek Design:** The LTSPRO boasts a slim, one-inch low-profile design, ensuring it discreetly fits beneath your cabinets while delivering ample illumination.

*   **Customizable Kelvin Color Temperature:** Effortlessly tailor the lighting to your preferences using LTSPRO’s Kelvin color temperature control, offering both warm and cool lighting options.

*   **Glare-Free Illumination:** Thanks to cutting-edge edge-lit technology, the LTSPRO provides uniformly distributed, pixel-free lighting that enhances the appearance of your countertop.

*   **Exceptional Color Rendering (90 CRI):** Witness true-to-life color representation, making the LTSPRO the perfect LED task light for work that demands precision.

*   **Energy Efficiency (Up to 60 Lumens per Watt):** The LTSPRO offers energy-efficient lighting, reducing electricity expenses while maintaining optimal brightness.

*   **Extended Lifespan (Rated for 50,000 Hours):** Bid farewell to frequent replacements, as the LTSPRO boasts an extended operational lifespan.

*   **Dimming at Your Fingertips:** Easily set the desired ambiance by dimming LTSPRO with compatible ELV dimmers.

**Why opt for Magic Lite’s LED Task Star Pro?**

This LED task light offers simplicity, energy savings, and precise color rendering. The comprehensive installation manual and included components ensure a hassle-free setup process.

*   **Effortless Installation:**The LTSPRO arrives complete with all the necessary components, including secure mounting screws, a cord and plug, an end-to-end connector, and a romex connector for hardwiring. Follow the step-by-step user manual for a trouble-free installation.
*   **Interconnection:**When connecting multiple fixtures, remember that the first fixture should be powered from the right side (INPUT), while subsequent fixtures can be connected using either an in-line connector or jumper cable. Refer to the installation manual for in-depth installation instructions.
