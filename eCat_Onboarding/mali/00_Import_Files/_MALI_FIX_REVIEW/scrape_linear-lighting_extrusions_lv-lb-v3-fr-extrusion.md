The LV-LB-V3-FR is a corner surface mounted aluminum extrusion designed to protect and enhance LED strip installations. It comes with a frosted PC cover to diffuse light evenly and reduce glare.

****Product Codes**:**

*   LV-LB-V3-FR

****End Caps**:**

*   LV-V1/V3-EC (2/bag)

*   LV-V1/V3-EC-WF (2/bag)

**Mounting Clips:**

*   LV-V1/V3-MC (6/bag)

The LV-LB-V3-FR is ideal for corner lighting applications in residential, commercial, and retail settings, providing a clean and modern lighting solution.
