# Switchex

Switchex® simplifies LED array lighting systems by combining an in-wall LED dimmer switch and power supply into a single integrated unit. Switchex® mounts in a standard in-wall switch box, accepts 120V AC and converts to low voltage DC. Switchex® is compatible with 2-channel 12V and 24VDC tape light and constant voltage fixtures.
