Migration Confidence Matrix (2D Risk)
Two-dimensional classification combines price delta band (6 levels) with client health band (5 levels + Unscored) to produce a migration confidence level. This replaces v5's one-dimensional risk labels as the primary operational classification.
ConfidenceMeaning
value_ledHigh health absorbs significant increase. Lead with outcomes.
confidentStrong relationship supports migration. Straightforward conversation.
standardHealthy enough for standard migration with value narrative.
carefulMixed health or meaningful increase. Account-owner involvement needed.
high_touchLow health + significant increase. Re-engagement must precede pricing.
critical_holdDo not lead with pricing. Recovery conversation first.
zero_frictionPrice decrease on healthy account. Communicate and move on.
watch_sequenceMinimal change but health needs monitoring.

Health-Adjusted Churn ScenariosProbability modulated by health band
Base churn rates are modulated by health band: Thriving accounts have 0.5× the base rate, Healthy 0.8×, Watch 1.3×, At Risk 1.8×, Critical 2.5×. Ghost accounts get an additional +10pp penalty.
Migration Playbook BackboneRevenue-optimized sequencing by confidence level
Accounts are sequenced for maximum MRR realization: within each confidence level, accounts are sorted by delta dollars descending. Health scores serve as a triage mechanism — they determine the approach and preparation required, not the sequencing priority.
