## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: user_rate_normalization**

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2011. That rate reflects the pricing in place at signing — it hasn't been updated to match the current rate card, and we're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

Your platform base is also adjusting — from $1,515 (the 2011 rate for Commerce Enterprise) to $2,295 (the current Commerce Enterprise standard).

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Here's how that plays out for your account:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,515 (Commerce Enterprise, 2011 pricing) | $2,295 (Commerce Enterprise, current standard) |
| Included users | 25 | **40** |
| Additional billable users | excess users at $20 = **$1,360/month** | 38 users at graduated rate = **$840/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$2,875** | **$3,135** |

The new included base of 40 absorbs 15 of your previously billed additional users — those users no longer generate a charge. Your team of 78 means 38 remain above the included base. Those 38 users break down as: 10 users × $25 = $250, plus 15 users × $22 = $330, plus 13 users × $20 = $260, totaling $840/month.
