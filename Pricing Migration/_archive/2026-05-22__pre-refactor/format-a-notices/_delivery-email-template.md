# Format A — Delivery Email Template
*60-Day Notice | ≤10% delta | CS-sent*

---

**When to use:** Accounts where delta ≤10% AND absolute delta is modest. The Format A brief document does the work. This email is the wrapper that delivers it — nothing more. No persuasion, no call commitment, no expansion language.

**Who sends it:** CS team. No CEO co-signature.

**Length:** 4 sentences. Do not exceed.

**What is never in this email:**
- A percentage figure (the brief has it; the email leads with dollars)
- "We're adjusting your pricing" — forbidden
- "Modest," "small," "minor" — minimizing language is prohibited
- An apology
- Any mention of other accounts, tiers, or expansion opportunities
- A pre-committed call (that's Format B — Format A accounts get a passive offer in the brief, not here)

**Subject:** [ACCOUNT NAME]: your SuperCat invoice is changing — effective [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

Hi [CONTACT NAME],

Effective **[EFFECTIVE_DATE]**, your monthly invoice is moving from **$[CURRENT_MRR]** to **$[NEW_MRR]** — a change of **+$[DELTA]/month**. I've attached a summary that explains exactly why the number is changing and what's included at the new rate.

[USE ONE — delete the other:]
[DEFAULT]: Your workflow, your team's access, your catalog, and your integrations are unchanged. The only thing changing is the invoice.
[IF `included_user_reduction` is primary or secondary driver]: Your workflow, your catalog, and your integrations are unchanged. The invoice and the included user count are both changing — the attached document explains exactly how.

If you'd like to talk through anything before [EFFECTIVE_DATE] — reach out directly. Happy to get on a call.

[CSM NAME] | Customer Success | SuperCat

> **Pre-send checklist:**
> - [ ] Attach the completed Format A brief PDF (generated from the brief template)
> - [ ] Confirm [EFFECTIVE_DATE] is at least 60 days from send date
> - [ ] Confirm account is not on HOLD (entity packet, health flag, or unscored)
> - [ ] Confirm annual contract accounts have 90-day notice window applied, not 60
> - [ ] Remove internal routing block above before sending
>
> **After sending:**
> - Log send date in CRM; start notice window clock
> - The brief's close actively invites a direct conversation about the rate — the email reinforces this; no additional follow-up call is required unless the account responds
> - Day 10: if no acknowledgment, send one-line follow-up (see below)
> - If account responds positively AND is expansion eligible: queue Format C — separate email, separate conversation

---

**Day 10 follow-up (if no acknowledgment):**

Hi [CONTACT NAME],

Just checking the above reached you — wanted to make sure the invoice change on [EFFECTIVE_DATE] isn't a surprise when it lands.

[CSM NAME] | SuperCat

---

**Voice calibration notes:**

The goal of this email is zero friction. The account is Thriving or Healthy, the change is small, and the brief document handles the full explanation. The email's only job is to deliver the brief with enough context that it gets opened, not filed.

Do not over-explain in the email body. If you find yourself adding a third paragraph, you've crossed into Format B territory.

The subject line uses the account name — not "SuperCat pricing update" — because personalization improves open rate and signals this is a direct communication, not a bulk notice.
