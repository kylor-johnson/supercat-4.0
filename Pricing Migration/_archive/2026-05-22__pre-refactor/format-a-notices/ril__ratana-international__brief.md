## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: tier_base_increase**

Ratana International's platform base has been **$1,880/month** since the account was set up in 2024 — a legacy rate for Commerce Enterprise that was set below the current standard at the time of signing. As part of this refresh, all accounts are moving to the current book rate for their tier. The current Commerce Enterprise standard is **$2,295/month**.

Alongside the platform base update, the included-user base for Commerce Enterprise expands from 25 to 40 users. Your team's trailing average of 31 users falls entirely within the new included base — your additional-user charge goes to $0.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,880 (Commerce Enterprise, legacy rate) | $2,295 (Commerce Enterprise, current standard) |
| Included users | 25 | **40** |
| Additional billable users | $275/month | 0 users — **$0/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$2,155** | **$2,295** |

The expanded included base absorbs your entire team — the user charge that previously appeared on your invoice is going away.
