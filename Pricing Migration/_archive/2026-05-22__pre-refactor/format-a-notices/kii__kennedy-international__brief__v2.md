## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: at_book_tier_shift**

When Kennedy International joined in 2015, the Commerce Professional tier was priced at **$1,387/month**. The current standard for Commerce Professional is **$1,295/month**. Your tier is not changing, and neither is what your team has access to.

The included-user base for this tier also standardizes to 15 users going forward. Your team averages 19 users — 4 are above the new included base and billed at the graduated rate.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,387 (Commerce Professional, 2015 pricing) | $1,295 (Commerce Professional, current book rate) |
| Included users | 25 | **15** |
| Additional billable users | 0 users at $20 = **$0/month** | 4 users at graduated rate = **$100/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$1,387** | **$1,395** |

The platform base moves down by $92/month. The 4 users now above the included base are billed at $25/user — $100/month. Net change: +$8/month.
