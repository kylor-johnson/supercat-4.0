## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: user_rate_normalization**

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2011 — a rate that predates the current pricing structure by over a decade. We're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Here's how that plays out for your account:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $2,005 | $2,295 (Commerce Enterprise, T3) |
| Included users | 25 | **40** |
| Additional billable users | 12 users at $20 = **$240/month** | 0 users (within included base) = **$0/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$2,245** | **$2,295** |

The new included base absorbs all of your current additional users — your team averages 32 users, comfortably within the new 40-user included count. User charges drop to zero at the new rate.
