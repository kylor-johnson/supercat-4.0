## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: at_book_tier_shift**

When Kennedy International joined in 2015, the Commerce Professional tier was priced at approximately **$1,387/month** — the rate in the book at that time. The current book rate for Commerce Professional is **$1,295/month**. As part of this refresh, all accounts are moving to the current book price for their tier — your tier is not changing, only the rate.

The included-user base for Commerce Professional also standardizes to 15 users at this tier. Your team's trailing average of 19 users means 4 fall above the included base, billed at the graduated rate:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,387 (Commerce Professional, 2015 pricing) | $1,295 (Commerce Professional, current book rate) |
| Included users | 25 | **15** |
| Additional billable users | 0 users at $20 = **$0/month** | 4 users at graduated rate = **$100/month** |
