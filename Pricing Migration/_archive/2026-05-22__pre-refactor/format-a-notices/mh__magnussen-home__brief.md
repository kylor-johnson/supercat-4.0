## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: user_rate_normalization / included_user_reduction**

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2023. That rate reflects the pricing in place at signing — it hasn't been updated to match the current rate card, and we're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

Your platform base is also adjusting — from $725 (the 2023 rate for Catalog Essentials) to $749 (the current Catalog Essentials standard).

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Here's how that plays out for your account:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $725 (Catalog Essentials, 2023 pricing) | $749 (Catalog Essentials, current standard) |
| Included users | 25 | **10** |
| Additional billable users | excess users at $20 = **$1,020/month** | 53 users at graduated rate = **$1,134/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$1,745** | **$1,883** |

Your team averages around 63 users per month. With 10 users included in the base, 53 fall into the graduated tier: 10 users × $25 = $250, plus 15 users × $22 = $330, plus 25 users × $20 = $500, plus 3 users × $18 = $54, totaling $1,134/month.
