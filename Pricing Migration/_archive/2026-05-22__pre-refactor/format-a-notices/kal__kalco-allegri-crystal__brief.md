## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: user_rate_normalization**

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2019. That rate reflects the pricing in place at signing — it hasn't been updated to match the current rate card, and we're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Here's how that plays out for your account:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,810 | $2,295 (Commerce Enterprise, T3) |
| Included users | 25 | **40** |
| Additional billable users | 39 users at $20 = **$780/month** | 22 users at graduated rate = **$514/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$2,590** | **$2,809** |

The new included base absorbs 15 of your current additional users — those users no longer generate a charge. The remaining 22 users are billed at the graduated rate: 10 users × $25 = $250, plus 12 users × $22 = $264, totaling $514/month. The net effect on your user charges is –$266/month.
