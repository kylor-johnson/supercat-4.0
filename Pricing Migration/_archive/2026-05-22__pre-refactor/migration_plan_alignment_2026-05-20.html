Section 2

Where We Diverge

Seven areas where the two documents are in tension or explicit conflict. Severity indicates impact on the ability to send notices in the next two weeks.

D1 — Critical
Meeting architecture — the biggest gap
Critical
Comms Progress Brief says
Format B actively offers a follow-up meeting in the close. 19 accounts are built with meeting offers as a default feature of the communication.
v3.1 Execution Plan says
No default meeting for Core or Narrative accounts. "The artifact IS the personal outreach for ~100 accounts." Proactive meetings only for entity parents (~10–11 conversations) and Strategic (~4–5). Total proactive meeting load: ~16–17 across the entire migration.
Impact
If Format B goes out as built, CPB implies ~19 proactive meeting offers. v3.1 would reduce this to ~0 (inbound only for Core/Narrative). Decision required before Format B sends.
D2 — Material
Segmentation thresholds — different split criteria
Material
Comms Progress Brief says
Splits on percentage only: ≤10% = Format A; >10% = Format B. CEO Letter = relationship-led selection for delta >20%.
v3.1 Execution Plan says
Splits on two dimensions — percentage AND absolute dollar. Core = 10–20% AND ≤$400/mo absolute. Narrative = >20% OR >$400/mo. CEO exec letter = >$400/mo absolute OR any entity parent (mechanical, not relationship-led).
Impact
Accounts like Currey & Company (+$305/+13.9%) are Format B in CPB but Core in v3.1 — no meeting offer, no full artifact. Hubbardton Forge (+$420/+13.8%) is CEO Letter in CPB; v3.1's absolute threshold also routes it to Narrative + CEO letter — incidentally aligned but for different reasons.
D3 — Material
Value artifact depth — per-order anchor vs. two-axis bespoke HTML
Material
Comms Progress Brief says
Format B includes a per-order cost value anchor (annual subscription ÷ LTM eCat orders), auto-suppressed if >$200. Format B brief = 1–2 page doc + 5-sentence delivery email. Production-ready today.
v3.1 Execution Plan says
Bespoke HTML artifacts for all >$400/mo accounts (~25), composed dynamically on two axes: migration_driver (pricing explanation spine) + health profile (value demonstration). Simplified summaries for $150–400/mo (~20 accounts). Standard notice only for <$150/mo (~40 accounts).
Impact
Current CPB briefs are functional but simpler than v3.1's full artifact vision. The per-order anchor is one data point; v3.1 wants composite health score, 4 dimension scores, dimension narratives, and driver-aware framing. Are current briefs "good enough to send" or are they first drafts of the full artifact?
D4 — Material
Readiness sprint vs. "production-ready now"
Material

Templates and agent prompts look like sprint outputs already. Likely synthesis: Good-News + Format A can send now; Format B holds until objection scripts exist.
