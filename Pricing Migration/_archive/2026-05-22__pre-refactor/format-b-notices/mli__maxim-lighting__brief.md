Your team submitted 86 eCat orders across 65 customers last year, generating $676,993 in sales through the platform. Your monthly invoice is moving from **$1,985** to **$2,297** — a change of **$312/month** — and this document explains exactly why.

SuperCat is standardizing its pricing across all accounts in 2026 — the first time we've applied a consistent structure across the board. Your rate was set at signing and hasn't been updated since. Below is exactly why your number is changing and what you're getting at the new rate.

---

## Why the Number Is Changing

Your additional-user rate has been **$18/user** since your account was set up in 2019. That rate reflected two factors at the time: a below-book rate locked in at signing, and a multi-organization pricing program that applied cross-entity benefits across your account group. Both are being retired as part of this refresh. Each entity moves to standard individual pricing, and all accounts are moving to the same graduated user rate structure.

Your platform base is also standardizing from $725 to the current Catalog Essentials rate of $749 — this reflects the move from legacy module pricing to the current bundled tier structure.

At the same time, the included user base for this tier is moving from 25 to 10. With your team averaging around 86 users, 76 users are now above the included base and billed at the graduated rate.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

With 76 excess users: 10 × $25 = $250; 15 × $22 = $330; 25 × $20 = $500; 26 × $18 = $468 — total = **$1,548/month**.

Here's how that plays out on your invoice:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $725 | $749 (Catalog Essentials) |
| Included users | 25 | **10** |
| Additional billable users | Legacy billing at $18/user = **$1,260/month** | 76 users at graduated rate = **$1,548/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$1,985** | **$2,297** |

*User billing is based on enabled accounts in your SuperCat environment — the user figures above reflect your current enabled count.*
