## Why the Number Is Changing

Your account was set up with **25 included users** in 2024 — more than the 10 users standard for the Catalog Essentials tier. That expanded allotment was part of your original arrangement. As part of this refresh, the included base is moving to the current standard.

Your current $820/month also includes a $95/month image upgrade that was billed as a separate add-on. Under the new tier structure, that capability is built into the Catalog Essentials rate — it's no longer a separate line item.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|

| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

With your team averaging around 19 users, 9 users are above the new 10-user included base, billed at the graduated rate: 9 × $25 = **$225/month**.
