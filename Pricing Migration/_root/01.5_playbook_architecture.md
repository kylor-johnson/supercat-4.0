## §0. Timing model: billing-cycle-anchored monthly cohorts

### §0.1. Governing mechanic

The migration operates on **monthly cohorts anchored to billing deadlines**, not abstract week counts. The governing mechanic: arrears billing + 60-day notice = **90-day gap** between notice and P&L realization. Each month's 1st is a binary gate.

```
Notice before July 1  →  new pricing Sept 1  →  first new-rate invoice Oct 1
Notice before Aug 1   →  new pricing Oct 1   →  first new-rate invoice Nov 1
```

Operator-stamped Source-fix Session E 2026-05-27. The mechanic is not negotiable — each missed billing-cycle boundary slips P&L realization by one full month for the affected account. Cohort-assignment rationale lives at `_root/02 §1` lines 13–28 (cohort column) and `_root/02 §6` (cohort assignment).

### §0.6. Why entity conversations ARE the pilot

Entity conversations are the highest-fidelity validation of the migration's operational mechanics and customer reception. They test:

- Packet delivery + receipt confirmation mechanics
- Multi-brand consolidation framing
- Customer reaction to pricing changes at meaningful delta
- CEO + Kylor handoff dynamics
- Objection script coverage against real customer responses

Feedback from these conversations directly refines the Executive and Pre-Engagement artifacts produced for the July cohort. No separate formal pilot session is convened.

### §1.3. Notice vs. Artifact vs. Meeting — capacity-realistic comm architecture

Operator-stamped Source-fix Session E 2026-05-27. The key reframe: communication architecture is **not uniform across segments** — it scales to delta + cohort capacity. The notice triggers the legal 60-day clock; companion materials + meetings carry the substance.

| Segment | Communication Architecture |
|---|---|
| **Tailwind** | Good News Notice IS the communication. No meeting, no artifact, no call. Deferred — each month of delay preserves higher legacy revenue. |
| **Core** | Standard Notice + simplified value summary. Kylor-owned, digital-first. Follow-up at Day 7 (Healthy) or Day 10 (Thriving) if non-response. June cohort. |
| **Narrative** | Simplified value summary + Value Notice. Kylor-owned, digital-first. June cohort. |
| **Executive** | Full bespoke artifact + CEO co-authored exec letter + Value Notice. No default meeting. July cohort. |
| **Pre-Engagement** | Full bespoke artifact + CEO-initiated call before notice. CEO leads. July cohort. |
| **Entity** (all children) | Entity packet IS the notice. Packet sent first, meeting proposed after. June cohort — serves as high-fidelity pilot. |
| **Strategic** | CEO-led conversation precedes notice. Addressed post-migration. |
| **Annual** | Same architecture as natural segment. Renewal-date driven. Addressed post-migration. |

**The key reframe**: For Core + Narrative standalone accounts (28 accts), the digital artifact IS the personal outreach. For Entity accounts (~27 children, ~17 packets), the entity packet IS the notice — packet first, meeting proposed after. For Executive + Pre-Engagement (16 accts), the CEO is directly involved in the communication.

**Meetings are proactively scheduled for:**

- Entity parents (meeting proposed after packet delivery, June)
- Pre-Engagement accounts (CEO-initiated call, July — call precedes notice)
- Inbound requests from any account

## §2. Tailwind playbook

### §2.1. Segment definition
By reference to `_root/02 §1` line 21 (6 accounts, Δ ≤ $0, avg −$136/mo, median −$96/mo, Kylor-owned, deferred).

### §2.2. Owner
**Kylor.** No escalation path needed under normal operation — price is decreasing.

### §2.3. Communication sequence
Single touchpoint: Good News Notice sent when cohort capacity allows (post-June / post-July priority). Receipt confirmation captured. Billing migrated at next cycle. Closed.

### §2.4. Notice template
**Good News Notice.** See `_root/03.5` Good News Notice row for substance, length budget, and delivery semantics.

### §2.5. Value artifact / companion
**None.** Good News Notice IS the communication. No upsell, no expansion ask, no relationship preamble (per `_root/03.5` Good News Notice "What It Does NOT Contain" column).

### §2.6. Objection scripts — STARTER (operator-pending refinement at Phase 4 template authoring)
Rare (price is decreasing) but possible:

- **"What's the catch?"** → Open: "There isn't one. Your modular utilization is decreasing and our pricing reflects that."
- **"Are services being reduced?"** → Open: "No. Everything you have today stays. The change is mechanical, tied to your usage profile."
- **"Should I expect future increases?"** → Open: "Not from this migration. Future changes follow our standard notice cadence."

### §2.7. Concession menu
By reference to `_root/04.5 Tailwind row`. **None** in normal posture — concessions not relevant to a price-decrease account.

### §2.8. Escalation triggers
Escalate to CEO only if:

- Account requests a service-level change tied to the price decrease
- Account requests removal from migration (treat as Strategic-routing question per `_root/02 §4` health-override rule)

### §2.9. CRM stage checklist — STARTER (operator-pending refinement at Phase 4 template authoring)
1. Notice sent (Day 0; Kylor)
2. Receipt confirmed (Day 0–7; Kylor)
3. Billing migrated at effective date (next billing cycle; Ops)
4. Complete (post-effective-date confirmation; Kylor)

Expected total duration: 60 days, near-zero effort per account.

### §2.10. Exit criteria
- Notice delivered + receipt confirmed
- Billing migrated to new (lower) rate at effective date
- No open service-level requests

## §9. Annual playbook

### §9.1. Segment definition
By reference to `_root/02 §1` line 28 (11 accounts, `deal_type = 'Annual'` overlay rule per `_root/02 §5`, Kylor + CEO, renewal-driven timing).

**Key principle**: Annual is a **segment label, not a separate operational motion.** Each annual account uses the playbook (Core / Narrative / Executive / Strategic) that matches its natural segment characteristics. The Annual playbook layer adds renewal-date-driven timing on top of the natural segment.

### §9.2. Owner
**Kylor (notice execution) + CEO (entity annuals).** Entity annuals (e.g., Coleto Progress, Jonathan Charles annual brand) coordinated with the corresponding entity packet and parent conversation.

### §9.3. Communication sequence
1. Renewal date audit (operator-flagged in Source-fix Session E as a readiness-sprint task; 24-hour ask for Jonathan Charles specifically — surfaced for operator-stamped scheduling clarification at Phase 4 or earlier).
2. For each annual account: determine natural segment per `_root/02 §1` routing logic.
3. Notice sent no later than renewal date − 90 days (the ≥90-day notice window).
4. If a renewal is imminent (<90 days from today): fast-track notice for that specific account; does not change the broader sequencing posture.
5. Notice substance: uses the variant (Standard / Value / Strategic) that matches the natural segment.
6. Effective date = next renewal date (NOT tied to monthly migration timing per `_root/02 §5`).

### §9.4. Notice template
**Annual Renewal Notice.** See `_root/03.5` Annual Renewal Notice row. Effective at next renewal date; renewal-specific framing; substance matches natural segment.

### §9.5. Value artifact / companion
Inherits natural segment's companion (Simplified value summary for Core/Narrative; Full value artifact + CEO exec letter for Executive; CEO call for Pre-Engagement; none for Tailwind; none pre-conversation for Strategic).

### §9.6. Objection scripts — STARTER (operator-pending refinement at Phase 4 template authoring)
Inherits natural segment's objection scripts, with one Annual-specific addition:

- **"When does this take effect?"** → "At your next renewal on [DATE], which is the standard cadence for your annual agreement."
- **"Can we lock in current pricing for another year?"** → Honest framing: legacy pricing is being retired across the program; renewal lock-in at legacy rate is not pre-authorized. Standard concession menu per natural segment applies.

### §9.7. Concession menu
Inherits natural segment's concession menu (Core / Narrative / Executive / Strategic). No Annual-specific concession overlay defined for Phase 1; if operator stamps an annual-specific concession at `_root/04.5`, this section is updated.

### §9.8. Escalation triggers
Inherits natural segment's escalation triggers. Annual-specific escalation:

- Account requests pricing lock at legacy rate for the upcoming renewal → CEO (treat as Strategic-routing escalation).
- Renewal date <90 days and notice has not been sent → CEO (fast-track posture; surfaced immediately).

### §9.9. CRM stage checklist — STARTER (operator-pending refinement at Phase 4 template authoring)
1. Renewal date audit complete (readiness sprint; Kylor + CEO; per §9.3)
2. Natural segment determined for each annual account (Kylor)
3. Notice scheduled (renewal date − 90 days; Kylor)
4. Notice sent (per scheduled date; Kylor)
5. Receipt confirmed (Day 0–3; Kylor)
6. Inbound handled per natural segment (Days 0–90; Kylor + CEO as escalation)
7. Billing migrated at renewal date (Ops; account-specific)
8. Complete (post-renewal confirmation; Kylor)

Expected total duration: ≥90 days notice + handling window per account.

### §9.10. Exit criteria
- Renewal date audit complete and natural segment confirmed
- Notice delivered ≥90 days before renewal (or fast-track-justified if <90 days)
- All inbound questions resolved per natural segment escalation rules
- Billing migrated to target tier rate at renewal date
