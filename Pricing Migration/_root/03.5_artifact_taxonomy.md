## §0. The legal-clock-vs-substance-carrier split rule

**Operator-stamped Source-fix Session E 2026-05-27 (handoff §2.5 + §2.6 verbatim):**

> **The notice triggers the legal clock; the supporting materials carry the substance.**

This is the foundational pairing rule for every artifact decision in Stage 5. Every per-segment communication architecture decomposes into:

- **Notice** — the formal written document satisfying the 60-day contractual requirement. Distinct from — and delivered alongside (or after) — the companion material. The notice is the legal-clock vehicle.
- **Companion material** — the substance carrier. Value artifact (bespoke or parameterized), exec letter, or CEO call. Where the bulk of the customer-facing value-and-context substance lives.
- **Meeting** — the conversational substance carrier. Used where capacity supports it (Entity / Pre-Engagement / Strategic / inbound from any segment).

For some segments (Tailwind, Strategic) the notice and the carrier collapse into one another:

- **Tailwind**: Good News Notice IS the entire communication. No companion. No meeting. The price decrease is short enough to fit in the notice itself.
- **Strategic**: The CEO conversation precedes the notice. The conversation IS the substance carrier. The post-conversation Strategic Migration Notice confirms agreed terms in compact form.
- **Entity**: The Entity Migration Packet IS the notice. The packet itself carries entity-level impact framing and per-brand pricing detail. Satisfies the 60-day notice for all child brands simultaneously.

For all other segments (Core / Narrative / Executive / Pre-Engagement / Annual), the notice is the legal vehicle; a companion material carries the substance.

This rule resolves operator concern §2.3 item 1 ("Objective of the notice / balancing substance contained in the notice vs attached artifact"). It is the canonical input every Phase 3a / Phase 3c / Phase 4 prose rewrite respects.

## §3. Delivery semantics — the notice-companion-meeting pairings

Per handoff §2.5 + Source-fix Session E "Notice vs. Artifact vs. Meeting" capacity-realistic resolution (mirrored at `_root/01.5 §1.3`):

| Segment | Notice | Companion | Meeting / Call | Delivery sequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tailwind** | Good News Notice | None | None | Single send (deferred timing) |
| **Core** | Standard Migration Notice | Simplified value summary (attached) | Inbound only | Notice + companion sent together; Day 7/10 follow-up if non-response |
| **Narrative** | Value Migration Notice | Simplified value summary (attached) | Inbound only | Notice + companion sent together |
| **Executive** | Value Migration Notice | Full value artifact + CEO exec letter (delivered alongside) | No default meeting; CEO exec letter signals direct ownership | Notice + Full artifact + CEO letter all delivered together (Kylor + CEO co-authored send) |
| **Pre-Engagement** | Value Migration Notice (sent AFTER CEO call) | Full value artifact (delivered with notice post-call) | **CEO-initiated call precedes notice** | (1) CEO call → (2) Notice + Full artifact sent post-call |
| **Entity** | Entity Migration Packet (IS the notice) | None separate (packet carries substance) | Meeting proposed AFTER packet delivery | (1) Packet delivered → (2) Meeting proposed to entity parent |
| **Strategic** | Strategic Migration Notice (sent AFTER CEO conversation) | None pre-conversation | **CEO-led conversation precedes notice** | (1) CEO conversation → (2) Notice confirms agreed terms |
| **Annual** | Annual Renewal Notice (substance matches natural segment) | Inherits natural segment's companion | Inherits natural segment | Sent ≥90 days before renewal date |

**Delivery-sequence invariants** (operator-stamped Source-fix Session E + handoff §2.5 verbatim):

1. **Companion delivery semantics are operator-stamped verbatim** — "Delivered with or ahead of notice" (Full value artifact); "Attached to notice" (Simplified value summary); "Delivered alongside notice" (CEO exec letter); "CEO initiates before any notice is sent" (CEO-initiated call). Any future deviation requires `_root/CONTRACTS.md §3` rule-change protocol.
2. **Pre-Engagement notice sequencing**: notice is sent ONLY AFTER CEO call completes — this is not an "ideally before" but a hard sequencing rule per `_root/01.5 §6.3` step 4.
3. **Strategic notice sequencing**: notice is sent ONLY AFTER CEO conversation — same hard sequencing rule per `_root/01.5 §8.3` step 6.
4. **Entity packet sequencing**: packet delivered FIRST; meeting proposed AFTER (not the reverse) per `_root/01.5 §7.3` step 3.
5. **Tailwind no-meeting**: deliberate per playbook framing rule; price-decrease accounts do not get proactive outreach.
