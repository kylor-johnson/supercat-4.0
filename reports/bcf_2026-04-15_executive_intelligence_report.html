What the Gap Means

Braxton Culler ranks top quartile on engagement — reps and buyers who use the platform engage deeply and derive high session value. The gap shows up in order output: at 755 orders in Q4 2025 vs a peer median of 852, the platform is leaving roughly 97 orders per quarter unrealized relative to comparable peers. The data points to catalog completeness (82% vs 95%) as the upstream constraint: incomplete product data suppresses search results, configurator completeness, and buyer confidence to order — directly reducing conversion on both the iPad and online channels.

The 13-point catalog completeness gap is the single highest-leverage platform health action available. Moving to peer median on completeness is the most direct path to improving order velocity without changing sales capacity or buyer acquisition.

Report Scope
This report covers eCat platform activity for Braxton Culler (account: bcf) for the trailing 12-month period ending April 15, 2026. All order counts and GMV figures reflect platform-recorded activity only. Report generated April 15, 2026.
eCat Order Sources — Definition
Two order channels are tracked in this report:
  • iPad Orders: Orders submitted by sales reps using the eCat iPad application. These are rep-mediated and attributed to individual rep accounts.
  • eCat Online Orders: Orders submitted directly by buyers through the eCat Online B2B platform. Rep attribution is not applicable; orders are attributed to buyer accounts directly.
GMV figures reflect order totals at time of submission and do not account for post-submission adjustments, cancellations, or shipping charges unless included in the order total.
Order History (Business System) — Partial Sync Disclosure
A separate order history sourced from Braxton Culler's business system is available in the platform and covers 1,631 orders ($2.19M) in the LTM period. This figure reflects a partial import and does not represent total business volume. Because eCat platform GMV ($6.57M) is substantially higher than the imported order history, direct comparisons between these two figures are not made in this report. The imported history provides rep assignment and customer attribution context but is not used as the primary commerce reference.
Customer Count Definition
“Ever ordered via eCat” counts reflect unique customer account codes (customer_num) present on at least one submitted order. Three reps (Barbara Harper, Jaime Hernandez, Jane Harrington) have submitted a combined 378 LTM orders with incomplete customer code attribution — meaning some customers they serve may not be counted in eCat order-customer figures. The true unique customer reach of the platform may be modestly higher than reported.
Inventory OOS — Methodology Note
Out-of-stock items are identified as inventory records where qty_available ≤ 0. Recorded restock dates reflect what was entered in the last inventory file upload; all visible dates are from 2025 and have passed. This report does not confirm whether those items have since been restocked — it flags the data state as requiring verification against current warehouse records.
Peer Benchmarking — Methodology
Peer benchmarking uses the most recent benchmark run (April 14, 2026) based on platform operational data across comparable accounts. The comparison group consists of platforms in the furniture and home furnishings vertical with a similar full-platform configuration. Benchmark data reflects platform metrics only — not total business revenue, market share, or off-platform activity. Percentile rankings use a 0–1 scale internally; this report expresses them as ordinal percentages (e.g., 83rd percentile) and quartile designations (Q1–Q4). No individual peer account data is disclosed.
Product Revenue — Methodology Note
Top products are ranked by all-time invoiced GMV from the order history data (sales_data), which reflects historical invoice records imported from the business system. This covers a longer period than the trailing 12-month LTM window used for order counts and is provided for catalog context. LTM product-level GMV by eCat order is not separately reported in this edition.
Geographic Data Note
Geographic distribution is based on billing state as recorded on iPad order submissions. A minor data quality issue exists for Maryland orders: two state codes (“MD” and “MD.”) appear in the dataset, reflecting an inconsistent entry in order records. Combined Maryland GMV is approximately $217K LTM. This does not affect any other reported figures.
