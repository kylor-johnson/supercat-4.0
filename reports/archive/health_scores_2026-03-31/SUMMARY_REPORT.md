## How Health Scores Are Calculated

Health Intelligence v3 uses a **4-layer weighted composite model**:

### Layer Weights
1. **Engagement Health** (25%) — How actively users engage with the platform
2. **Value Realization** (40%) — How much business value the client derives
3. **Trend Momentum** (20%) — Direction of key metrics over time
4. **Risk Signals** (15%) — Early warning indicators of churn risk

### Health Bands
- **Excellent** (0.80+): Thriving clients with high engagement and value realization
- **Good** (0.60-0.79): Healthy clients with solid usage patterns
- **Fair** (0.40-0.59): Moderate health, may need attention
- **At Risk** (0.30-0.39): Low health, intervention recommended
- **Critical** (<0.30): Severe health issues, immediate action required

### Key Flags
- **Churn Risk:** Paying customers with zero/low engagement OR score < 0.30
- **Expansion Ready:** High-performing clients ready for upsell opportunities
- **Healthy Complete:** Excellent health with no expansion gaps
