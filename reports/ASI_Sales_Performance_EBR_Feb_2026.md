## Opportunities to Sell Better

### 1. Scale Leo's Workflow
Train other reps on:
- Creating PDF catalogs for customer presentations
- Using "Show Sales in Catalog" before meetings
- Checking Customer Favorites and Backorders

### 2. Increase SmartPicks Adoption
Only 12 of 45 users view SmartPicks. These curated collections accelerate product discovery.

### 3. Leverage Customer History
View Cust. Favorites (132 uses) and View Cust. Backorders (103 uses) are underutilized. Knowing what customers bought — and what's delayed — builds trust.

### 4. Explore Presentation Tools
- My Lists: Create curated collections for repeat presentations
- Share My List: Send selections to buyers for review
- Email Item Info: Quick product shares
