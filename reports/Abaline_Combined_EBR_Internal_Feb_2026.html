3. Challenges & Solutions

Challenge 1: MPC Lacks Sales Portal Visibility

Current State: MPC generates $13.59M in annual order revenue (5,177 orders) with 13 active reps but has no Sales Portal subscription. Managers have no territory-level analytics or order visibility outside iPad.

Root Cause: Sales Portal was not part of original MPC deployment. Ordering workflow works without it, so no pain trigger.

Business Impact: Limited visibility into rep performance and territory trends. Managers cannot proactively identify underperforming territories.

Proposed Solution

Enable Sales Portal for MPC at $395/mo. Configure territory views to match MPC's structure. Train jhunter (admin contact) on Portal dashboards.

Expected Outcome: "With Sales Portal, MPC managers gain real-time visibility into $13.59M in annual orders. Identify which of your 13 reps are over/underperforming and spot territory opportunities."

Challenge 2: SmartPicks/Customer Intelligence Not Used at MPC

Current State: ASI has 162 SmartPicks views, 81 favorites views, 109 backorder views. MPC has 0 across all customer intelligence features.

Root Cause: MPC reps are product-search focused (73,300 searches) vs. customer-relationship focused. Training/awareness gap.

Business Impact: Missed cross-sell/upsell opportunities. Reps manually identifying products vs. algorithmic recommendations.

Proposed Solution

Schedule SmartPicks training for top 5 MPC reps (mfeit, sTischler, dfutterman, jcaronia, jRosenblum). Configure SmartPicks algorithm for MPC's product catalog.

Expected Outcome: "SmartPicks can surface relevant products based on customer purchase history. For a team doing $13.59M/year, even 5% AOV uplift from better targeting = $679K incremental revenue."

Challenge 3: User Concentration Risk at ASI

Current State: Leo Elias operates 3 devices (leoelias, leoelias3, le99) submitting ~6,238 orders—approximately 70% of ASI's total order volume.

Root Cause: Leo is the most effective/efficient rep. Other reps may lack training or confidence.

Business Impact: Single point of failure. Limited scalability without adding Leo-equivalent capacity.

Proposed Solution

Identify next-tier reps (mikeaba, delias, almo) for advanced training. Document Leo's workflow and best practices. Consider territory rebalancing.

Expected Outcome: "Developing 2-3 additional power users reduces single-point-of-failure risk and increases capacity for growth without proportional headcount increase."
