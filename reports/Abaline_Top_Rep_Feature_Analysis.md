## Recommendations

### For ASI

1. **Document Leo's Workflow** — Create training materials based on his customer-first, intelligence-driven approach
2. **Coach Lower Performers** — Focus on customer search and order review habits
3. **Filter Training** — Only D Elias uses filters meaningfully; train others on filter efficiency

### For MPC

1. **SmartPicks Training** — Zero usage despite ASI's 162 views; immediate opportunity
2. **Customer Intelligence Features** — Train on favorites, backorders, sales data
3. **Identify Top Rep Workflows** — Get LTM user-level report to analyze mfeit/sTischler patterns
4. **Leverage Email Strength** — MPC already strong on email item info (318 vs ASI's 24)

### Combined

1. **Cross-Entity Best Practice Sharing** — ASI's customer intelligence usage + MPC's email engagement
2. **Feature Adoption Benchmarking** — Track feature usage by rep quarterly
3. **Power User Recognition** — Leo Elias, mfeit, jcaronia as potential champions
