## 3. Challenges & Solutions

### Challenge 1: MPC Lacks Sales Portal Visibility

**Current State:**
- MPC generates **$13.59M in annual order revenue** (5,177 orders) with 13 active reps
- No Sales Portal subscription ($0/mo vs. ASI's $395/mo)
- Managers have no territory-level analytics or order visibility outside iPad

**Root Cause:**
- Sales Portal was not part of original MPC deployment
- Ordering workflow works without it, so no pain trigger

**Business Impact:**
- Limited visibility into rep performance and territory trends
- Managers cannot proactively identify underperforming territories
- No web-based order review for office staff

**Proposed Solution:**
- Enable Sales Portal for MPC at $395/mo
- Configure territory views to match MPC's structure
- Train jhunter (admin contact) on Portal dashboards

**Expected Outcome:**
> "With Sales Portal, MPC managers gain real-time visibility into $13.59M in annual orders. Identify which of your 13 reps are over/underperforming and spot territory opportunities."

---

### Challenge 2: SmartPicks/Customer Intelligence Not Used at MPC

**Current State:**
- ASI: 162 SmartPicks views, 81 favorites views, 109 backorder views
- MPC: 0 across all customer intelligence features

**Root Cause:**
- MPC reps are product-search focused (73,300 searches) vs. customer-relationship focused
- Training/awareness gap on customer intelligence features

**Business Impact:**
- Missed cross-sell/upsell opportunities
- Reps manually identifying products vs. algorithmic recommendations
- Customer favorites and backorder data not leveraged

**Proposed Solution:**
- Schedule SmartPicks training for top 5 MPC reps (mfeit, sTischler, dfutterman, jcaronia, jRosenblum)
- Configure SmartPicks algorithm for MPC's product catalog
- Create "Customer Intelligence Quick Start" guide

**Expected Outcome:**
> "SmartPicks can surface relevant products based on customer purchase history. For a team doing $13.59M/year, even 5% AOV uplift from better targeting = $679K incremental revenue."

---

### Challenge 3: User Concentration Risk at ASI

**Current State:**
- Leo Elias operates 3 devices (leoelias, leoelias3, le99) submitting ~6,238 orders
- This represents ~70% of ASI's total order volume
- Single point of failure if Leo is unavailable

**Root Cause:**
- Leo is likely the most effective/efficient rep
- Other reps may lack training or confidence
- Business model may funnel orders through central figure

**Business Impact:**
- Business continuity risk
- Limited scalability without adding Leo-equivalent capacity
- Potential bottleneck during peak periods

**Proposed Solution:**
- Identify next-tier reps (mikeaba, delias, almo) for advanced training
- Document Leo's workflow and best practices
- Consider territory rebalancing to distribute load

**Expected Outcome:**
> "Developing 2-3 additional power users reduces single-point-of-failure risk and increases capacity for growth without proportional headcount increase."
