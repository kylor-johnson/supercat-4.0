Executive Summary

Kalco Lighting routes $2.75M — 23% of its $11.9M total business — through eCat at a $13K average order value driven by high-stakes project workflows, but only 87 of 404 ever-registered buyers placed an order last year, making dormant account reactivation and rep-led acceleration the two highest-leverage near-term opportunities.

  • Project-scale commerce defines the eCat channel. eCat captures 23% of total GMV ($2.75M of $11.9M) across just 211 orders — a $13K average that reflects how reps are using eCat for high-value work: Quote orders average $34K, Hold-for-Confirmation orders average $17K. The platform is being used exactly as intended for large-project business. See Commerce Analytics →
  • 317 dormant eCat buyers represent the largest untapped pipeline. Of 404 customers with documented eCat history, only 87 placed an order in the trailing 12 months — LBX Lighting Inc. (7 prior orders, $91K total, silent since October 2025) and Light & Day LLC (9 orders, $50K total, also silent since October 2025) are the most immediately addressable. See Customer & Buyer →
  • Rep momentum is accelerating but heavily concentrated at the top. Claudia Carrillo (44 LTM orders, $664K) and Bob Ross (4 orders, $595K at a $149K AOV on large projects) account for 47% of team eCat GMV — Robert Parker's 1,100% surge in the last 90 days signals the next growth driver, while DBA Associates dropped 86% in the same window.
