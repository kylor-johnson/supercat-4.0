### 8.2 Definitions
- **Route-to-market archetype**: derived from `Has Dealer Network`, `Has Trade Program`, `Has eCommerce`.
- **Trade show tiers**: 0 / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6+ (based on `Trade Show Count`).
- **SKU tiers**: <1k / 1k–5k / 5k–25k / 25k+ (based on `SKU Estimate`).
- **Category tiers**: 1–5 / 6–10 / 11–20 / 21+ (based on `Category Count`).
- **CMS normalization**: collapses variants like themes/modules into stable families (e.g., Shopify; WordPress + WooCommerce).
- **Observed vendor**: `ERP Tech` / `PIM System` / `DAM System` treated as observed **only if** the text does **not** begin with "none detected / not detected / no public ...".

### 8.3 Output artifacts
All tables are exported as CSVs in `reports/state_of_industry/` with today’s date in the filename.
