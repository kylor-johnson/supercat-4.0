Executive Summary

April 2025 – March 2026 (Trailing 12 Months)

  1. eCat is a high-AOV selling tool for Craftmade's rep team. 245 iPad-submitted eCat orders generated $973K in eCat GMV in the trailing 12 months, at a team-average AOV of $3,972 — a strong per-order figure anchored by Shayna Petty ($25,132 AOV) and Andrew Rivera ($17,492 AOV). The platform is not yet a volume channel, but it is clearly a high-value transaction tool. See Commerce Analytics →
  2. A January 2026 spike accounts for 59% of LTM eCat GMV. 145 of 245 LTM eCat orders ($489K of $973K) occurred in January 2026. This concentration — likely tied to a trade show, market event, or seasonal drive — means the underlying monthly run rate outside that month is approximately 8–10 orders per month. Understanding what drove January is essential before setting eCat expectations. See Commerce Analytics →
  3. 153 unique buyers are active on eCat, against 5,155 ERP customers. eCat retention among buyers who have ever ordered is 44.5% (153 active of 344 ever ordered). The vast majority of ERP accounts — 4,811 — have never placed an eCat order, representing platform headroom. Reps are the primary acquisition engine: 24 first-time eCat buyers were activated in January 2026 alone. See Customer & Buyer Intelligence →
  4. The portal is healthy and actively used. The eCat Online Closed Catalog (Clicky-tracked) averaged 113–131 unique daily visitors in Q1 2026, with an average session exceeding 7 minutes. Texas, North Carolina, Georgia, and Arkansas are the top-traffic states. Direct traffic accounts for 63% of visits — a strong signal of dealer awareness and routine use. See Portal Engagement →
  5. The product catalog is strong; options and pricing data are stale. 2,907 visible products score 93.4% completeness (image + price) — above the platform median of ~74%. However, options, option groups, and matrix options data have not been updated in 229 days (Stale). For a product like ceiling fans where finish and blade options are central, this is a usability risk. See Platform & Feature Utilization →
  6. Buyer registration is active but onboarding velocity needs attention. 1,457 accepted applicants are enrolled, with 134 accepted in the trailing 12 months. Only 5.2% of LTM-accepted applicants have placed a first eCat order, with an average of 227 days to conversion. No conversions occurred within the first 60 days. Accepted enrollees need a clearer activation path after acceptance. See Customer & Buyer Intelligence →
