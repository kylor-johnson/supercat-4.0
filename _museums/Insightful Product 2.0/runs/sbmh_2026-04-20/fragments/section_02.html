2.2 Behavioral Scorecard Spotlight

Platform engagement data from the trailing 12 months reveals how reps move through the selling workflow — from customer targeting, through product discovery and configuration, to presentation and order submission. Comparing these stages exposes where deals lose momentum before reaching checkout.

Widespread Configuration Gap

Across the top producers, configuration-tool usage runs well below 5% of product-browsing activity. The organization-wide ratio of configuration events to product-discovery events sits far below the benchmark threshold, indicating that reps browse products extensively but rarely bundle, customize, or configure items before moving to checkout. This pattern suppresses average order values and limits cross-sell opportunities.

Presentation Workflow Under-Used

Several reps with strong product-discovery activity rarely transition into the presentation workflow. Fletcher Flynn, for example, shows a presentation-to-configuration ratio of just 0.04 over the trailing 12 months — well below the cohort norm — suggesting configured selections are not being shared with buyers as curated presentations before order submission.

Reps who do engage the full funnel — discovery through configuration through presentation — tend to carry higher average order values. Eleanor Ward and Jennifer Whipple both exceed the $3,589 organization median, and both show meaningfully higher configuration and presentation engagement relative to their order volumes.

What this means: The largest revenue-improvement lever across the sales team is not more prospecting or more orders — it is deeper engagement with the configuration and presentation tools that already exist. Closing the configuration gap among the top five volume producers alone could materially lift average order values without requiring any additional customer acquisition.

2.4 Coaching Opportunities

The following coaching cards identify the highest-impact behavioral shifts based on trailing-12-month platform engagement data and order outcomes. Projected upside figures represent estimates assuming average order values move toward the organization median of $3,589.

Edward Gandy Configuration Gap

Edward leads the team with 541 orders over the trailing 12 months, yet his configuration-to-discovery ratio is 0.042 — meaning fewer than 1 in 20 product-browsing sessions include a configuration step. His current AOV of $3,118 trails the $3,589 organization median by $471 per order.

Introduce short configurator walkthroughs during weekly check-ins; pair with Eleanor Ward for a joint-call session showcasing presentation-led selling.
If AOV rose to the organization median across his trailing order volume, the potential annual upside could reach approximately $255K.
Beth Burmeister Configuration Gap

Beth placed 179 orders across 82 unique customers in the trailing 12 months with strong product-discovery engagement (2,578 events), but her configuration ratio of 0.019 is the lowest among the top-five producers. Her AOV of $2,509 sits $1,080 below the organization median.

Focus on two or three high-frequency product categories where bundling is most natural; provide category-specific configuration templates to reduce friction.
Closing even half the AOV gap across her order volume represents a potential annual upside of up to $193K.
Karen Kepley Configuration Refinement

Karen is the team’s broadest-reach seller with 461 orders spanning 162 unique customers over the trailing 12 months. Her configuration ratio of 0.061 is above Edward’s and Beth’s but still well below the target threshold. Her AOV of $3,541 is just $48 below the org median — a small gap, but one that compounds across her high volume.

Leverage Karen’s existing presentation strength (1,246 events — highest on the team) by connecting presentation workflows to configuration steps so configured items flow directly into buyer-facing presentations.
Even modest AOV improvement at her volume could yield an estimated $92K in potential annual upside.
Fletcher Flynn Dual Gap: Configuration + Presentation

Fletcher placed 94 orders over the trailing 12 months but shows two workflow gaps: a configuration-to-discovery ratio of 0.056 and a presentation-to-configuration ratio of just 0.042 (only 1 presentation event in the period). His AOV of $2,922 is $667 below the organization median.

Start with the presentation workflow — even a small increase from near-zero usage could improve buyer engagement. Then layer in configuration training once the presentation habit is established.
Addressing both gaps could unlock an estimated $63K in potential annual upside from AOV improvement alone.
