At just 1.1% of total eCat orders over the trailing 12 months, the quote workflow is largely unused — yet it carries the highest average value among standard order types at $7,088, more than 2.2× the $3,235 confirmed-order average. The 8 Hold for Confirmation orders at $12,040 average further suggest that multi-step, high-value transactions benefit from structured approval workflows.

What this means: The overwhelming majority of eCat volume runs through the confirmed-order pathway, which is efficient and appropriate for standard transactions. However, the 2.2× AOV premium on quotes signals that buyers engaging the quote workflow are purchasing at a fundamentally different price point. Expanding awareness and adoption of quoting — particularly among reps handling larger accounts — could lift the blended average order value without increasing order volume.

The remaining non-eCat volume likely represents smaller, simpler transactions that may be better suited to their current channels.
