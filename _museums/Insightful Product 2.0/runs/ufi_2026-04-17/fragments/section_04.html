Pricing Configuration Note

The 0% completeness score reflects Universal Furniture's pricing architecture, not missing data. All 2,201 visible products have images (100% image coverage), but pricing is managed through price levels rather than the standard net price field. The completeness formula requires both image and standard net price — since price levels are used instead, the standard field is intentionally blank. This is a configuration pattern, not a catalog gap.

What this tells you: Your catalog is in strong shape where it matters — every product has imagery, and pricing reaches buyers through your price-level configuration. The 0% completeness metric is an artifact of how pricing is structured and does not reflect a gap in what buyers see when browsing or ordering.
