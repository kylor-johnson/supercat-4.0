Retention Bottleneck

Of the 411 accounts that have ordered via eCat at some point, only 105 (25.5%) placed an order in the trailing 12 months. The remaining 306 lapsed buyers — nearly three times the active count — represent proven platform users who stopped ordering. Meanwhile, 1,105 accounts (72.9% of your full account base) have never placed an eCat order at all. Activity continues to narrow at 90 days: just 36 accounts ordered in the last quarter, down from 57 in the last 6 months.

What this means: eCat has reached 27% of the account base over its lifetime, but the platform struggles to keep those buyers active. Three out of four accounts that tried eCat have since lapsed. The 306 lapsed buyers are the highest-leverage recovery pool — they already know the platform and placed at least one order. Re-engaging even 10% of that group would add 30 accounts to the active base, a 29% increase over today’s 105. The 1,105 never-activated accounts represent a longer-term opportunity that depends on rep-driven onboarding.
