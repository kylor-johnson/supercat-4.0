January 2026 Dominates the Trailing Period

January 2026 produced 389 orders and $2.28M in GMV — accounting for 31.7% of all trailing-period orders and 62.2% of total GMV. The $5,853 AOV was nearly 2× the blended average. Outside January, monthly volume averaged 70 orders at roughly $1,653 AOV. June 2025 (156 orders, $699K) was the only other month to approach comparable scale, suggesting ordering clusters around market events.

What this means: eCat order volume is heavily event-driven. January 2026 alone generated more GMV ($2.28M) than the other 12 months combined ($1.38M). June 2025 (156 orders, $699K) and October 2025 (76 orders, $111K) also showed elevated activity. The base-case monthly run rate outside these peaks is approximately 65–70 orders at $900–$1,200 AOV — a steady but modest cadence. Understanding which events drive the January and June spikes allows for targeted pre-market catalog and inventory preparation.

Accommodation Accounts Dominate the Top Line

The top two buyers — Accommodation Acct GL/MC ($318K, 9.0%) and City Plumbing & Electric Supply ($191K, 5.4%) — together represent 14.4% of total eCat GMV on just 12 combined orders. Both are dormant (last ordered January 2026 or earlier). If these accounts do not reactivate, the top-5 concentration drops substantially and the trailing GMV base shrinks accordingly.

What this means: Revenue concentration is healthy at the aggregate level — the top 5 account for 22.7% of eCat GMV, below the 25% concentration-risk threshold. However, the top 3 buyers are all accommodation or institutional accounts with low order frequency and high per-order value, and all three are currently dormant. The most dependable volume comes from Manasquan Lighting (70 orders, $85,563) and Hi-Light Decorating (57 orders, $56,027), whose frequent ordering patterns make them more predictable revenue contributors despite lower individual GMV rankings.

HFC & Quote Workflows Drive 59% of GMV on 26% of Volume

Hold For Confirmation orders average $5,929 — 3.6× the confirmed-order AOV of $1,632. These 284 orders represent 23.2% of volume but carry 46.0% of total GMV ($1.68M of $3.66M). Quote orders at $15,794 avg are even higher but account for just 31 orders. Together, HFC and quote workflows generate $2.17M — 59.4% of all eCat GMV — on just 25.7% of order volume.

What this means: Visual Comfort – Studio /Fans’ order workflow reveals a two-tier pattern: routine confirmed orders form the volume backbone (74.3%), while HFC and quote workflows capture high-value, complex transactions at a significant AOV premium. The HFC workflow is particularly important — at 284 orders generating $1.68M, it is operationally significant and its $5,929 AOV suggests these are curated, approval-gated purchases. Ensuring HFC approval turnaround is fast directly protects nearly half of annual eCat GMV. The quote workflow, at $15,794 average, captures the most complex and highest-value transactions in the pipeline.
