What Top Performers Do
What Top Performers Do

Behavioral patterns observed among the highest-performing accounts in the comparison group:

  • Comprehensive import schedules. Top performers maintain consistent import cadence across all entity types — not just products and inventory but also options, price levels, and customer records — keeping the full catalog data set current.
  • Broad feature utilization in daily workflows. High-performing teams use camera scanning, PDF catalog generation, and Smart Stacks as standard parts of their rep workflow, rather than relying on product search alone.
  • Regular customer data refreshes. Leading accounts update customer records at least monthly, ensuring reps always have current buyer information during presentations and order entry.
  • Consistent data freshness across all entity types. Accounts with the strongest data freshness profiles refresh options, price levels, and configuration data on the same cadence as their product and inventory imports.
What this means: The gap between top performers and the rest of the group is not about features — it is about operational consistency. Keeping all data entities on a regular refresh cycle is the behavior most strongly associated with above-median performance across all benchmark dimensions.
