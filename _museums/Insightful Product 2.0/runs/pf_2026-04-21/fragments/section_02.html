Archetypes are coaching tools, not performance labels

Each archetype reflects a natural selling style and responds best to different coaching strategies. A Volume Relationship Seller like Novitch or Sgambellone needs AOV support (configuration training, bundling workflows) — not breadth training they’ve already mastered. A Curated Discovery Seller like DiLauro needs frequency incentives and cadence planning — not configuration coaching they’re already excelling at. Aligning coaching to selling style produces faster results than one-size-fits-all enablement.

What this tells you: Your team’s top performers divide into volume-driven and value-driven sellers. Both archetypes are effective — the Volume Relationship pattern drives breadth and frequency, while the Curated Discovery pattern drives depth and order value. Targeted coaching that plays to each style’s strength offers the clearest path to GMV growth.
