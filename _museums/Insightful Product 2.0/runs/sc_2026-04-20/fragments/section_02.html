Potential impact: Stabilizing Julie’s output at her trailing-12-month rate could help preserve an estimated $2–3M in annualized eCat volume.
Dierdre Essix Presentation Tool Gap

Dierdre ranks #3 by GMV ($7.2M trailing 12 months) with the highest order submission rate among profiled reps (1,892 submissions tracked in platform engagement data, all-time). However, she has zero recorded usage of presentation tools — no PDF catalogs, no emailed product information, no shared lists — despite heavy configuration and bundling activity (4,688 events all-time).

Suggested action: Introduce Dierdre to catalog sharing and email communication tools during a brief training session. Her existing workflow already includes deep product configuration — adding a presentation step before order submission could strengthen buyer relationships.
Potential impact: Presentation tools help buyers review and share selections internally, which could support larger or more frequent orders from her 289 unique accounts.
Charles Hindt Growth + AOV Opportunity

Charles is the fastest-accelerating seller on the team: orders surged 150.6% in the current 90-day window (156 → 391) with logins up 19.2%. His $2,786 average order value, however, is the lowest among high-volume reps — roughly 61% of the org average of $4,551 (trailing 12 months).

Suggested action: Pair Charles with a Precision Closer like Christine Atwell ($7,918 AOV) for a cross-coaching session focused on configuration, kit-building, and order bundling techniques. His volume surge suggests strong account engagement — adding bundling skills could lift per-order value.
Potential impact: At his current 90-day run rate of 391 orders, even a modest AOV increase could meaningfully boost his quarterly GMV contribution.
Alaena Schulz Engagement-Conversion Divergence

Alaena’s logins increased 45.6% in the current 90-day window (169 → 246), but her orders declined 22.2% (189 → 147). She’s spending more time on the platform while converting less — a pattern that can indicate friction in the product mix, territory dynamics, or workflow.

Suggested action: Review Alaena’s recent activity to identify where the workflow bottleneck is occurring. A brief conversation could surface whether the issue is inventory availability, account-level changes, or a workflow shift.
Potential impact: Alaena generated $3.6M in trailing-12-month GMV across 194 accounts. Reversing the conversion decline could restore her prior 90-day pace.
