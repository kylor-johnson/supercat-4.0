Activation Funnel

Of 91,667 total account records, 33,108 (36.1%) have placed at least one eCat order historically. However, only 5,816 of those activated accounts (17.6%) remain active in the trailing 12 months. Another 27,292 accounts ordered via eCat at some point but have not placed an order in the past year, forming a substantial re-engagement pool. An additional 58,559 accounts in your system have never placed an eCat order.

What this tells you: The 27,292 lapsed eCat accounts and 58,559 never-activated accounts represent your largest growth levers. Prioritizing win-back campaigns for lapsed buyers — who already know the platform — is typically the highest-conversion path to increased eCat adoption.
