Platform Engagement Efficiency Varies Widely Across the Team
Among qualifying reps with platform engagement data, the events-per-order ratio ranges from 64 (Rip Nance) to 184 (Joan Harrison) trailing 12 months. Nance converts platform activity to orders with minimal browsing — a targeted closer who joined the platform 132 days ago and has already generated $124,859 in iPad GMV. Harrison invests nearly 3× the engagement per order but maintains a strong $4,399 AOV. The contrast highlights two viable but very different selling workflows on the same platform.

Four reps (John Anhut, Deborah Klein, Charity Queen, Joan Harrison) each log more than 5,400 total platform events trailing 12 months, indicating deep product exploration and customer engagement activity. Meanwhile, reps with below-median events per order (Barnard at 99, Green at 70, Nance at 64) tend to convert browsing to orders more efficiently — a signal that discovery-to-order friction may be costing the team’s heavier browsers additional throughput.

What this tells you: Reps who invest heavily in platform discovery (Joan Harrison, Kurt Muller at 177 events/order) could benefit from workflow streamlining to convert more of that research into submitted orders. Conversely, the team’s most efficient converters (Rip Nance, Danielle Green) demonstrate that strong AOV does not require high event counts — their approach could inform coaching for engagement-heavy reps.

Archetypes Are Coaching Tools, Not Performance Labels
These classifications reflect how reps use the platform, not how well they perform. A Volume Relationship Seller like John Anhut doesn’t need breadth training — he already reaches 36 accounts. He needs AOV support (bundling, configuration). A Precision Closer like Clyde Barnard doesn’t need configuration coaching — his $7,918 AOV shows he already bundles effectively. Coaching should be adapted to selling style, not applied uniformly.
What this tells you: The four archetypes reveal that Sarreid’s rep team operates with distinct selling patterns, not a single approach. The Volume Relationship Sellers (4 reps) collectively manage 106 customer accounts trailing 12 months and represent the largest AOV uplift opportunity. The Deep-Account Specialists carry concentration risk that account expansion coaching could mitigate.
