Portal visitor volume has declined 30.4% over the trailing six months. However, engagement quality among active visitors remains strong — average session duration and bounce rate have held at favorable levels throughout the period.

What this tells you: Fewer visitors are arriving at the portal, but those who do are spending meaningful time browsing. The combination of declining volume with steady engagement depth suggests the drop may reflect fewer casual visits rather than disengagement from active buyers.
