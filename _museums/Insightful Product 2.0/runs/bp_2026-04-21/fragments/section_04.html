Buyers browsing these items see incomplete listings, which erodes conversion confidence and makes it difficult for reps to build accurate quotes. An additional 397 products (17.3%) are missing images, compounding the gap. What this tells you: Catalog completeness at 45.2% means more than half your visible products are missing at least one critical data element. The pricing gap is the larger problem — 1,226 products without price data cannot convert, regardless of whether they have images. Prioritize adding pricing to your highest-traffic and best-selling items first, then close the 397-item image gap. If bulk pricing data is available in your business system, a single feed update could resolve the majority of this gap in one pass.
