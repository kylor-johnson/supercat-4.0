Team-Wide Presentation Underutilization

17 of 20 top reps by iPad GMV show below-threshold presentation feature usage across their cumulative engagement history. Reps consistently configure product orders but rarely leverage presentation tools to showcase curated selections to customers before closing. This configuration-to-presentation drop-off is the most widespread behavioral gap on the team.

Discovery Efficiency Among Top Closers

The top 3 reps by iPad GMV (trailing 12 months) show notably low product exploration relative to their customer targeting activity — discovery-to-targeting ratios of 0.35, 0.27, and 0.42 respectively (all-time). These efficient closers focus on known products, which drives strong volume but may limit cross-sell and new product introduction opportunities.

What this tells you: The most actionable team-wide opportunity is presentation feature adoption. Reps who configure products but skip the presentation step miss the chance to engage customers visually before order submission — a behavior change that could improve close rates and average order values across the team.

Archetypes Are Coaching Tools, Not Performance Labels

Each selling style has distinct strengths that should be reinforced, not overridden. A Volume Relationship Seller benefits from AOV support, not breadth training they already excel at. A Precision Closer needs frequency incentives and product discovery nudges, not configuration coaching. Adapt enablement to each rep’s natural selling pattern for the highest impact.

What this tells you: Your team spans four distinct selling archetypes, each with a different growth lever. The biggest untapped opportunity is helping Volume Relationship Sellers lift their average order value — even a modest AOV increase across their high transaction counts could meaningfully grow total iPad GMV.
