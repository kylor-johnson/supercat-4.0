Quebec and Ontario together account for 41% of all portal visitors over the trailing 6 months — consistent with RENWIL's Canadian headquarters and primary market. The U.S. footprint is more dispersed, with Georgia, North Carolina, and California as the leading states.

What this tells you: Portal demand is concentrated in RENWIL's home market (Eastern Canada) with a meaningful and geographically diverse U.S. presence. The spread across 14 U.S. states suggests the portal is reaching buyers beyond a single sales territory.

All 253 portal visitors over the trailing 6 months arrived via direct navigation — typing the URL directly or using a bookmark. No traffic was attributed to search engines, referral links, or social channels.

Closed Portal Pattern

A 100% direct traffic profile is typical for a closed B2B catalog portal. Visitors already know the URL and navigate directly — this is expected behavior for a portal that is not indexed by search engines and is shared with buyers via direct outreach.

What this tells you: Portal traffic is entirely driven by direct awareness — buyers who already have the URL. There is no organic discovery channel feeding new visitors. If broadening portal reach is a goal, introducing the portal URL into email campaigns, sales collateral, or outbound rep communication would be the lever to pull.
