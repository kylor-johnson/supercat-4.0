Archetypes Are Coaching Tools, Not Performance Labels

Archetypes reflect selling style, not rep quality. A Volume Relationship Seller like Daniel Ratchford (410 orders, $836K GMV) benefits from AOV-growth coaching — upselling and cross-category strategies — not breadth training he already excels at. A Curated Discovery Seller like Whit Barnes ($17,493 AOV) needs frequency and conversion support, not product discovery coaching. Tailor enablement to each style rather than applying a single training playbook across the team.

What this tells you: Your team skews heavily toward Volume Relationship Sellers (18 of 35 classified reps), reflecting a culture built on repeat-buyer relationships. Your 8 Curated Discovery Sellers carry higher average order values — Whit Barnes at $17,493 and Madeline Cole at $5,085 — suggesting that discovery-led selling drives larger transactions. Coaching more reps toward curated presentation workflows could lift AOV across the team.
