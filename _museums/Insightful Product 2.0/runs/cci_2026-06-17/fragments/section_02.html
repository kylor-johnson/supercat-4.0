Archetypes Are Coaching Tools
These classifications describe natural selling patterns, not performance grades. Coaching should be adapted to each style: a Volume Relationship Seller needs AOV support (upsell and bundle tactics), not breadth training. A Direct Transaction Seller needs incentives to increase order frequency, not presentation coaching. The most effective development plans work with each rep's natural motion, not against it.
What this tells you: Your team uses the platform in fundamentally different ways. The Curated Discovery group invests heavily in product exploration and presentation — they need conversion support. The Volume Relationship group already has the customer breadth — they need AOV lift. Matching coaching to archetype is the fastest path to team-wide improvement.
