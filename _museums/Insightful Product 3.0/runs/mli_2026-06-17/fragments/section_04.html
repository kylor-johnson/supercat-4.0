Acquisition Concentrated in One Window

52 of 64 first-time eCat buyers (81%) placed their first order in January 2026, with the remaining months adding 1–2 each. Every new buyer arrived through a rep on iPad — eCat is currently a rep-onboarding tool, not a self-service acquisition channel.

Action: The January wave proves your reps can onboard buyers onto eCat at scale when there's a forcing event — the challenge is that acquisition flatlines to 1–2/month the rest of the year. Build a quarterly onboarding cadence so reps activate new eCat buyers continuously rather than in one annual burst. Account-level first-order trajectories would show which of these 52 January buyers are becoming repeat orderers — request customer-level first-order dates next cycle to track the cohort's growth arc.
Action: eCat ordering is real but episodic — $690K LTM concentrated in a January burst and a top-5 buyer set worth $307,432, with high-value Hold-for-Confirmation orders ($613K) carrying the volume. The highest-leverage move is converting the January cohort into year-round reorderers; sustaining even Feb–May's pace across all twelve months would meaningfully lift recurring eCat GMV. Connecting online self-service ordering would add a low-touch reorder channel and show where the digital ordering runway sits.
