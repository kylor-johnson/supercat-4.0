Investigative Context

Declining unit prices may reflect several dynamics: competitive pricing pressure driving buyers to request discounts, a natural shift toward lower-priced product tiers, changing buyer mix within an account, or seasonal promotional activity. These patterns warrant individual account review — they are signals to investigate, not conclusions to act on.

Action: PDI - Plumbing Distributors and Lighting Design LLC are the two to look at first — both are large, active accounts ($156K and $115K current-quarter) whose order volume held up while average unit price fell sharply (−40.9% and −48.5%), suggesting the relationship is healthy but the product mix has tilted toward lower-priced items. The most likely explanations are a shift in what they’re buying or competitive pressure on specific tiers, not a lost account. A focused conversation with each — anchored on which categories drove the mix shift — would confirm whether this is a deliberate buying change or an opening for a competitor.
