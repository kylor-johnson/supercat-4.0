Order Type & Workflow
Confirmed Orders Carry Higher Value

Confirmed orders average $924 versus $595 for Hold-for-Confirmation (HFC) orders — a 1.6x premium. Confirmed orders make up 73% of volume and 81% of eCat sales, so the cleanest path to growth is moving more transactions straight to confirmed rather than holding them.
