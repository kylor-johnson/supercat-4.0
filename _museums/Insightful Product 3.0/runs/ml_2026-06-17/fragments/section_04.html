Order Type & Workflow
Approval-Gated Ordering

Nearly a third of eCat orders (14 of 47, 29.8%) route through a Hold-for-Confirmation approval step before release. These average $2,434 each — close to your $2,882 confirmed-order average — so the approval workflow is governing meaningful, not marginal, volume.
