## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: user_rate_normalization**

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2021. That rate reflects the pricing in place at signing — it hasn't been updated to the current standard. Going forward, every account moves to the same graduated user structure.

Your platform base is also moving from $1,710 to $2,295 — this is the current Commerce Enterprise standard.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Your included user base is expanding from 25 to 40 — absorbing 15 users who no longer generate a charge. The remaining 8 excess users are billed at the graduated rate: 8 users × $25 = $200/month.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,710 | $2,295 (Commerce Enterprise (T3)) |
| Included users | 25 | **40** |
| Additional billable users | 24 users at $20 = **$480/month** | 8 users at graduated rate = **$200/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$2,190** | **$2,495** |

*User billing is based on enabled accounts in your SuperCat environment — the user figures above reflect your current enabled count.*

The platform has grown considerably since 2021 — more surfaces, more capability, the infrastructure behind it. The rate now reflects what the platform is today.

## What You're Getting at $2,495/Month

**Commerce Enterprise (T3):** At this rate, your team keeps everything they're already running — the rep app your field team sells from, your buyer-facing catalog, online ordering for your accounts, order and invoice tracking, and your sales intelligence dashboard. Up to 40 users are included, along with a dedicated CSM, priority support, and CPQ and credit card processing. No separate line items for any of it.

The annual rate increase works out to approximately $0.92 per order.

At this rate, you have access to two releases already in progress:
