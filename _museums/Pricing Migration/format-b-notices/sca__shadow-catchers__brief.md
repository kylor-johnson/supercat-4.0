## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: user_rate_normalization**

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2015. That rate reflects the pricing in place at signing — it hasn't been updated to the current standard. Going forward, every account moves to the same graduated user structure.

Your platform base is also moving from $725 to $749 — this is the current Catalog Essentials standard.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Your included base is also adjusting from the legacy 25 to the current Catalog Essentials standard of 10. With 18 average users, 8 are now above the 10-user included base, billed at the graduated rate: 8 users × $25 = $200/month.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $725 | $749 (Catalog Essentials (T1)) |
| Included users | 25 | **10** |
| Additional billable users | 0 users at $20 = **$0/month** | 8 users at graduated rate = **$200/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$725** | **$949** |

*User billing is based on enabled accounts in your SuperCat environment — the user figures above reflect your current enabled count.*

The platform has grown considerably since 2015 — more surfaces, more capability, the infrastructure behind it. The rate now reflects what the platform is today.
