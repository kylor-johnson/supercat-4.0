## Why the Number Is Changing

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2012. That rate was locked in at signing and hasn't been updated to the current rate card. We're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

Your platform base is also standardizing from $1,515 to the current Commerce Enterprise rate of $2,295 — this reflects the move from legacy module pricing to the current bundled tier structure.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Your included user base is expanding from 25 to 40 — absorbing 15 users who no longer generate a charge. The remaining 17 users above the new included base are billed at the graduated rate: 10 × $25 + 7 × $22 = $250 + $154 = **$404/month**.

Here's how that plays out on your invoice:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,515 | $2,295 (Commerce Enterprise, T3) |

| Additional billable users | 33 users at $20 = **$660/month** | 17 users at graduated rate = **$404/month** |

*User billing is based on enabled accounts in your SuperCat environment — the user figures above reflect your current enabled count.*
