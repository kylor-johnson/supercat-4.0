## Why the Number Is Changing

Along with the rate, your account has carried a legacy 25-user included base since signing — the standard Catalog Essentials tier includes 10 users. Both the per-user rate and the included base are moving to the current standard as part of this refresh.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |
