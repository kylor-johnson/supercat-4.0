**Operator notes (remove before sending):**
- Attach the CEO Letter brief PDF before sending
- "[CEO NAME] will call you by [SPECIFIC DATE]" — this is a hard commitment. Put it on the CEO calendar before the email goes out
- The call date must be specific (e.g., "May 28") — never "in the next few days" or "soon"
- For early adopter accounts (pre-2016 cohort), add one sentence after the short-version paragraph acknowledging tenure: "You've been with us since [YEAR] — that relationship matters and I want you to hear this from me directly."
- For `included_user_reduction` accounts (primary or secondary driver): replace "Everything stays the same except the invoice — your team's access, your catalog, your integrations, and your workflows are all unchanged." with: "Your team's access, your catalog, your integrations, and your workflows are all unchanged — the included user base and the invoice are both changing, and the attached brief explains exactly how."
- Never adjust the effective date in the email without updating the brief to match
- Do not add expansion language or upgrade mentions to this email under any circumstances
- CEO must review and approve this email before it goes out — do not send from CS queue
