---

## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: user_rate_normalization / included_user_reduction**

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2019. That rate reflects the pricing in place at signing — it hasn't been updated to match the current rate card, and we're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

Your platform base is also adjusting — from $725 (the 2019 rate for Catalog Essentials) to $749 (the current Catalog Essentials standard).

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Here's how that plays out for your account:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $725 (Catalog Essentials, 2019 pricing) | $749 (Catalog Essentials, current standard) |
| Included users | 25 | **10** |
| Additional billable users | 0 users at $20 = **$0/month** | 2 users at graduated rate = **$50/month** |
| **Monthly total** | **$725** | **$799** |

Your team averages around 12 users per month. The new included base of 10 means the 2 users above that threshold are billed at $25/user each — $50/month.

## What You're Getting at $799/Month

At this rate, your team keeps everything they're already running — the iPad rep app, admin console, and the buyer-facing product catalog. Up to 10 users are included, with standard support. No separate line items.

## How This Compares

At **$799**, your new rate is at the base rate for accounts at this platform level. The same pricing structure is going to every account we work with — there are no account-specific adjustments in how your number was calculated.
