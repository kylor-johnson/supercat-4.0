## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: at_book_tier_shift**

When Lifestyle Solutions joined in 2023, the Catalog Essentials tier was priced at **$725/month** — the rate in the book at that time. The current standard for Catalog Essentials is **$749/month**. The platform has grown considerably since 2023 — more surfaces, more capability, the infrastructure behind it. The rate now reflects what the platform is today. Your tier is not changing, and neither is what your team has access to.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $725 (Catalog Essentials, 2023 pricing) | $749 (Catalog Essentials, current book rate) |
| Included users | 25 | **10** |
| Additional billable users | 0 users — $0 | 0 users — $0 |
| **Monthly total** | **$725** | **$749** |

The included user count for this tier standardizes to 10 users going forward. Your team averages around 7 users — well inside the new included base, so no additional user charges apply. At your current team size, you have 3 users of room before additional charges apply at $25/user.

## What You're Getting at $749/Month

At this rate, your team keeps the iPad rep app, admin console, and buyer-facing product catalog — the tools your reps use to sell from the field, your buyers use to browse, and your team uses to manage catalog and orders. Up to 10 users are included, with standard support. No separate line items.
