## Why the Number Is Changing

**Driver: platform_discount_correction / user_rate_normalization**

Wildwood/Chelsea House has been on SuperCat since 2011, under a billing structure that reflected a discount from the standard Commerce Enterprise rate and a two-site configuration that distributed costs across your two catalog environments. As part of this refresh, legacy discounts and multi-site billing structures are being retired across all accounts. Every account is moving to the standard Commerce Enterprise rate.

Your additional-user rate has also been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2011 — the pricing in place at signing. We're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh. Alongside that change, the included-user base for Commerce Enterprise expands from 25 to 40 users, absorbing the majority of your team.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Here's how that plays out for your account:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,810 (Commerce Enterprise, discounted) | $2,295 (Commerce Enterprise, current standard) |
| Included users | 25 | **40** |

| **Monthly total** | **$2,310** | **$2,495** |

Your team averages around 48 users per month. The new included base of 40 absorbs the majority of previously billed additional users. The 8 users above the included base are billed at $25/user each = $200/month.
