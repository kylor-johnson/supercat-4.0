## Why the Number Is Changing

Your additional-user rate has been **$20/user** since your account was set up in 2021. That rate was locked in at signing and hasn't been updated to the current rate card. We're standardizing all accounts to the same graduated structure as part of this refresh.

The new user pricing follows a graduated curve applied consistently across all accounts:

| Additional users above included base | Rate |
|---|---|
| 1–10 excess users | $25/user |
| 11–25 excess users | $22/user |
| 26–50 excess users | $20/user |
| 51+ excess users | $18/user |

Your included user base is expanding from 25 to 40 — absorbing 15 users who no longer generate a charge. The remaining 8 users above the new included base are billed at the graduated rate: 8 × $25 = **$200/month**.

Here's how that plays out on your invoice:

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Platform base | $1,710 | $2,295 (Commerce Enterprise, T3) |
| Included users | 25 | **40** |
| Additional billable users | 24 users at $20 = **$480/month** | 8 users at graduated rate = **$200/month** |
