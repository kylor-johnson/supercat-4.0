# Lane 3 — Mid-Market × Lighting

**This lane did not survive contact with the sampling frame. Read §1 before the candidate table.**

---

## 1. The frame problem — two measured findings

**(a) The anchor is not a High Point exhibitor.** Savoy House Lighting does not appear in the HPMKT
exhibitor directory. The alphabetical `S` listing runs *Saatva → Saba Italia → Sabine Maes Art →
Sabrina's Gallery → Sadice → Safavieh → Sagebrook → Sakina → Salted Oak → Sam Moore → SAMS →
Samuel Lawrence → Sanderson → Sandy Luther → SANGIACOMO → Sarah Drake → Sarah Von Dreele → SARIZZA
→ Sarreid → Sauder* with **no Savoy** `[MEASURED: 60 exhibitor names harvested across S pages 1–6,
2026-08-25]`.

So "High Point-native" — which lanes 1 and 2 get by construction from the frame — **cannot be true
for lane 3's anchor.** Mid-market decorative lighting of the Savoy House / Kichler / Craftmade type
largely shows at Dallas Market Center (Lightovation), not High Point. That is an `[INFERRED]`
explanation from the anchor's absence plus the frame's composition; it has not been verified against
the Dallas exhibitor list.

**(b) The HPMKT lighting category is not mid-market lighting.** The Lamp & Lighting frame returned
273 rows / ~109 unique non-customer companies, and what is actually in it is decorative-accessory
houses, artisan lantern makers and importers — not program-driven lighting manufacturers with price
codes and trade+retail+online distribution. Of 42 lighting candidates observed automatically:
**3/42 publish a consumer "where to buy"** (the lane's strongest positive criterion, which ran 9/11
in the roster cohort), **2/42 publish a rep network**, and **4/42 offer a downloadable catalog or
price list** `[MEASURED]`.

**Consequence:** lane 3 cannot be filled to 8–12 credible candidates from an HPMKT-only frame. Four
are listed below. Padding to 12 would mean promoting décor-accessory importers into a lighting lane,
which is the failure mode lane 2's "NOT décor accessories" rule exists to prevent.

## 3. Recommendation

**Do not run lane 3 at High Point as specified.** Three options, in order of preference:

1. **Re-frame lane 3 to a Dallas/Lightovation sampling frame** where the anchor and its peers
   actually exhibit. This keeps the lane definition intact and changes only the frame.
2. **Re-anchor lane 3 to an HPMKT-present mid-market lighting company** — but the frame evidence says
   few exist, so this likely reduces to option 1.
3. **Run lane 3 at High Point anyway with Coast Lamp Mfg as the single genuine test**, and treat the
   thin yield as the measurement. Cheapest, and consistent with "over-selection is fine, record it."

**Not recommended:** padding to 8–12 from the décor-accessory pool. That would make the list look
complete while measuring nothing.

**Every row is NEEDS-FIELD-VERIFICATION on 3.5 (multi-channel evidence)** — none of the four
publishes enough to judge it from a desk, and the criterion was written as manual for that reason.
