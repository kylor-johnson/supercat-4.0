## The Headline

> With MCP/agentic access into each customer's instance, SuperCat can now surface insights customers cannot easily generate themselves—turning raw operational data into interpretation, benchmarks, and recommendations.

---

## What "Insights" Means (vs. Analytics)

| Dimension | Analytics (existing) | Insights Layer (new) |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Who generates** | Customer self-service | SuperCat (proactive) |
| **What it shows** | Raw metrics, dashboards | Interpretation + recommendations |
| **Context** | Single-customer view | Cross-customer benchmarks, peer comparison |
| **Actionability** | "Here's your data" | "Here's what to do and why" |
| **Cadence** | On-demand, self-pull | Delivered (monthly/quarterly QBR) |
| **Effort required** | Customer must analyze | Pre-packaged, ready-to-act |

## Insights Layer: Three Pillars

### 1. Sales Effectiveness Insights

**Audience**: CXOs, VPs of Sales, Sales Directors

**What it surfaces**:
- Revenue performance vs. peer benchmarks (percentile ranking across 114+ peers)
- Channel mix health (iPad vs. eCat Online) with ideal omnichannel pattern diagnosis
- Average Order Value vs. segment median and top 10%
- Customer activation rate vs. benchmark (e.g., 19.6% vs. 9.2% benchmark = 2x)
- **Revenue leakage**: inactive customers × AOV = quantified opportunity (e.g., "$8.2M untapped")
- Seasonal pattern analysis (market peaks, summer slowdowns)
- Self-service rate maturity (industry average 15-25%; best-in-class 40%+)

**Key insight types**:
- "Your AOV of $3,302 is 28% above segment median"
- "9,457 inactive customers = $3.1M opportunity at 10% reactivation"
- "April is your peak; pre-position resources"

---

### 2. Instance Health Insights

**Audience**: IT Admins, Operations Managers, Data Managers

**What it surfaces**:
- Data sync status and freshness (products, customers, inventory, images)
- Import error rate and trend (zero errors = "exceptional performance")
- Data completeness audit (e.g., "29% of products missing images")
- Integration health by data type (daily, real-time, as-needed)
- Admin efficiency signals (manual vs. automated updates)

**Key insight types**:
- "Zero import errors in 30 days—exceptional performance"
- "2,382 products (29%) missing images—affects browsing experience"
- "Price level configuration: 83 active"

---

### 3. Feature Adoption & ROI Insights

**Audience**: Sales Reps, Sales Leaders, Customer Success

**What it surfaces**:
- Feature usage vs. adoption benchmarks (SmartPicks, PDF Catalogs, Scan to Search)
- Underutilization identification with impact quantification
- Training opportunity signals
- Platform mastery scoring

**Key insight types**:
- "SmartPicks usage: 131 views (target: 67% adoption). Reps using SmartPicks see +23% higher AOV."
- "PDF Catalogs: 1,198/year (target: 24/month per rep)"
- "Recommendation: 30-minute training session could unlock $X value"

## Why Customers Can't Do This Themselves

1. **Cross-customer benchmarking**: Requires access to anonymized peer data across 114+ organizations
2. **Pattern recognition at scale**: Seasonal trends, channel mix diagnosis, feature ROI—requires aggregate intelligence
3. **Proactive interpretation**: Translates metrics into "so what" and "now what"
4. **Time-to-insight**: Delivered pre-packaged; no analyst effort required

## Packaging Implications

### Tier 3 (Commerce Enterprise) — Insights Layer Included

Tier 3 customers receive:
- **Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR)** with full insights layer
- **Sales Effectiveness Reports** (monthly)
- **Instance Health Reports** (monthly)
- **Account Health Score** with trend tracking
- **Peer benchmarking** (anonymized segment comparison)
- **Proactive recommendations** with impact quantification

### Tier 1/2 — Insights Layer as Upgrade Path

- Tier 1/2 customers have access to **raw analytics** (Sales Portal, Admin Console dashboards)
- **Insights Layer is a Tier 3 differentiator**: interpretation, benchmarks, recommendations
- This creates a natural upgrade path: "Want to know how you compare to peers? Want proactive recommendations? → Tier 3"
