## Tier-by-Tier Competitive Mapping

### T1: Catalog Essentials — SuperCat range: $695–$795/mo

**What T1 includes**: iPad app, admin console, Online Product Catalog (browse), scanning, lists, library, reports, pricing, inventory, sales data, FTP import, ERP export, standard support, **15 included users, 1 brand**.

| Competitor | Equivalent | Price | Total at 15 users | vs. SuperCat T1 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| **AmpTab Starter** | iOS app + buyers portal + unlimited SKUs + basic reporting | $500/mo | **$500/mo** | SuperCat is **39–59% more** |
| **Pepperi Pro** | Web + mobile apps + barcode + multiple price lists | $500 + $48×15 | **$1,220/mo** | SuperCat is **35–43% less** |
| **RepZio** | iOS app + offline + inventory + reports (no online catalog) | $25×15 | **$375/mo** | SuperCat is **85–112% more** |
| **Candid Pro** | B2B ecommerce only (no iPad app, 1 user) | $179–219/mo | **$219/mo** | Not comparable (narrower) |
| **Onsight Business** | iPad sales app only (horizontal, no online catalog) | $65×15 | **$975/mo** | SuperCat is **19–29% less** |

**Competitive position**: SuperCat T1 sits between AmpTab Starter ($500) and Pepperi Pro ($1,220). The primary competitive conversation is with AmpTab.

**AmpTab gap analysis** ($695–795 vs. $500):
- SuperCat premium: $195–295/mo (39–59%)
- Justification: SuperCat includes Online Product Catalog (browse) in T1; AmpTab Starter's "buyers portal" is basic. SuperCat's vertical specialization in furniture/lighting (CPQ add-on, configurable products) is unmatched. AmpTab's $5,000 setup fee partially offsets the monthly premium over 12 months ($417/mo amortized → effective AmpTab = $917/mo in Y1).
- Risk: AmpTab's unlimited users at $500 is a powerful simplicity message. At 15 users, the gap is modest. At 30+ users (where SuperCat charges for additional users), the gap widens in AmpTab's favor on sticker price — but SuperCat captures expansion revenue.

## Competitive Positioning Analysis

### The "Unlimited Users" Narrative

AmpTab's most potent competitive message is "unlimited users at every tier." This directly attacks SuperCat's per-user model. The response narrative:

1. **"Unlimited users" is priced into the platform fee.** AmpTab Starter at $500 with unlimited users vs. SuperCat T1 at $695–795 with 15 included — AmpTab is effectively charging a higher platform fee that bundles user costs. For accounts with <15 users, SuperCat is the better deal because you're not paying for users you don't need.

2. **SuperCat's model rewards right-sizing.** Customers pay for what they use. A 10-user team shouldn't subsidize a 200-user deployment. The included user base (15/25/50) covers the typical team at each maturity stage.

3. **User charges are the expansion engine.** 23% of SuperCat's MRR ($416K/yr) comes from user expansion. Eliminating this would require raising platform fees by ~30% to maintain revenue — making SuperCat less competitive at entry points.

4. **The real comparison is total cost.** At 50 users, SuperCat T3 ($2,295–2,695) is still cheaper than AmpTab Pro ($3,000). The unlimited-users narrative only matters if the customer is comparing sticker prices, not total cost of ownership.

### Where SuperCat Wins

| Advantage | vs. AmpTab | vs. Pepperi | vs. WizCommerce | vs. RepZio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Vertical depth** (furniture/lighting) | Comparable | SuperCat wins | SuperCat wins | SuperCat wins |
| **CPQ / Configurable products** | AmpTab has it at Advanced | Not mentioned | Not available | Basic only |
| **Order & Invoice Tracking in T2** | AmpTab gates at Pro ($3K) | Available | Unknown | Basic |
| **Price transparency** | Both transparent | Both transparent | Quote-only (SC wins) | Both transparent |
| **Expansion economics** | SC captures user growth | Both capture | Unknown | SC broader platform |
| **Implementation depth** | Comparable | Pepperi more expensive | WizCommerce faster | SC more depth |

### Where SuperCat is Vulnerable

| Vulnerability | Competitor | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Unlimited users** | AmpTab (all tiers) | Total-cost-of-ownership narrative; user charges fund product investment |
| **AI capabilities** | WizCommerce (Kai, AI search, recommendations) | Product roadmap question — outside pricing scope |
| **Implementation speed** | WizCommerce (<30 days) | Emphasize quality and depth for complex data (furniture/lighting) |
| **Network effects** | MarketTime (300K buyers) | Different value prop — SuperCat is platform, not marketplace |
| **Enterprise breadth** | Pepperi (multi-language, DSD, merchandising) | SuperCat targets vertical, not horizontal enterprise |

## Price Range Refinement

Overlaying competitive benchmarks on Exercise 1's revealed WTP ranges:

### T1: Catalog Essentials

| Anchor | Value |
|---|---:|
| Exercise 1 revealed median | $725 |
| Exercise 1 range | $695–$795 |
| AmpTab Starter | $500 |
| AmpTab Starter Y1 effective | $917 |

**Refined range: $695–$795** (unchanged). SuperCat sits in the competitive sweet spot between AmpTab Starter ($500 sticker / $917 Y1 effective) and Pepperi Pro ($1,220). Pricing below $695 would sacrifice revenue without gaining competitive position; pricing above $795 widens the AmpTab sticker gap uncomfortably.

### T2: Commerce Professional

| Anchor | Value |
|---|---:|
| Exercise 1 revealed median | $1,414 |
| Exercise 1 range | $1,195–$1,395 |
| AmpTab Advanced | $1,000 |
| AmpTab Advanced Y1 effective | $1,833 |
| Pepperi Corporate (25 users) | $3,450 |

**Refined range: $1,195–$1,395** (unchanged). At the low end ($1,195), SuperCat is only $195/mo more than AmpTab Advanced — easily justified by the Order & Invoice Tracking capability that AmpTab gates at Pro ($3,000). At the high end ($1,395), SuperCat is still well below the AmpTab Y1 effective ($1,833). The hero tier is competitively positioned for adoption.

Note: AmpTab Advanced includes CPQ. SuperCat T2 + CPQ add-on ($195/mo) = $1,390–$1,590. Against AmpTab Advanced at $1,000, the CPQ-inclusive comparison is $390–590 more — but AmpTab's setup fee ($10K vs. SuperCat's ~$7.5–10K) narrows the gap.

### T3: Commerce Enterprise

| Anchor | Value |
|---|---:|
| Exercise 1 revealed median | $1,670 |
| Exercise 1 range | $1,795–$2,195 |
| AmpTab Pro | $3,000 |
| AmpTab Pro Y1 effective | $5,500 |
| Pepperi Ultimate (50 users) | $6,400+ |

**Refined range: $1,995–$2,495** (tightened and shifted up from $1,795–$2,195). Competitive benchmarking reveals significant headroom above the Exercise 1 range. AmpTab Pro at $3,000 sets a clear ceiling that SuperCat can price comfortably below while capturing more value than the revealed WTP median ($1,670) suggested. Raising the floor from $1,795 to $1,995 and the ceiling from $2,195 to $2,495:

- Creates a wider T2→T3 gap (T3/T2 ratio improves from ~1.3x to ~1.6x at midpoints)
- Remains 17–33% below AmpTab Pro
- Justifies the premium over revealed median with Sales Intelligence, priority support, dedicated CSM, executive business reviews, and 50 included users
- The "more value at comparable cost" narrative still holds — current T3 accounts pay a median of $1,670 in platform-only fees but would gain 8+ new capabilities
