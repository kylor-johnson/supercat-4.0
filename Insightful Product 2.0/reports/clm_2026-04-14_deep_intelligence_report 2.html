What Top Performers Do

Among lighting manufacturers on the same platform bundle, the accounts with the strongest engagement and trajectory profiles share these behavioral patterns:

  • Consistent ordering cadence outside peak events. Top performers maintain eCat ordering activity across 8–10 months of the year rather than concentrating in 1–2 peak windows. This is the single largest behavioral differentiator between top-quartile and below-median engagement accounts.
  • Catalog data updated on a regular automated schedule. Top performers run automated imports for products, inventories, and customers on a consistent weekly or bi-weekly cadence. Crystorama's daily import cadence for core data is already aligned with this pattern — a genuine advantage.
  • Rep teams actively using catalog presentation features — not just order submission. Accounts with strong engagement scores show high utilization of PDF catalog creation, product sharing, and customer favorites — not just submit-order events. Using eCat as a selling and presentation layer, not just an order-capture tool, drives higher engagement scores.
  • Portal activity and eCat orders are coordinated. Top performers with both portal traffic and eCat ordering show higher conversion from browse events to rep follow-up orders. This is the gap Crystorama has the infrastructure to close — portal traffic is healthy; the connection to rep activity is the next step.
What this tells you: Crystorama's platform foundation (adoption, data health, catalog quality) is genuinely top-quartile. The engagement gap is behavioral, not structural — it reflects how consistently the rep team uses the platform across the full selling calendar, not whether the platform is set up correctly. The fix is operational, not technical.
