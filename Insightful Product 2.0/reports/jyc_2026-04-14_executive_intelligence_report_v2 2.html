Executive Summary

Jamie Young is running high eCat volume with strong self-service momentum, but nearly half the buyer base that was active in the trailing 12 months went quiet in Q1 — and 10 top-selling items are currently out of stock with restock dates 4–11 weeks out.

  • eCat Online is a real ordering channel, not just a browse layer. In the trailing 12 months, 3,882 orders placed via eCat Online generated $2.77M in GMV — 46.5% of total eCat order volume. Monthly first-time buyers through self-service consistently outpace rep-acquired buyers by 2–3×, signaling strong end-dealer demand. See Customer & Buyer →
  • 10 historically high-volume SKUs are currently out of stock with replenishment 4–11 weeks away. The Stacked Horn Table Lamp, Perignon Chandelier, Theory Chandelier, and Vapor Pendant lead the list, representing substantial all-time invoiced demand. Dealers are likely to route around to substitutes or competitors in the interim. See Product & Inventory →
  • 3,641 buyers have ordered via eCat at least once but have not placed an order in the last 12 months — a large reactivation pool. Of the 1,988 buyers active in the trailing 12 months, 1,189 did not place an order in the last 3 months. These are not lost accounts; they are warm and reachable. See Customer & Buyer →
