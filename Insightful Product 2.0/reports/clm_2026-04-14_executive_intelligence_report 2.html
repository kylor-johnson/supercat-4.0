Executive Summary

Crystorama's rep team is driving strong order volume with a high-AOV HFC workflow, but 493 previously active accounts have gone quiet via eCat and rep engagement intensity is running below the midpoint of comparable lighting accounts — two gaps with direct revenue implications heading into the second half of the year.

📋
They use customer targeting as an entry point for every session. Top performers on this platform bundle show consistently high customer-targeting event rates — selecting or searching for a specific customer before browsing the catalog, rather than browsing generically. This behavior is associated with higher submission rates and higher AOV per session.
🔍
They use product discovery tools (search, collections, filters) at least 3× per customer engagement. Top accounts have a notably higher ratio of product discovery events to customer selection events — indicating that reps are actively exploring the catalog with the customer in mind, not just navigating to known SKUs. This behavior is the primary driver of the engagement score gap.
📧
They share content externally — PDF catalogs, item info emails, and Smart Stacks. Top performers convert discovery sessions into shareable presentations. Email and PDF sharing activity is 2–3× higher in top-quartile engagement accounts. This behavior turns the platform session into a multi-touch selling sequence rather than a single interaction.
📅
They maintain consistent import cadences across products, inventory, and customer data. Top accounts on this bundle run automated or scheduled imports across all entity types on a regular cadence — typically weekly or more frequent — keeping catalog and customer data current. This consistency is the primary factor behind top-quartile catalog freshness and configuration reliability.
What this tells you: Crystorama is in an exceptional position on catalog quality and platform adoption — genuinely top-quartile. The one clear gap is rep engagement intensity, which runs below the peer midpoint. The behaviors that top performers use to close this gap are specific and teachable: more customer-led discovery, more external content sharing, and a consistent session structure that moves from customer selection to product discovery to presentation. A focused rep training cycle built around these behaviors is the most direct path to improving the engagement score and the order frequency that follows.
