Platform Role
The ratio of configuration events (2,632) to order submissions (419) gives a submit-through rate of about 3.9%. This indicates the platform serves primarily as a selling and presentation layer — reps browse, configure, and present products far more than they submit orders through the app. This is a healthy usage pattern for a catalog-forward team.
What this tells you: Product search, Library, and customer targeting are the most-used features — the core selling workflow is well-adopted. PDF Catalog creation (324 events) and Library email sharing (283 events) confirm the presentation layer is actively used. Features like My List and Collections see light usage and could benefit from targeted training if collaboration workflows are a priority.
