Channel Shift
eCat Online's share of total orders has grown substantially — in April 2025, online accounted for just 17% of orders (8 of 46). By Q1 2026, the split is nearly even (50/50 in January and March). While iPad still dominates GMV (76% of total) due to its higher AOV, order count is converging.
What this tells you: The self-service channel is maturing — buyer adoption is real and accelerating. However, iPad orders contribute 3.2x more GMV per order. The opportunity is to grow eCat Online volume without cannibalizing rep-driven AOV — ideally by routing complex, high-value orders through reps while letting routine reorders flow through self-service.

Hold For Confirmation (HFC) orders average $8,933 — more than 3x the confirmed order AOV. These 19 orders represent just 3.6% of volume but carry an outsized 9.7% of total GMV. Quote orders follow a similar pattern at $5,421 AOV. Both workflows indicate complex, high-consideration purchases.
What this tells you: The overwhelming majority of orders (87%) flow through the standard confirmed workflow. The quote and HFC workflows handle higher-value, more complex transactions — supporting these workflows with streamlined approval processes could accelerate high-AOV order conversion.
