Ordering Extends Beyond Platform Logins

While 30 users have actively engaged the platform, 199 unique users placed orders across all channels in the trailing 90 days. This gap indicates that a significant share of order volume flows through online ordering or other channels that don’t require a direct platform login — reinforcing the importance of keeping portal-facing data (categories, pricing, inventory) current for both rep and buyer audiences.

What this means: The platform’s most-used features are discovery-oriented — product search (41,796) and customer search (10,957) dominate all-time activity. Order submission (2,989) and email product info (3,241) confirm the team uses the platform to transact and communicate, not just browse. The under-adopted tier — Configured Items (831), PDF Catalogs (202), Customer Favorites (85), and SmartPicks (30) — represents available capability that could deepen each selling session without requiring new features. With 30 of 36 users still active all-time, the user base is engaged; the opportunity is to increase feature depth per user rather than expand the user pool.
