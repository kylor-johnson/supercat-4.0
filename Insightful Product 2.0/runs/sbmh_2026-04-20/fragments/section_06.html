What this means: The portal relies almost entirely on referral links (61%) and direct visits (38%) over the trailing 7 months. This means the portal is effectively invisible to buyers who are not already linked from an existing source or typing the URL directly. Investing in basic search-engine optimization or shareable catalog links could open a discovery channel that currently contributes almost nothing to portal traffic.
