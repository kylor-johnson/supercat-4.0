Of 14,476 total account records, 2,860 have placed at least one eCat order. In the trailing 12 months, 560 of those accounts remained active—a 19.6% retention rate. Meanwhile, 2,300 previously activated accounts have lapsed, and 11,616 accounts (80.2% of your full account base) have never placed an eCat order.

Low Retention Among Activated Accounts

Of the 2,860 accounts that have ever ordered through eCat, only 560 (19.6%) placed an order in the trailing 12 months. The remaining 2,300 lapsed accounts represent a significant re-engagement opportunity—these buyers already know the platform but have stopped using it.

What this tells you: The primary challenge is twofold: re-activating the 2,300 accounts that have used eCat before but gone dormant, and beginning to penetrate the 11,616 accounts that have never ordered through the platform. Even modest re-engagement—converting 10% of lapsed accounts—could potentially add 230 active buyers back to the network.
