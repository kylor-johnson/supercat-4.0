Enablement Opportunity

eCat captures 9.8% of all-channel GMV but only 3.5% of order count (trailing 12 months). This gap means eCat orders are substantially larger on average than orders placed through other channels — reps are using eCat for high-value transactions. Expanding eCat adoption for routine, mid-value orders could significantly increase both capture rate and digital ordering visibility.

What this tells you: eCat is currently an enablement tool used selectively for larger transactions. The 2.8× AOV premium over your all-channel average (trailing 12 months) is a strong signal that reps see value in eCat for significant orders. The path to a higher capture rate likely runs through expanding eCat usage into mid-tier and routine reorders — the volume segment where most of your 60,000+ annual orders live.
