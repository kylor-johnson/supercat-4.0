Higher-value orders flow through eCat

eCat captures 9.8% of all-channel GMV but only 3.5% of order count over the trailing 12 months, meaning eCat-submitted orders average roughly 2.8× the value of an all-channel order ($6,311 vs. approximately $2,262). Reps appear to favor the platform for larger transactions.

What this tells you: eCat is an established ordering channel but currently handles a modest share of total volume. The gap between GMV capture (9.8%) and order-count capture (3.5%) confirms reps use eCat preferentially for higher-value orders. Expanding adoption across routine and mid-tier orders could potentially increase the platform's share of your all-channel business.
