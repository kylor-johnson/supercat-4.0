Archetypes reflect selling style, not rep quality. A Precision Closer like Jimmilea King ($4,794 AOV) needs frequency and customer expansion support, not configuration coaching. Tailor enablement to each style rather than applying a single training playbook across the team.

What this tells you: Your team skews heavily toward Volume Relationship Sellers (25 of 55 classified reps), reflecting a culture built on repeat-buyer relationships and high order frequency. Your 5 Precision Closers carry the highest per-order values ($1,515–$4,794 AOV) through deliberate use of configuration and bundling tools — a workflow that most Volume Relationship Sellers have not adopted. Coaching more volume-oriented reps toward configuration could lift AOV across the team without requiring more customers or more orders.
