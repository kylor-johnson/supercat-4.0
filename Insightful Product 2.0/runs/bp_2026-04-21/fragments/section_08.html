Search-driven workflow with strong library engagement

Product Search (2,244) and Customer Search (1,586) together account for 60% of all feature events. Library Viewing ranks third at 891 events, indicating the team actively uses shared resources as a selling and follow-up tool—not just the product catalog.

The selling workflow shows clear stages: 2,476 customer-targeting events (search + selection) feed into 2,585 product-discovery events (search + filtering + sort), supported by 891 library views and 66 order submissions all-time. Of 81 active user seats, 48 have logged in within the last 90 days (59%), but only 3 users (3.7%) have submitted orders in that window.

What this means: The team actively uses the platform for product discovery and customer lookup—the core browsing loop is healthy. The drop-off between browsing (thousands of events) and transacting (66 orders) suggests the platform is primarily used as a reference tool. Presentation features (29 PDF catalogs, 28 email sends) and export capabilities (CSV, Excel) are underutilized and represent adoption opportunities. Camera scanning and flipbook viewing have not been used.

Recurring import issues detected

Inventory imports produce recurring warnings: “product not found” for items present in the inventory file but absent from the product catalog. Customer imports produce recurring validation errors: missing shipping address fields cause individual records to fail. Neither issue blocks the overall import, but affected records are skipped silently.

What this means: The daily automated pipeline is the reason customers and inventory remain Fresh at 0 days. The recurring warnings and errors are low-severity but persistent—resolving the product-not-found mismatches would ensure all inventory rows load cleanly, and fixing the missing shipping address fields would prevent customer records from being silently dropped during import.
