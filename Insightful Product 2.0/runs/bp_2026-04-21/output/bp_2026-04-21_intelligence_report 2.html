Minimal Penetration Into a Large Account Base

Only 16 of 7,084 total account records have ever placed an eCat order — an activation rate of just 0.2%. Of those 16, 7 ordered in the trailing 12 months and only 2 remain active in the last 90 days. The platform has barely scratched the surface of Buster & Punch’s dealer network. What this means: eCat adoption is at a pre-scale stage for Buster & Punch. The 7,068 accounts that have never placed an eCat order represent an enormous untapped opportunity, but the current pace of activation is negligible. The upside is that the small cohort of eCat adopters shows healthy retention (43.8%), meaning the platform works for those who use it. The challenge is entirely an onboarding and awareness problem, not a product-satisfaction problem. Scaling from 16 to even 50 eCat buyers would represent a 3× increase while still reaching less than 1% of the account base.
