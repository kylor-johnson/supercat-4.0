January 2026 Spike

January 2026 accounted for 65 orders and $319K in GMV — 39% of all trailing-12-month eCat orders and 57% of eCat GMV. This likely reflects a market event such as a buying show, demonstrating strong rep engagement when the platform aligns with concentrated selling opportunities. During that month, eCat briefly captured 10.2% of all-channel GMV.

What this tells you: eCat ordering spikes sharply around market events but remains modest in baseline months — averaging roughly 9 orders and $20K in GMV outside of January. Building habitual between-show ordering habits among reps would significantly lift sustained volume.
