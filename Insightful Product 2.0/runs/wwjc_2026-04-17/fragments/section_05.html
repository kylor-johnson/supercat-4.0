The most recent full quarter (January–March 2026) delivered 1,725 orders and $2.5M in GMV.

What this tells you: eCat ordering activity is stable and well-established, with monthly volumes consistently above 470 orders for full months. The platform now captures over half of all-channel GMV, and the most recent quarter shows that share climbing to 58% — indicating that eCat is becoming the dominant order entry point for your sales team and buyers.
