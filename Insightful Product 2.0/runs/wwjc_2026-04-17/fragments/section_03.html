Of 13,788 total account records, 9,438 have placed at least one eCat order—a 68.5% all-time activation rate, indicating strong initial platform adoption. However, only 2,394 of those activated accounts ordered in the trailing 12 months, yielding a 25.4% retention rate. The remaining 7,044 lapsed accounts represent the largest opportunity in this section: these are buyers who already know the platform but have stopped using it. An additional 4,350 accounts have never placed an eCat order.

Retention Gap

Nearly three-quarters of accounts that have ordered via eCat at some point are no longer active. The 7,044 lapsed accounts dwarf the 4,350 never-activated accounts, suggesting re-engagement of known buyers is a higher-leverage play than net-new activation.

What this tells you: Your initial activation is strong—more than two-thirds of your account base has ordered through eCat at least once. The challenge is keeping them active. With 7,044 lapsed accounts, targeted re-engagement campaigns could yield significant results: converting even 5% of lapsed buyers back to active status would potentially add roughly 350 buyers to the trailing 12-month active count.
