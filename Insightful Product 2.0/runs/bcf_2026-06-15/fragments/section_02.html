Coaching by Archetype

Archetypes are coaching tools, not performance labels. A Volume Relationship Seller benefits from upselling support and bundling training, not broader prospecting. A Precision Closer benefits from faster workflows and current configuration data, not presentation coaching. Match coaching investments to each rep's natural selling style for the highest return.

Four distinct selling archetypes are active on your team. The strongest returns will come from coaching that builds on existing strengths — helping Volume Relationship Sellers increase deal sizes rather than change their approach, and giving Curated Discovery Sellers the latest presentation materials to maintain their consultative advantage.
