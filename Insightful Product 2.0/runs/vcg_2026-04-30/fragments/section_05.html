January 2026 Spike
January 2026 produced 200 orders and $1.99M in GMV — accounting for 26.5% of all trailing-period orders and 55.7% of total GMV. The $9,943 AOV was 2.1x the overall average. This pattern is consistent with a market event driving concentrated high-value ordering. Outside January, monthly volume averaged 46 orders at roughly $2,634 AOV.
What this means: eCat order volume is seasonal and market-event-driven. The January 2026 surge dominates the trailing period — without it, annualized GMV would run closer to $1.58M. June 2025 (90 orders, $427K) and October 2025 (72 orders, $269K) also showed elevated activity, likely tied to market timing. Understanding which events drive these spikes allows for targeted pre-market catalog and inventory preparation.
