Archetypes Are Coaching Tools, Not Performance Labels

These classifications reflect behavioral patterns, not rankings. A Volume Relationship Seller who covers 101 accounts isn't underperforming — they need AOV support, not breadth training. A Curated Discovery Seller with a $48,023 AOV doesn't need configuration coaching — they need frequency incentives. Coaching should be adapted to each archetype's natural selling style, not applied generically across the team. Note: your product catalog does not currently use configurable product features, so the Precision Closer archetype (which requires configuration activity) does not apply to this team.

Your team splits roughly evenly between volume-based sellers (9 reps averaging $4,264 AOV across broad customer bases) and value-based sellers (9 reps averaging $13,940 AOV with more focused relationships), plus one deep-account specialist. This split reveals a clear two-track coaching strategy: raise per-order value for your breadth sellers, and expand customer reach for your premium sellers.
