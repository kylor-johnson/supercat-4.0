Coaching Should Match the Archetype
Archetypes are coaching tools, not performance labels. A Volume Coverage Seller like Neil Phillips needs AOV support, not breadth training — he already reaches 101 customers. A Precision Closer like Brad Bowman needs frequency incentives and prospecting support, not configuration coaching. Coaching that matches the selling motion accelerates what's already working.
What this tells you: Your team doesn't sell one way. Volume Coverage Sellers generate $4.9M through breadth; Precision Closers generate $4.6M through depth. Neither approach is wrong — but coaching strategies must adapt. The biggest missed opportunity is when a Precision Closer gets breadth training, or a Volume Seller gets told to increase deal size without tools to do it.
