New Buyer Retention Signal

Among 303 new buyers acquired in the trailing 12 months, 44.2% returned to place a repeat order within 90 days. This early-stage retention rate indicates strong engagement once accounts are onboarded to the platform.

What this tells you: Roughly a third of activated accounts have lapsed—203 accounts that previously ordered through eCat have gone quiet. The 44.2% 90-day repeat rate among new buyers confirms that once accounts are onboarded, nearly half become repeat customers quickly.
