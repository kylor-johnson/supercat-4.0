What this tells you: Revenue is heavily concentrated in the Southeast, with North Carolina and Florida providing the broadest customer bases and Virginia delivering the deepest per-account engagement. Markets like Pennsylvania, Illinois, and Ohio show moderate GMV from smaller customer pools, suggesting room to expand account coverage in the mid-Atlantic and Midwest.
