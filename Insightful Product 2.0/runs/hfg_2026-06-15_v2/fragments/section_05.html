eCat GMV share fluctuates significantly month-to-month

Monthly eCat GMV share of all-channel business ranged from 12% to 91% over the trailing 12 months. This volatility reflects the project-based nature of eCat orders — a few large specification orders in a given month can dramatically shift the ratio.

What this tells you: eCat is a high-value ordering channel with meaningful month-to-month variability driven by project timing. The platform consistently generates outsized GMV relative to order count, indicating reps use it for their largest specification orders rather than routine reorders.

Top-5 concentration: 39% of eCat GMV

The five largest buyers account for 39% of total eCat GMV (trailing 12 months). A single account — The Parker Company — represents 22% alone, with just 28 orders generating $3.4M. This level of concentration creates revenue risk if that relationship shifts.

Top eCat buyers are distinct from largest all-channel accounts

None of the top 10 eCat buyers appear among the highest-volume all-channel accounts. The largest all-channel customers (e-commerce and national distributors) show 0% eCat adoption, while eCat is used primarily by project-focused and specialty accounts. This represents both a segmentation pattern and a growth opportunity.

What this tells you: eCat revenue is concentrated in a small number of project-driven accounts operating independently from your highest-volume distribution channels. Diversifying eCat adoption into mid-tier all-channel accounts could reduce concentration risk while capturing incremental platform value.

High-value channel with headroom for volume growth

eCat captures nearly 40% of total business value with fewer than 1 in 10 orders. The 4.7× AOV premium indicates reps reserve the platform for their largest project specifications. Expanding eCat usage to mid-value transactions could potentially increase order capture while maintaining strong AOV — even a shift to 15% order capture at current AOV levels could represent roughly $25M in annual platform GMV.

What this tells you: eCat is firmly established as the channel for high-value project orders. The gap between 8.5% order capture and 39.9% GMV capture reveals an opportunity — reps clearly trust the platform for large transactions but aren't yet using it for their full book of business. Growing order capture toward the GMV capture rate would deepen platform adoption without requiring new customer acquisition.
