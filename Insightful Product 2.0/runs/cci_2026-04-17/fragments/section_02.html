Archetypes Are Coaching Tools, Not Performance Labels

A Volume Relationship Seller needs AOV support and deeper product presentation training — not more customer breadth. A Curated Discovery Seller benefits from maintaining their presentation-first approach while expanding customer reach. Coaching should be adapted to selling style rather than applied uniformly. Note: Deep-Account Specialist and Precision Closer archetypes were evaluated but no reps matched their criteria — no reps have 3 or fewer unique customers, and configurable item features are not active for this organization.

What this tells you: The two dominant selling styles at Currey are Curated Discovery Sellers (higher-value, presentation-driven) and Volume Relationship Sellers (high-frequency, broad customer base, lower AOV). This split suggests differentiated coaching: Volume Relationship Sellers benefit most from AOV-focused training and presentation tool adoption, while Curated Discovery Sellers should be encouraged to broaden their customer reach while maintaining their presentation-first approach.
