Strong Reach, Weak Retention

Nearly one in three accounts (31.6%) has placed at least one eCat order — a broader reach than most comparable programs. However, only 23.7% of those buyers (397 of 1,674) returned in the trailing 12 months, and the 90-day active count drops sharply to 95. With 4,902 accounts currently dormant — 92.5% of your full account base — retention, not reach, is the binding constraint on growth.

Mid-Term Activity Signal

308 accounts were active within the past 6 months, meaning 77.6% of the trailing-12-month cohort placed an order in the more recent half of the period. The drop to 95 in the last 90 days suggests a seasonal trough or gap between market events rather than wholesale disengagement — but the trend warrants monitoring heading into the next quarter.

What this means: The eCat platform has reached nearly a third of your account base — a strong adoption footprint — but three out of four accounts that ever ordered have since lapsed. The 3,625 never-activated accounts represent the largest addressable pool, but the 1,277 lapsed buyers who already know the platform are the higher-probability re-engagement target. The 90-day active count of 95 (down from 308 at the 6-month mark) signals that ordering activity clusters around market events and seasonal peaks rather than steady-state engagement.
