Coaching Opportunities

Reps with the most actionable funnel gaps from the trailing 12 months. Impact projections are potential estimates based on current performance patterns.

Charity Queen
Presentation → Order Conversion
Charity Queen leads the team in presentation activity (432 events) but converts at just 6% — the lowest rate among assessed reps. Her $15,338 AOV means each additional closed order carries outsized impact. She already excels at discovery and curation; the gap is in the final step.
Introduce structured close techniques paired with presentation sessions — commitment checkpoints, follow-up cadences, and client-readiness scoring during presentations.
+$77K potential annual GMV if 5 additional presentations convert at her current AOV
Neil Graves
Customer Targeting → Product Discovery
Neil has the second-highest customer targeting effort on the team (3,776 events) but an exploration ratio of just 0.7:1 against a 3:1 benchmark. He’s investing heavily in finding customers but not translating that into proportional product exploration. His $4,366 AOV sits near the org median, suggesting room to push higher with deeper discovery.
Pre-load curated product selections by customer segment to reduce targeting-to-discovery friction. Pair with showroom walk-throughs focused on customer-specific product matches.
+$44K potential annual GMV with 10 additional orders at his current AOV
Kelly Dougher
Presentation → Order Conversion
Kelly has the strongest exploration ratio on the team (5.9:1) and substantial presentation activity (71 events), but only 14 orders submitted — a 0.2 close rate. She does excellent top-of-funnel work that doesn’t translate to proportional order submissions.
Implement a presentation follow-up workflow: schedule post-presentation check-ins within 48 hours, with order commitment as the explicit goal of each follow-up.
+$26K potential annual GMV with 5 additional orders at her $5,139 AOV
Rodney Jennings
Customer Targeting → Product Discovery + AOV Lift
Rodney’s 1,650 customer targeting events produce only 1,015 product discovery events (0.6:1 ratio). His $1,791 AOV is less than half the org median of $4,089. With 27 orders across 12 customers, he has the relationship breadth — deeper product exploration per customer visit could meaningfully lift order values.
Introduce premium product discovery paths during customer visits. Pair with a higher-AOV rep for joint selling sessions to model upselling techniques.
+$62K potential annual GMV if AOV increases toward the org median across his current order volume
John Anhut
Product Discovery Depth + AOV Lift
John is the #6 rep by GMV ($159K) with 115 orders and the broadest customer base on the team at 46 accounts. But his $1,383 AOV is the lowest among the top 6 performers, and his exploration ratio of 1.1:1 is well below the 3:1 benchmark. More thorough product exploration per customer visit could lift order values without changing his high-activity approach.
Set AOV targets by customer segment and train on upselling and premium product positioning. Focus on depth per visit rather than visit frequency.
+$58K potential annual GMV if AOV increases by $500 per order across current volume
