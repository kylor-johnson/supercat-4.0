Activation Funnel
Of 1,935 total accounts, 1,137 (59%) have ever placed an eCat order. But only 419 (22%) ordered in the trailing 12 months, and 194 (10%) ordered in the last 90 days. The gap between 1,137 ever-ordered and 419 LTM-active represents 718 lapsed eCat buyers — accounts that once used the platform but have since gone dormant.
What this tells you: More than half your account base has ordered via eCat at some point, but retention is the challenge — 63% of eCat buyers have gone dormant. Re-engaging even a fraction of those 718 lapsed accounts would materially lift eCat penetration without requiring new account acquisition.
