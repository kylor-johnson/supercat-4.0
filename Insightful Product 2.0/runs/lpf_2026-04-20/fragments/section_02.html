These classifications reflect behavioral patterns, not performance judgments. A Volume Relationship Seller needs AOV support, not breadth training. A Precision Closer needs frequency incentives, not configuration coaching. Tailor enablement to the selling style.
What this tells you: Four distinct behavioral clusters exist within the sales team. Coaching aligned to archetype — rather than a one-size-fits-all approach — will produce measurably faster improvement in per-rep economics.
