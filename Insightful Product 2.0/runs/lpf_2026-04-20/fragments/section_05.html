Capture Rate Divergence
The GMV capture rate (14.4%) is 6× higher than the order capture rate (2.4%), indicating eCat orders are significantly larger on average than orders from other channels. eCat handles a small fraction of total order volume but captures a disproportionate share of revenue — suggesting reps use the platform for their highest-value transactions.
What this tells you: At 14.4% GMV capture, eCat is a meaningful revenue channel, though there is substantial room to grow. Moving from 14% to 20% capture would represent roughly $1.6M in additional eCat-attributed revenue. The high AOV suggests reps are selectively using eCat for their best customers — broadening adoption across the full account base would significantly increase capture.
