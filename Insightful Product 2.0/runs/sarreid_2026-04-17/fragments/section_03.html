Of your 4,683 total account records, 1,831 (39.1%) have placed at least one eCat order in their lifetime. However, only 265 remain active in the trailing 12 months — a 14.5% retention rate among accounts that have ever ordered. Activity narrows further to 151 in the trailing 6 months and 65 in the trailing 3 months. The largest untapped segment is the 2,852 accounts (60.9%) that have never placed an eCat order.

What this tells you: The steep drop from 1,831 lifetime eCat buyers to 265 active in the trailing 12 months signals significant account lapse. Re-engaging even a portion of the 1,566 lapsed accounts — alongside activating accounts from the never-ordered base — represents a substantial growth lever for eCat order volume.
