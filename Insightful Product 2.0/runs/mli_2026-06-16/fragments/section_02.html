How to use archetypes
Archetypes replace one-size-fits-all coaching. A Volume Relationship Seller needs AOV support, not breadth training. A Precision Closer needs frequency incentives, not configuration coaching. Match development to the style.
What this tells you: Your team contains distinct selling styles that respond to different coaching approaches. Volume sellers drive order count but leave value on the table. Deep-account specialists and precision closers demonstrate that fewer, more targeted interactions can produce premium results — the challenge is replicating that behavior more often or across more accounts.
