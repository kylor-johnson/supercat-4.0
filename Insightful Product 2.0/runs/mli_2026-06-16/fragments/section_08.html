Untapped feature: Kit Items
430 kit items have been configured in the system, but reps have generated exactly zero kit-related events (no views, no orders). This is the largest feature utilization gap. Kits can streamline the ordering process for bundled products — reintroducing them through training or in-app promotion could improve order efficiency and AOV.
