Mirrors & Art: Highest Revenue per Item

At $50,726 per item (all-time), Mirrors & Art generates the highest revenue density of any category — 34% more per item than Lifestyle ($44,318) and nearly triple Accessories ($18,099). Each new mirror or art piece added to the catalog carries outsized revenue potential.

What this tells you: Jamie Young Company's revenue is anchored by the Coastal collection, which generates more than double the business of any other style. Table Lamps lead by category volume, but Mirrors & Art punches above its weight on a per-item basis. The long tail of Industrial ($498K) and Rustic ($435K) collections is small but consistent — these niche styles serve specific buyer segments without requiring heavy catalog investment. Any catalog expansion strategy should weigh the high per-item yield of Mirrors & Art against the breadth advantage of Coastal.
