Of 9,352 total account records, 5,640 have placed at least one eCat order—a 60.3% activation rate that reflects strong platform adoption. However, only 1,986 of those activated accounts ordered in the trailing 12 months, yielding a 35.2% retention rate. The 3,654 lapsed accounts represent a significant re-engagement opportunity, as these buyers have already demonstrated willingness to order through the platform.

Healthy Revenue Diversification

Among active buyers in the trailing 12 months, no single account exceeds 2.7% of eCat order value. The top 5 accounts collectively represent 10.7% of total eCat GMV (trailing 12 months)—indicating broad-based demand with no outsized concentration risk.

What this tells you: Your activation rate is strong at 60.3%—well over half your account base has ordered through eCat. The primary challenge is retention: 3,654 previously active accounts have not ordered in the trailing 12 months. Re-engaging even 10% of this lapsed base could add roughly 365 active buyers back to the network. Meanwhile, revenue concentration is healthy—no single account represents more than 3% of eCat order value in the trailing 12 months.
