Investigative Context

Declining unit prices may reflect several dynamics: competitive pricing pressure driving buyers to request discounts, a natural shift toward lower-priced product tiers, changing buyer mix within an account, or seasonal promotional activity. These patterns warrant individual account review — they are signals to investigate, not conclusions to act on.

The pricing shifts across these 20 accounts are most actionable for high-volume relationships like Ferguson Enterprises ($336K in current-quarter business, -9% average price) and AFJ Distribution ($169K, -9%), where the relationships are clearly active and the price movement is material. Accounts with steeper declines but lower volume — like Valley Light Gallery (-38%) or Wolberg Electric (-36%) — may reflect a natural shift in product mix rather than competitive pressure. Individual account-level conversations will distinguish between benign mix changes and patterns that warrant a pricing or competitive response.
