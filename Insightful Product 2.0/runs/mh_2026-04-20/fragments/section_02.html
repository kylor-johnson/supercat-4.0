Org-Wide Configuration Gap

Configuration and bundling tools (kits, configured items) are virtually unused across the entire selling team. Only 70 total configuration events were recorded against 7,772 product discovery events—a 99.1% funnel drop. This may indicate that Magnussen Home’s catalog does not heavily feature configurable products, or that reps have not been trained on these capabilities. Addressing this gap at the team level rather than individual coaching may yield the highest return.

Half of Magnussen Home’s selling reps browse fewer than three products per customer interaction, and nearly the entire team skips configuration tools entirely. The strongest all-around funnel belongs to Greg Tripoli, who is the only rep actively using kit and configuration features alongside consistent product discovery. Heather Donnelly drives the most revenue but operates with a narrower browsing pattern than her volume would suggest—broadening her discovery workflow could further lift her already-strong output.

Archetypes Are Coaching Tools

These classifications reflect natural selling patterns, not performance grades. A Volume Relationship Seller like Gord Bennett benefits from AOV coaching (product bundling, presentation skills), not broader territory coverage—he already reaches 8 accounts. A Curated Discovery Seller like Heather Donnelly needs customer acquisition support, not product knowledge training—she already leads the team in product exploration. Align development plans to each rep’s natural selling style for the highest-leverage coaching impact.

Magnussen Home’s selling team splits into two primary modes: discovery-and-presentation sellers who leverage the iPad’s catalog capabilities for larger deals, and high-frequency relationship sellers who maintain broad customer coverage with smaller orders. Three additional team members show heavy platform usage without meaningful order volume—they may serve support or administrative functions where direct order capture is not their primary responsibility. Notably absent are Precision Closers (high AOV through complex configuration), consistent with the near-zero configuration tool usage observed across the team.

Coaching Opportunities

Based on platform engagement patterns from the top selling reps, these four opportunities offer the most actionable path to improved iPad GMV. Each card identifies a specific funnel gap, a recommended action, and potential upside.

Gord Bennett
AOV & Presentation Gap

Bennett placed 31 iPad orders trailing 12 months at $1,069 avg order value—the lowest among high-volume sellers. His 322 product discovery events show active browsing, but only 3 presentation events indicate he almost never shares product information with buyers before ordering.

Recommended action: Introduce PDF catalog creation and email sharing workflow before customer meetings. Bennett already discovers broadly (5.8:1 ratio); converting even a fraction of that browsing to customer-facing presentations could lift deal complexity and value.
Potential impact: If presentation-supported selling lifted avg order value from $1,069 to $1,800, potential +$23K annual iPad GMV at current order volume.
Blake Davis
Accelerating — Feature Adoption

Davis is the fastest-accelerating rep on the team: 10 iPad orders in the last 90 days versus 1 in the prior 90-day window, with $23,318 in current-quarter GMV. His 710 cumulative product discovery events show strong browsing habits, but only 4 configuration events and 33 presentation events suggest room to deepen his workflow.

Recommended action: Introduce kit ordering and product configuration tools while momentum is building. Davis’s browsing-to-ordering pattern is strong; layering in configuration and presentation capabilities could compound his rapid growth.
Potential impact: If configuration tools added 15% to avg order value at current growth trajectory, potential +$14K annual iPad GMV.
Javier Nudler
Discovery Bottleneck

Nudler placed 32 iPad orders at $3,176 avg order value trailing 12 months—above-average deal quality. However, his product discovery-to-customer targeting ratio of 2.4:1 falls below the 3:1 benchmark, suggesting he navigates to familiar products rather than browsing broadly after selecting a customer.

Recommended action: Expand product search and filtering habits after customer selection. Encourage collection browsing and cross-category exploration to surface products Nudler may not routinely show to buyers.
Potential impact: If broader discovery generated 6 incremental orders per year at current avg order value, potential +$19K annual iPad GMV.
Michel Champoux
Declining Trajectory

Champoux is the #2 iPad GMV contributor trailing 12 months ($108,152) but his order activity dropped 62.5% quarter-over-quarter (8 orders to 3 in the most recent 90-day window). With 446 days active and $4,506 avg order value, he represents significant revenue at risk.

Recommended action: Proactive re-engagement and workflow review. Champoux’s deep platform familiarity (highest days active on the team) and above-median deal quality make him a high-return coaching target. Explore whether workflow friction, territory changes, or seasonal patterns explain the decline.
Potential impact: Reversing the current decline could recover an estimated $54K in annual iPad GMV run rate.
