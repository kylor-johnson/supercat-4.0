Event-Driven Spikes
Two months — January 2026 (389 orders, $2.3M) and June 2025 (156 orders, $699K) — account for 43% of total LTM orders and 80% of LTM GMV. These spikes carry elevated AOVs ($5,853 and $4,484 vs. $700–$1,500 baseline), suggesting market events or trade shows drive large project orders. The baseline cadence of 40–80 orders/month at $700–$1,500 AOV represents the steady-state business.
What this tells you: Your eCat ordering pattern is heavily event-driven. Excluding the two peak months, the team averages 67 orders/month at approximately $800 AOV. Understanding what drives the January and June spikes — and replicating those conditions more frequently — could meaningfully increase annual GMV. The post-January decline (Feb: 80, Mar: 40, Apr: 41) warrants attention.
