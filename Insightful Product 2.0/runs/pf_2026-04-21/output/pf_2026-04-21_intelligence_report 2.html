A note on archetypes

These classifications are coaching tools, not performance labels — no archetype is inherently better. Coaching should be adapted to each selling style: a Volume Relationship Seller benefits most from AOV support (bundling, upsell prompts), not breadth training they already excel at. A Precision Closer benefits from frequency incentives, not configuration coaching. Matching the coaching intervention to the archetype is what drives results.

What this tells you: Your top 20 reps operate with distinctly different selling motions. The three archetypes suggest that a one-size-fits-all training approach would miss the mark — instead, coaching sessions can be structured around each rep's natural selling pattern to target their specific growth lever.
