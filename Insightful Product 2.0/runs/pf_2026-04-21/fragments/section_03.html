Activation Funnel

Of the 5,400 accounts that have placed at least one eCat order, only 1,080 remain active in the trailing 12 months. Activity narrows further in recent windows: 588 were active in the last 6 months and 396 in the last 3 months. Meanwhile, 11,886 accounts in your system — nearly 69% of your full account base — have never placed a single eCat order.

What this tells you: With 80% of eCat-activated accounts now lapsed and almost 69% of total accounts never having ordered through eCat, the two largest growth levers are re-engaging dormant buyers and converting unactivated accounts. Even a modest improvement in retention — moving from 20% to 25% — would add roughly 270 active accounts to the trailing-12-month base.
