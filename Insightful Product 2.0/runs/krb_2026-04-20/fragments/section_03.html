Near-Zero eCat Adoption

Of the 2,208 accounts in your full account base, only 1 has ever placed an order through eCat — and that single order was placed in August 2025. No accounts have ordered via eCat in the trailing 12 months. The platform has not yet been adopted as an ordering channel by the customer base.

What this means: The eCat platform has a 2,208-account customer base loaded and ready to sell to, but ordering adoption has not taken hold. With only one eCat order ever placed, the opportunity is foundational: getting even a small percentage of the existing account base to place their first eCat order would represent a significant step forward. The infrastructure — customer records, product catalog, pricing — is in place; the gap is in driving reps and buyers to transact through the platform.

The single eCat order demonstrates that the platform workflow functions end-to-end — a rep was able to select a customer, build an order, and submit it via iPad. This confirms the technical path works. The challenge is replicating this across the broader team and customer base.

What this means: New buyer acquisition through eCat has effectively stalled after one successful transaction. With 2,207 accounts that have never ordered via the platform, even modest activation — converting 1–2% of the base — would add 22–44 new eCat buyers. The single successful order by Tricia User in February 2025 provides a template for what works; identifying what drove that transaction and replicating it across the team is the most direct path to jumpstarting adoption.
