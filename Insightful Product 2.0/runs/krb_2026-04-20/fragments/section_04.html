Complete Image Gap

Every one of the 1,493 visible products is missing imagery. In a visual product category like home décor and rugs, images are the primary driver of buyer engagement and product discovery. Reps browsing the catalog on iPad and buyers viewing products see pricing but no visual representation of the products they are considering.

Pricing Is Fully Populated

While images are absent, every product in the catalog has pricing data. This means products are technically orderable — reps who know the product line can still search, select, and submit orders. The pricing foundation is solid; imagery is the missing layer needed to make the catalog functional as a visual selling tool.

What this means: The catalog is priced and ready to transact, but without a single product image, it cannot function as the visual discovery and selling tool that drives eCat adoption. For a home décor and rug manufacturer, product imagery is not optional — it is the core of the buyer experience. Adding images to even the top-selling or most-viewed product lines would transform the catalog from a price list into a visual selling platform. Until images are loaded, reps must rely on external materials or memory to present products, which likely contributes to the near-zero eCat ordering activity observed in §3.
