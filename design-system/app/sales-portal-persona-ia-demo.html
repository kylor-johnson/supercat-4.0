Problems we fix before Intelligence is trustworthy

Illustrative numbers for the switch — not a live warehouse join. Same ideas are wired into Invoices / Customers / Orders / Reports.

1. Picking a territory doesn’t change what I see

Today
You choose Northeast on Invoices or Dashboard and still see everyone (or the wrong set). People export to Excel.
Fixed
Northeast means Northeast — list rows and every total on that screen match. Empty territory assignment never dumps the whole company.

2. Export CSV doesn’t match the screen

Today
Screen says one sales total; the CSV adds up to something else. Nobody knows which number is real.
Fixed
Same filters, same definition: invoiced net. Export total = what’s on screen.

3. Quotes show up as “sales”

Today
Open quotes get counted as sales, so the total looks bigger than what you actually invoiced.
Fixed
Sales / invoiced = invoiced net only. Open orders stay labeled backlog — not sales.
