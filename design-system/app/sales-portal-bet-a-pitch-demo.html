What’s broken

Teaching surface · Broken vs Fixed

Reps can’t trust the numbers until these three things are true. Try the switch below — Broken is today’s portal behavior; Fixed is what Bet A ships.

Show me: Pick a territory. Broken ignores it. Fixed limits it to that territory.
Territory Where Broken · filter does nothing
Showing 1,417 customers · invoiced $16.02M (whole org — territory ignored)
Illustrative numbers for the switch — not a live warehouse join. Same ideas are wired into Invoices / Customers / Orders.

1. Picking a territory doesn’t change what I see

Today
You choose Northeast on Invoices and still see everyone (or the wrong set). People export to Excel.
Fixed
Northeast means Northeast — list rows and every total on that screen match. Empty territory assignment never dumps the whole company.

2. Export CSV doesn’t match the screen

Today
Screen says one sales total; the CSV adds up to something else. Nobody knows which number is real.
Fixed
Same filters, same definition: invoiced net. Export total = what’s on screen.

3. Quotes show up as “sales”

Today
Open quotes get counted as sales, so the total looks bigger than what you actually invoiced.
Fixed
Sales / invoiced = invoiced net only. Open orders stay labeled backlog — not sales.
