# Cursor Guide: Sales Week-Ahead Analyst

This guide shows you how to use the Sales Week-Ahead Analyst with Cursor AI.

---

## Quick Commands

### Generate Your Weekly Report

Just ask Cursor:

```
Generate my Sales Week-Ahead report
```

Or be more specific:

```
Generate my Sales Week-Ahead report for Kylor Johnson
```

Cursor will:
1. Run the analyst script
2. Pull data from HubSpot and Fathom
3. Generate your tactical weekly report
4. Show you a preview
5. Save it to the reports folder

## Example Workflows

### Monday Morning Routine

**You:** "Generate my Sales Week-Ahead report"

**Cursor:** [Generates report and shows preview]

**You:** "Open the full report and summarize my top 3 priorities"

**Cursor:** [Opens report and highlights key actions]

**You:** "Add calendar blocks for these 3 priorities"

**Cursor:** [Helps you schedule time]

---

### Discovery Call Prep

**You:** "I have a discovery call with Bright Lighting Solutions today. Pull their brief from my week-ahead report."

**Cursor:** [Extracts discovery brief]

**You:** "Based on this brief and recent Fathom calls, what are the top 5 questions I should ask?"

**Cursor:** [Generates tailored questions]

**You:** "Create a one-page call prep sheet for this call"

**Cursor:** [Formats prep sheet]

---

### Unblocking Stalled Deals

**You:** "Show me all stalled deals from my week-ahead report"

**Cursor:** [Lists stalled deals]

**You:** "For the biggest stalled deal, draft a re-engagement email using VoC language from recent calls"

**Cursor:** [Drafts email with relevant pain themes]

**You:** "Add this to my HubSpot tasks"

**Cursor:** [Helps log the task]

---

### VoC Analysis

**You:** "What are the top pain themes from my week-ahead report?"

**Cursor:** [Lists pain themes]

**You:** "Find Fathom calls where prospects mentioned [pain theme] and extract how we handled it"

**Cursor:** [Searches and summarizes]

**You:** "Turn this into a talk track I can use this week"

**Cursor:** [Creates talk track]
