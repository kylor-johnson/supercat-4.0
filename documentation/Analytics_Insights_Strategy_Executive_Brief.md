# SuperCat Analytics & Insights Strategy
## Executive Brief: Turning Customer Data into Expansion Opportunities

**Date:** January 2026  
**Prepared For:** SuperCat Leadership  
**Purpose:** Strategic framework for leveraging MCP/BigQuery data to drive executive engagement, QBRs, and expansion revenue

---

# Executive Summary

## The Opportunity

With MCP/agentic access into each customer's SuperCat instance, we can now surface insights customers cannot easily generate themselves—turning raw operational data into interpretation, benchmarks, and recommendations. This capability unlocks:

- **Deeper executive engagement** through data-driven QBR conversations
- **More effective customer success** with proactive health monitoring
- **Materially easier expansion conversations** by surfacing quantifiable gaps and ROI

## The Approach

We've developed a comprehensive metrics framework that:

1. **Aggregates data** from MCP (customer instance data), BigQuery (HubSpot, QuickBooks, Help Scout, Fathom, etc.), and Admin Console usage reports
2. **Organizes insights** by three key personas: Sales Leaders, Operations/IT Admins, and Sales Reps
3. **Maps metrics to expansion opportunities** with specific product recommendations (eCat Online, Sales Portal, Admin Console enhancements)
4. **Provides benchmarks** by industry segment to contextualize customer performance

## Expected Impact

| Outcome | Mechanism |
|---------|-----------|
| **Increased expansion revenue** | Data-driven identification of product fit gaps |
| **Higher retention** | Proactive health monitoring and intervention |
| **More effective QBRs** | Benchmarked insights customers can't self-generate |
| **Reduced churn risk** | Early warning signals from usage and support data |
| **Shorter sales cycles** | Quantified ROI for expansion conversations |

# Key Findings

## Finding 1: Significant Self-Service & After-Hours Access Gap

Based on Admin Console usage data analysis:

- **98%+ of orders** at most accounts are rep-assisted (eCat iPad)
- **Industry benchmark** for B2B self-service is 15-25%
- **Rep workload** on routine inquiries averages 10-12 hours/week
- **After-hours access gap:** Interior designers and trade buyers often prefer to browse and order outside business hours—currently impossible without eCat Online

**Case Study Proof:** Wildwood reports *"Many interior designers prefer to shop outside of business hours. With eCat Online, they can log in at night, filter products, view related items, and place orders instantly."*

**Implication:** eCat Online addresses two gaps—rep workload deflection AND after-hours buyer access. Every account with low self-service adoption is a qualified expansion target, especially those selling to designers.

---

## Finding 2: Sales Leaders Lack Visibility

Current state analysis reveals:

- No territory-level performance tracking for most accounts
- No customer demand trend visibility
- No rep-to-rep comparison capabilities
- Sales managers "flying blind" on pipeline and performance

**Implication:** eCat Sales Portal addresses a real pain point. Accounts with multiple territories or 5+ reps are prime candidates.

---

## Finding 3: Data Quality Issues Drive Support Tickets

From Help Scout and instance health analysis:

- Import failures create downstream pricing errors
- Manual admin updates average 450/month at typical accounts
- Data sync issues trace to 90+ support tickets monthly
- Stale inventory data causes customer complaints

**Implication:** Admin Console automation and integration upgrades have quantifiable ROI in reduced support burden and admin time.

---

## Finding 4: Platform Usage Directly Correlates to Revenue Performance

Feature usage analysis shows significant adoption gaps, but case studies prove the ROI:

| Feature | Typical Usage | Top Performer Usage | Gap |
|---------|--------------|--------------------:|-----|
| SmartPicks | 12% | 67% | -55% |
| PDF Catalogs | 8/mo | 24/mo | -67% |
| Customer Lists | 3 | 18 | -83% |
| Quick Reorder | 22% | 45% | -51% |

**Case Study Proof:**
- **Wildwood:** *"Every one of Wildwood's top five sales reps is also a top-five user of the eCat iPad app, showing a clear correlation between platform usage and revenue performance."*
- **Wildwood:** *"One rep who had resisted switching from the website to the iPad app wrote his first $20,000+ order in a single meeting after embracing the tool."*
- **Hubbardton Forge:** *"The data shows that the top-performing reps are also the most active SuperCat users, making the connection between enablement and revenue crystal clear."*

**Implication:** Training services have quantifiable, provable ROI. We can show customers: *"Your top performers use the platform 5x more than average. Here's how to close that gap."*

---

## Finding 5: Customer Activation Rates Below Benchmark

Across the customer base:

- Average customer activation rate: **6-8%**
- Industry segment benchmark: **9-12%**
- Gap represents **$1M+ annual revenue opportunity** per mid-size account

**Implication:** eCat Online enables inactive customers to self-serve, directly addressing activation gap.

---

## Finding 6: Omnichannel Strategy Creates Compound Value

The case studies reveal that eCat iPad and eCat Online are **complementary, not competitive**:

| Channel | Best For | Timing |
|---------|----------|--------|
| **eCat iPad** | Consultative selling, showroom, trade shows, field visits | Business hours, in-person |
| **eCat Online** | Self-service browsing, after-hours ordering, project saving, spec research | 24/7, any device |

**Wildwood Example:**
- iPad: *"Reps rely on the eCat iPad app to guide clients through 4,000+ SKUs with high-resolution visuals, instant pricing"*
- Online: *"Interior designers prefer to shop outside of business hours...log in at night, filter products, view related items, and place orders instantly"*

**Implication:** The expansion conversation isn't "replace iPad with Online"—it's "complement your consultative selling with always-on customer access." This removes the perceived threat to reps and positions Online as force multiplication.

- Low feature adoption → Training services

## Appendix F: Composite Health Score Calculation

### Score Components

```
Health Score = 
    (Engagement Score × 0.25) +
    (Sales Performance Score × 0.25) +
    (Data Health Score × 0.20) +
    (Support Health Score × 0.15) +
    (Feature Utilization Score × 0.15)
```

### Component Calculations

**Engagement Score (0-100):**
```
= (Login Frequency / Segment Median) × 50 +
  (Active User Ratio / 100) × 50
Cap at 100
```

**Sales Performance Score (0-100):**
```
= (Orders per User / Segment Median) × 50 +
  (Customer Activation / Segment Median) × 50
Cap at 100
```

**Data Health Score (0-100):**
```
= 100 - (Days Since Last Sync × 5) - (Import Error Rate × 100)
Floor at 0
```

**Support Health Score (0-100):**
```
= 100 - (Tickets per 100 Users × 2) - (Avg Resolution Days × 5)
Floor at 0
```

**Feature Utilization Score (0-100):**
```
= (Features Used / Features Available) × 100
```

### Score Interpretation

| Score Range | Status | Action |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| 80-100 | 🟢 Healthy | Expansion ready |
| 60-79 | 🟡 Stable | Optimization opportunities |
| 40-59 | 🟠 At Risk | Intervention needed |
| 0-39 | 🔴 Critical | Immediate attention |
