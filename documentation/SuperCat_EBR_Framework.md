# SuperCat Executive Business Review (EBR) Framework

**Purpose:** Annual/bi-annual strategic reviews with C-suite to prove ROI, align on goals, and identify expansion opportunities

---

## EBR vs QBR

| **Aspect** | **EBR** | **QBR** |
|------------|---------|---------|
| **Frequency** | Annual/bi-annual | Quarterly |
| **Attendees** | C-suite, VPs, Directors | Managers, team leads |
| **Focus** | Business outcomes, ROI | Usage metrics, tactical progress |
| **Duration** | 60-90 min | 30-45 min |

---

## Core Objectives

1. **Prove Value** → Show ROI to ensure renewal
2. **Discover Goals** → Uncover upsell/cross-sell opportunities
3. **Align Strategy** → Update success plan with new priorities

---

## EBR Structure (60-90 minutes)

### 1. Progress & Context (10 min)
- Action items from last EBR
- Customer's business changes
- Original goals recap

**Example:**
> "Last June you focused on: reducing rep workload, improving territory visibility, activating dormant customers. Here's where we are..."

---

### 2. Delivered Value (20 min)

Show ROI across 4 dimensions:

**Revenue Performance**
- Order value growth, AOV improvements, customer activation

> "Order value increased 18% YoY to $28.4M, AOV up 12% to $4,260—exceeding your $26M target by $2.4M."

**Sales Productivity**
- Time recovered, rep capacity gains, feature impact

> "eCat Online freed up 8.2 hrs/rep/week—equivalent to adding 2.1 FTE without hiring."

**Operational Efficiency**
- Self-service growth, admin work reduction, error elimination

> "Admin Console cut manual updates 82% (450→81/month), saving 15 hrs/month and eliminating 14 pricing tickets."

**Market Penetration**
- Activation rate, TTFV, dormant reactivation

> "Activation improved 6.8%→9.4%, exceeding 9.2% benchmark. 324 new active customers = $1.4M incremental revenue."

**Data Sources:** Sales Effectiveness, Instance Health, Rep Performance reports

---

### 3. Challenges & Solutions (15 min)

Pick 2-3 key challenges. Use this format:
- Current state + impact
- Root cause
- Proposed solution

**Example:**
> "eCat Online enabled but only 8% adoption. Root cause: customers don't know it exists + need setup help. Solution: targeted email campaign + 15-min onboarding calls."

**Common Challenge Types:**
- Adoption barriers (low feature usage, training gaps)
- Data quality (sync failures, accuracy issues)
- Organizational constraints (resource limits, process gaps)
- External factors (market conditions, supply chain)

---

### 4. Future Goals & Roadmap (20 min)

**A. Discover Their Priorities** (pre-work: survey + 2-3 stakeholder interviews)
- Top 3 business priorities for next 12 months
- How their sales org is evolving
- Success metrics for the year

**B. Updated Success Plan**

| Business Outcome | SuperCat Capability | KPI | Target |
|------------------|---------------------|-----|--------|
| Increase sales capacity | eCat Online | % self-service orders | 20% |
| Improve territory mgmt | Sales Portal | Manager usage rate | 80% |
| Reduce admin overhead | Admin Console | Manual updates/month | <50 |
| Faster onboarding | Streamlined setup | TTFV (days) | <14 |

**C. Expansion Opportunities** (present 2-3 recommendations)

| Current State | Opportunity | Business Case | Next Step |
|---------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| iPad only, <5% self-service | **eCat Online** | 8-10 hrs/rep/week freed up | Pilot: 50 customers |
| No territory analytics | **Sales Portal** | Regional visibility | Manager demo |
| High manual updates | **Admin Console** | 80% admin time reduction | Integration assessment |

---

### 5. Lessons Learned (10 min)

**What Worked**
> "Q3 iPad training: 92% attendance, correlated with 15% AOV increase in Q4."

**What Didn't**
> "eCat Online rollout: 8% adoption vs. 25% target. We didn't communicate value to end customers."

**Corrective Action**
> "Created 3-touch email campaign template for future launches. Piloting with Sales Portal."

---

## Preparation Checklist

**3 Weeks Before**
- [ ] Schedule EBR (60-90 min)
- [ ] Send pre-EBR survey to executives
- [ ] Pull data reports (Sales Effectiveness, Instance Health, TTFV)

**2 Weeks Before**
- [ ] Conduct 2-3 stakeholder interviews (15-20 min each)
- [ ] Identify 3-5 key wins with quantified impact
- [ ] Draft 2-3 expansion recommendations

**1 Week Before**
- [ ] Create presentation deck
- [ ] Prepare demo for recommended capabilities
- [ ] Send agenda to attendees

**After EBR**
- [ ] Send meeting notes + action items (within 48 hrs)
- [ ] Update CRM/account plan
- [ ] Schedule expansion follow-ups

## Pre-EBR Survey Template

**Subject:** Preparing for Our [Month] Executive Business Review

Hi [Executive Name],

Looking forward to our EBR on [Date]. Please take 5 minutes to answer:

1. **Business Priorities:** Top 3 priorities for next 6-12 months?
2. **Success Metrics:** What metrics define success for your team this year?
3. **Current Challenges:** Biggest challenges in sales effectiveness, operations, or customer engagement?
4. **Changes Ahead:** Any planned changes to sales process, tech stack, or org structure?
5. **Partnership Feedback:** What's working well? What could we improve?
6. **Topics:** Specific topics you'd like to cover?

Please reply by [Date - 1 week before]. Thanks!

## Presentation Structure

1. **Title & Agenda** - Date, attendees, 5-section overview
2. **Executive Summary** - 3-4 key takeaways, headline metrics
3. **Progress** - Action items, context changes, goals recap
4. **Delivered Value** - 4 slides (Revenue, Productivity, Efficiency, Market Penetration) with visuals
5. **Challenges** - 2-3 challenges with solutions
6. **Future Goals** - Evolving priorities, updated success plan, 2-3 expansion recommendations
7. **Lessons Learned** - What worked/didn't, corrective actions
8. **Next Steps** - Action items with owners/dates

---

## Value Narrative Templates

**Revenue Growth**
> "Since implementing [capability], order value grew [X%] to $[amount]—$[incremental] in additional revenue, exceeding your $[target] goal."

**Sales Productivity**
> "[Capability] freed up [X] hrs/rep/week—equivalent to adding [X] FTE without hiring. At $[cost]/rep, that's $[savings] annual savings."

**Operational Efficiency**
> "[Capability] reduced [activity] by [X%] (from [before] to [after]), saving [X] hrs/month and eliminating [X] [issues]."

**Market Penetration**
> "Activation improved [X%]→[Y%], exceeding [Z%] benchmark. [X] additional active customers = $[amount] incremental revenue."

## Expansion Conversation Framework

1. **Current State** - Describe situation with metrics
2. **Business Impact** - Consequence (time lost, revenue missed, risk)
3. **Solution** - How capability addresses it
4. **Expected Outcome** - Quantified result with timeframe
5. **Proof Point** - Customer example
6. **Next Step** - Low-friction action (pilot, demo, assessment)

**Example:**
> **Current State:** "98% of orders require rep involvement. Reps spend 10 hrs/week on routine inquiries."  
> **Impact:** "Limits sales capacity, prevents focus on high-value activities."  
> **Solution:** "eCat Online enables 24/7 self-service ordering, pricing, order status."  
> **Outcome:** "Expect 15-25% orders to self-service in 90 days, freeing 8-10 hrs/rep/week."  
> **Proof:** "ABC Distribution: 22% self-service in 60 days, expanded to 2 new territories without adding headcount."  
> **Next Step:** "Pilot with 50 active customers for 30 days. Minimal setup, clear adoption data."
