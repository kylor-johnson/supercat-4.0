## 2. Brand-by-Brand Analysis

### A. Gabby (GH) & Summer Classics Wholesale (SCW)
**Status:** Mature, Transactional Powerhouses.
*   **Usage:** Near 100% adoption among users. High correlation between logins and orders.
*   **Volume:** Combined ~34k orders submitted via the platform.
*   **Insight:** These brands are utilizing the platform exactly as intended for field sales efficiency.
*   **Opportunity:** Focus on "Quality of Life" improvements (bulk ordering tools, faster search) to support the high volume.

### B. Summer Classics Contract (SCCON)
**Status:** High Engagement, Non-Transactional.
*   **Observation:** 100% active user adoption and 50k+ searches, but **zero orders submitted**.
*   **Insight:** The team uses the app heavily as a digital catalog and likely for quoting or building "wishlists" (PDF creation), but the final transaction happens elsewhere (likely ERP direct or email).
*   **Strategic Question:** Is this the desired workflow?
    *   *If yes:* Optimize for "Presentation Mode" and PDF generation.
    *   *If no:* Identify blockers to submitting orders (e.g., complex pricing, custom freight, approval workflows).

### C. Summer Classics Retail (SC)
**Status:** High-Frequency "Search Engine".
*   **Usage:** Massive volume—417k product searches (4x other brands).
*   **Workflow:** High login count (56k) suggests frequent, short sessions—likely sales associates looking up stock/specs while on the floor with customers.
*   **Insight:** The app is a critical point-of-sale reference tool.
*   **Opportunity:** Ensure search speed and inventory accuracy are pristine. "Search" is the killer feature here.

## 3. Support & System Health

### Help Scout Insights
*   **Volume:** **554 tickets** in the last 12 months (~1.5 tickets/day).
*   **Distribution:** Vast majority tagged under "Gabriella White".
*   **Assessment:** Volume is moderate-to-high relative to the user base size (~360 users).
*   **Recommendation:** Conduct a "Ticket Theme Analysis" to identify recurring friction points (e.g., login issues, sync errors, or feature confusion). Reducing this by 20% would save significant support hours.

### Sales Intelligence (Fathom)
*   **Status:** **No recent meetings found** in Fathom with "Gabby" or "Summer Classics" in the title/domain for the last year.
*   **Gap:** We lack visibility into recent strategic conversations or "Voice of Customer" from sales calls.
*   **Action:** Re-establish regular check-ins or ensure meeting recording compliance to capture qualitative feedback.

## 4. Strategic Recommendations & Roadmap

### 1. Workflow Optimization (SCCON)
*   **Investigation:** Deep dive into the Contract team's workflow. Why are 0 orders submitted despite 50k searches?
*   **Action:** If they are building quotes, can we integrate a "Submit Quote" feature that pushes to the ERP as a draft order?

### 2. Search Optimization (Retail)
*   **Observation:** With 400k+ searches, even small latency or irrelevance hurts retail productivity.
*   **Action:** Review search logs for "Zero Result" queries or slow queries to optimize the Retail experience.

### 3. Support Efficiency
*   **Goal:** Reduce ticket volume from ~550/year to <400.
*   **Action:** Categorize the top 3 ticket drivers (e.g., Password Reset, Sync Issues) and implement self-service fixes or better documentation.

### 4. Expansion / Data Usage
*   **Mixpanel Trend:** Monthly Active Users (MAU) for Retail (SC) showed a slight dip in Nov/Dec (seasonality?) but is rebounding.
*   **Action:** Monitor adoption post-market (spring/summer) to ensure seasonal reps re-engage.
