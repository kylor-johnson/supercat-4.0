What to build, in order

Trust, then protect what works, then the two things the original brief asked for: eOL targeting by motion, and one iPad-embedded analytics shell.

This does not reopen v4.0. The 109-org roster, the four selling-motion names, and the stamped HTML/CSV in Customer Segmentation/current/ stay the authority for “what game is this client playing.” Personas answer “who is using SuperCat.”
1 · Portal

EBR-91 — export matches the screen. VP-1. Nothing else is worth building on an untrusted number.

2 · eOL + iPad

Inventory snapshot age on every quantity. Buyer price/stock becomes honest. Rep extract gets an age line.

3 · Admin

Catalog completeness list. Cheapest new admin job. Why a volume rep still opens the iPad.

4 · iPad-EC shell

One WebView, one extract, fail-closed, offline. Then spec + trade packs (REP-1). Skip volume L panel.

5 · eOL GTM

Turn Cart on where spec/trade buyers will use it. Do not run a volume eOL campaign.

Client, not sprint

Invoice feed for the 71 unfed roster orgs. Territory files so more than 16% of reps can see a book.
