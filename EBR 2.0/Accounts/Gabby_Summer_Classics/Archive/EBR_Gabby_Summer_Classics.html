Growth Opportunities + Cross-Entity Playbook

Customer Reactivation

Gap
91%+ of customer databases are dormant across GH and SCW. 8,000–11,800 records with single-digit activation rates.
Path
Targeted eCat Online campaigns using the existing self-service channel. Start with lapsed buyers (ordered 6–12 months ago) in high-AOV territories.
Outcome
5% reactivation at current AOVs = 400+ new active customers and incremental revenue at scale.

Camera Scan Expansion

Gap
SC leads with 13,332 scans. GH and SCW show adoption (678 / 447) but SCCON has zero.
Path
Transfer SC's scan-to-order workflow to wholesale reps. Evaluate SCCON for scan-to-quote use case.
Outcome
Faster product lookup for field reps. Reduced manual search time. Potential SCCON workflow unlock.

SmartPicks Activation

Gap
185 total SmartPicks views across all entities — near-zero relative to 200K product searches.
Path
Pilot with SCW ($5,182 AOV). AI recommendations on high-value orders can increase basket size without additional rep effort.
Outcome
Even 2–3% basket size lift on SCW's volume represents meaningful incremental revenue.

Cross-Entity Best-Practice Transfers

→
SC → GH, SCW — Camera Scan workflow. SC's 13,332 scans demonstrate proven adoption. Wholesale reps can use the same pattern for showroom and warehouse scenarios.
→
SCCON → GH, SCW, SC — Customer Product Lists. SCCON leads with 81 views — buyer-specific curation for project-based selling. Transferable to wholesale and retail for curated buyer presentations.
→
GH → SCW, SCCON — Sales Portal adoption. GH logs 5,695 Portal sessions vs. SCCON's 557. Portal value in GH can inform training approach for other entities.

This is a menu, not a prescription. Tell us which items resonate and we'll build the plan together.
