C6 — Discovery

Based on These Comparisons — What Stands Out?

These questions are anchored in the cross-entity benchmarks we just reviewed. The goal is to understand your priorities so recommendations are grounded in what matters to you.

  1. GH leads self-service at 35.8% — is that a deliberate digital strategy, or has it emerged organically from rep and customer behavior?
  2. SCW's 45.6% order growth surge — what's driving it? A seasonal push, new reps, expanded territory? Can that playbook transfer to other divisions?
  3. SC retail has the highest volume but lowest digital adoption — is that by design for the retail store model, or is there interest in enabling B2B Cart for reordering?
  4. Power users average 16–19 feature breadth vs. org average 9.8 — what would it take to close that gap? Is it a training opportunity or a role-based constraint?
Let the client drive this section. These are conversation starters, not an interrogation. Listen for which gaps they care about vs. dismiss — that signals where expansion energy should focus.

C7 — Recommendations

Closing the Gaps

1. Transfer GH's Portal Playbook Across Entities

The Gap
Portal usage — GH 5,695 views vs. SC 577. A 10x difference in analytics visibility.
The Path
Identify what makes Portal valuable to GH users. Replicate that workflow training for SC and SCCON teams.
Outcome
Unified analytics visibility across all 4 divisions. Leadership gets cross-entity performance views.

2. Launch Flipbook Pilot in SCCON

The Gap
Zero Flipbook, Placements, or Commitments usage globally across all entities.
The Path
Flipbook pilot in SCCON (highest self-service orientation at 52.9% quote rate, 202 existing PDFs to replace). Then roll to other entities.
Outcome
Replace static PDF catalogs with interactive digital content. Increase buyer engagement and reduce catalog production overhead.

3. Configure Territories for SC Wholesale

The Gap
SC has 0 configured territories despite 42 territory codes and 15+ physical retail stores generating $11.2M.
The Path
Map existing 42 territory codes to Portal. Quick-win configuration — no data migration required.
Outcome
Unlock per-store analytics for the highest-volume entity. Store managers gain visibility into their own performance.
