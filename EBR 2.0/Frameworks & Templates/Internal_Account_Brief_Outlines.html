Internal Account Brief

Framework & Structural Outlines
v1.0  ·  Feb 26, 2026  ·  SuperCat Solutions

Two document formats for internal account intelligence. Both are promptable via Cursor/MCP against BigQuery (166 tables) and PostgreSQL (108 tables). Neither is scoped to a specific meeting type, role, or engagement. Version A is a subset of Version B — they share the same analytical backbone (the Account Scoring Model) and differ only in depth.

Version A Account Snapshot

Answer "what's going on with this account right now?" in 60 seconds. Designed for daily account management, portfolio triage, and quick context before any interaction. Fully promptable — can be generated for any of the 129 active accounts at any time.

Audience: Anyone — no context assumed Length: One page / one screen Generation: On-demand, single or batch Principle: Density over depth

A3 Top 3 Signals

Auto-surfaced from scoring components. The three most anomalous, actionable, or time-sensitive signals right now. Not a fixed list — the prompt selects whatever matters most.

Selection logic: Any component in top/bottom 10% of portfolio · Any metric with 3+ months directional trend · Any binary flag (payment overdue, data stale, Fathom silence >60d) · Any newly triggered expansion signal

→ "MAU declining 3 consecutive months (72 → 64 → 58) — engagement decay, investigate cause"
→ "3 expansion signals triggered: No SmartPicks, High PDF + No Flipbook, Growing User Count"
→ "Invoice 47 days overdue ($2,340) — financial flag, verify with accounting"
→ "Fathom: competitor mentioned in last 2 calls (NuORDER) — competitive pressure signal"

— Snapshot Design Principles
Everything fits on one screen. If it scrolls, it's too long.
Quadrant placement is the headline. Visual hierarchy starts there.
Signals over metrics. Three interpreted signals beat twenty raw numbers.
Recency over history. Show what's happening now, not six months ago.
Use-case flex is automatic. The prompt detects ordering vs. non-ordering and adjusts A4.
Version B Account Intelligence Brief

Answer "what do I need to know about this account?" in full depth. Designed to give any team member — CS, support, leadership, or someone new to the account — a complete picture of the account's current state, history, and dynamics. Context-neutral by default; an optional final section adapts to the specific engagement when specified.

Audience: Anyone — no meeting type assumed Length: 3–5 pages Generation: On-demand, typically before high-stakes engagement Principle: Depth over density

— Intelligence Brief Design Principles
Context-neutral by default. The body makes no assumptions about why you're reading it. It's a factual intelligence reference.
Scoring is the spine. Quadrant placement and component breakdown frame every other section.
Data-sourced, not opinion-based. Every element traces to a specific data source. Interpretation lives in the scoring model and the optional Context section.
Use-case flex is silent. The prompt detects ordering vs. non-ordering and adjusts B3, B4, and Business Impact automatically.
Insufficient data is surfaced, not hidden. If a data source has no records, the brief says so rather than omitting the section.
The optional Context section is the only subjective layer. Everything above it is factual. Context is where the brief becomes a talk track.
